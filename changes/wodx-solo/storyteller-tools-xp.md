Add experience for the lone player (B4806). The Storyteller tools on the solo panel gain an Add experience button: the player names the points (a whole number from 1 to 100) and a reason, and the Storyteller's seat gives the award through WoDX Creation, so it lands on the sheet's experience list and log like any Storyteller award and can be spent at once. It has the same limits as the other tools: solo mode on, outside the Live world, nobody else playing.
