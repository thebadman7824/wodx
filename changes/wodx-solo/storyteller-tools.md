Storyteller tools for the lone player (B4800 to B4805). A switch under More on the solo panel shows Storyteller options, so the one person playing can check that everything works as meant and give their character items to test with. The player stays a player in Foundry: each tool is an ask the hidden Storyteller's seat answers. The Storyteller's cards shows every card, roll and whisper meant for the Storyteller alone as it arrives, with its buttons pressed by the seat when picked; Give my character an item searches the world's items and every Item compendium and makes the pick on the character's sheet; Check the systems lists the WoDX parts running and their versions, any setting the switch made that has changed since, the approval queue, cards left waiting and errors the seat has seen; What is hidden here lists the hidden tokens on the player's scene. The tools go on only while The Lone Chronicle is on, outside the Live world, for a player, with nobody else logged in, and go off by themselves when another player logs in or the mode is turned off. A Storyteller can take the switch off the panel with the new setting Offer Storyteller tools to the lone player.
