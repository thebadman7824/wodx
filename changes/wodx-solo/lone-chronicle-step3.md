Step 3 of The Lone Chronicle (B5980 to B5985). Ask the oracle: the player writes a yes-or-no question and picks its odds (Almost certain, Likely, Even odds, Unlikely, Hardly); the seat rolls one d10 that both see and answers "Yes, and", "Yes", "Yes, but", "No, but", "No" or "No, and" on a card whispered to the player, and an "and" sends a foreshadowing through the approval queue. Leads: a window of what the character could follow up, from the signs and dreams sent to them, their open oaths, debts, boons and objectives in the Ledger (a consequence stays the Storyteller's) and the oracle's last answers, each with Let it go. Night encounters: as the clock moves at night, each part of the night rolls once (one in three) and a hit puts the people of one of the night's encounters (town or wilds, by the scene) on the character's scene, four to eight squares off and in plain sight; world setting "Night encounters find the lone player" (on), scene flag wodx-solo.encounters (town, wilds or none).
