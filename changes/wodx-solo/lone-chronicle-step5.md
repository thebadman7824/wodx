Lone Chronicle step 5 (B6530 to B6536). The Beast played by the game: when the lone player's character falls into frenzy, the seat makes it a stand-in, so WoDX NPC plays its turns; with no fight on, it goes for an enemy in sight, else the nearest living being, and the fight starts on the map. A Kindred or ghoul player may spend a Willpower point for one turn of control (V20 pdf 307). The frenzy ends when the fight is over; a botched one is left to WoDX Actions. Torpor: Sleep the torpor away runs the clock to the night of rising (Pass the Years when a year or more) and rolls the rising. Sleep through the years on the panel: a Cainite goes into voluntary torpor, no shorter than half the Road's time; anyone else lives the years; the world ages with them, to 2022, or 2099 on the Future Darkness branch. Death and heirs: when the character dies, Carry on offers a childe, a ghoul, an heir from the years, the family, a tie on the sheet or the player's own others, or a new character; the heir's token stands by the body and every owner record of the dead passes to the heir.
