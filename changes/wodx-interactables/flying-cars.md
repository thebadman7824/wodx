Flying cars (B6620-B6627, B6629, review desk 2026-11-16): four new kinds of mount and vehicle from 2023, the aerodyne, the aerocab, the armoured AV and the hover bike. Their figures are Storyteller figures on the Mage 20th chart rows of the vehicles that do their jobs (the News Copter, the Large Helicopter, the Military Utility Helicopter; the ATV and the Light Motorcycle for the hover bike on the ground), a quarter faster and one Maneuver better from 2040. Every flying kind (balloon, aeroplane, helicopter, jet, gryphon and the new four) now has a height on the map: the one at the controls sets their token's elevation and the craft, its tile or token, and everyone aboard follow, held to its ceiling (a hover bike 20 metres) and to Pilot (a hover bike rides on Drive and flies on Pilot); a hover bike with cargo in its hold stays down. Wrecked in the air, or an AV left with nobody flying it, it falls and everyone aboard takes the crash: the fall's dice less its Durability, halved when strapped in (V20 core pdf p. 304, Mage 20th p. 459); others glide, autorotate, drift or fly on with a card. In a chase, a flier low among the rooftops takes the ground's difficulty and cover, +1 flat out (none with an armoured AV's terrain-following radar), and above them has nowhere to hide. api.flight {altOf, groundUnder, onPilotHeight, bringDown}.
