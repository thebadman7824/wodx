what: Mounts, vehicles, chases and flight: aerodynes, armoured AVs and hover bikes on the Future Darkness branch; height for everything that flies
effect: the pilot's token elevation flies the craft and everyone aboard up or down, held to its ceiling and to Pilot; a craft wrecked or left without a pilot in the air falls and everyone aboard takes the crash through soak; someone stepping off in the air falls; flying low and fast over a crowd or streets is harder in a chase, and a quarry high above has no cover
visible: the craft and its riders rise on the map (their elevation), with a crash card, a fall card and a Lift-off or Touch-down word where it leaves or reaches the ground

Flying cars (B6620-B6627): three new kinds of mount and vehicle from 2023, the aerodyne, the armoured AV and the hover bike, with Cyberpunk 2020 figures turned to V20 terms (Durability = SP / 6, Structure = SDP / 10, which puts the AV-6 on Mage 20th's military utility helicopter). Every flying kind (balloon, aeroplane, helicopter, jet, gryphon and the new three) now has a height on the map: the one at the controls sets their token's elevation and the craft, its tile or token, and everyone aboard follow, held to its ceiling (a hover bike 20 metres) and to Pilot (a hover bike rides on Drive and flies on Pilot). Wrecked in the air, or an AV left with nobody flying it, it falls and everyone aboard takes the crash: the fall's dice less its Durability, halved when strapped in (V20 core pdf p. 304, Mage 20th p. 459); others glide, autorotate, drift or fly on with a card. In a chase, a flier low among the rooftops takes the ground's difficulty and cover, +1 flat out (none with an armoured AV's terrain-following radar), and above them has nowhere to hide. api.flight {altOf, groundUnder, onPilotHeight, bringDown}.
