Carrying the fallen and moving large things (B5070-B5086, Ben's Carry Bodies Desk): anyone next to a character who is down carries them (under the VtDA20 lifting chart) or drags them (over it), alone or together; furniture, carts and stores are pushed, pulled or hauled by the same chart, with a roll each turn up to two steps over it and help from others past that. Wheels halve the load. A carried character who wakes can struggle free. A thing left in a doorway blocks it and is good cover. Setting "Carrying the fallen and moving large things"; tile settings Weight, On wheels and Fixed in place.
