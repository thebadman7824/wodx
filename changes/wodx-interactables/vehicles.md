what: Lorries, armoured cars, tanks, balloons, aeroplanes, helicopters and jets as mounts and vehicles on map tiles, each with the book's Durability and Structure by year, and damage kept on the tile
effect: the new kinds can be driven or flown from the tile's Interact menu in their years (aircraft need Pilot, military vehicles Drive 3, a lorry below Drive 3 is a roll), run in chases at their own speeds, and a wrecked vehicle cannot be driven and stays put when its driver moves

Vehicles for a fight (B4790, B4791). New kinds of mount or vehicle on a tile: a lorry or truck (from 1900), an armoured car or carrier (1914), a tank (1916), a balloon or airship (1783), a propeller aeroplane (1909), a jet (1944) and a helicopter (1945). Each runs in a chase with a speed and Maneuver by year (Mage 20th pp. 461-463 for the modern rows, house figures before) and has a body: Durability, Structure, mass, and whether it shelters those inside (Mage 20th pp. 458-463). Aircraft fly over any ground but indoors and take no ground difficulty; tanks go on rough ground at 20 yards a turn, lorries cannot. Pilot is read from the sheet. The book's minimum skills: a lorry needs Drive 3 or the start is a roll; a military vehicle needs Drive 3 and an aircraft Pilot, or there is no option to drive it at all (anyone may ride along). Bring forward turns a tank into an armoured car, a car or a carriage, a jet or helicopter into an aeroplane, an aeroplane into a balloon, a lorry into a cart. Damage is kept on the tile (flags hull: Structure lost); a wrecked vehicle offers only Get out, and its driver who moves climbs out while it stays. The tile config gains "Damage taken" to set or mend it.
