bump: minor
effect: a shot at someone crouched behind a table, a counter, a barricade or a low wall is at +1, +2 or +3 difficulty on WoDX Combat's card, and Interact offers "Tip it over for cover" on a table
visible: a tipped table shows the "Tipped over" marker on the map with a floating word and a crash heard as far as the noise carries; the attack card names the cover
---
Cover on the map (B4671-B4673): a tile can be cover (a table, a counter or a bar, a barricade, a low wall), set in the tile's window or by a made map's file on Adopt. Shots at someone within a square behind it, with the line of the shot crossing it, are at the V20 cover difficulty (light +1, good +2, superior +3; pdf p. 286), and shots from behind it one less. A table is light cover and can be tipped over in a turn, loudly, for good cover, and set on its legs again. The api gives coverOnShot(shooter, target) for WoDX Combat.
