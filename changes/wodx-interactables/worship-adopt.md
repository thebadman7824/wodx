Houses of worship by the faith of the place (B3200-B3201). Adopting a generated town now tags its chapel for the faith the scene keeps (the scene's own, or its region's in the chronicle's year): a mosque in Cairo, a temple in Lhasa or Kyoto, a synagogue for a Jewish quarter, a church at home. The town generator can also set the faith itself with --faith.
