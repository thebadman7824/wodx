bump: minor
effect: a church, mosque, synagogue or temple region on the map is holy ground kept for its faith: a demon starting its turn there takes lethal damage, and WoDX Actions' True Faith and prayer read the ground and its faith
visible: the demon's wounds show on its token and health bar, and the holy ground card names the house and its faith
---
Houses of worship are holy ground: a region an adopted town map tags as a church, mosque, synagogue or temple counts as an altar of rating 1 (or its own), kept for the faith of the place, and an altar tile takes that faith too; holyUnder gives the faith with the ground, and a demon who starts a turn in any of them is seared (B6362).
