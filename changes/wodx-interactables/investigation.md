Investigation (B3364-B3368): the case board gains theories. Anyone writes one in plain words (who, what, where, why) and picks the lines on the board it rests on; a character tests it with Perception or Intelligence + Investigation at 7 (V20 pdf 272), and the board says how much holds up, never which part: one success says whether any of it does, two roughly how much, three the number of lines, five also how many true links it leaves out. The Storyteller marks which lines are true in a window of their own (What is true); players' boards never show it. Deeds leave evidence at the scene, whoever does them: feeding leaves blood and a witness, a lethal or worse wound blood and traces, a rival's probe of a haven tracks, a witness or ash, harm done on a grudge blood and a witness. Evidence fades at its own pace (tracks in two days, blood in a week, a witness in a month, paper in a year; four times as long indoors) and rain or snow washes what lies outdoors. Search here for traces rolls Perception + Investigation; each piece is found at 7, harder as it fades, and goes on the open board. What a find tells follows the year: prints name someone from 1892, blood is typed from 1901, a camera's tape exists from 1970, DNA matches from 1986. The Storyteller's Evidence here window lists what is left and can leave more by hand. New setting "Deeds leave evidence". api.caseBoard.inquiry.
