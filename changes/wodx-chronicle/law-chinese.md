Law of the land, Chinese imperial law and its neighbours (B3494): the written code and the county magistrate, confession before sentence, the bamboo, families and neighbour groups answering for one another, land tax, labour service and the salt monopoly. China's own rows run from the Song and Jin through the Yuan, Ming and Qing, the treaty ports, the Republic, the Nationalists and the People's Republic; Korea from Goryeo through Joseon, Japanese rule and division; mainland Southeast Asia from Angkor, Pagan and Đại Việt through the colonies to independence.
