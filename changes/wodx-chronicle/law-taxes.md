Taxes on the month's upkeep: the Law here card tells a player how big the usual taxes are where they live, and the owner can keep them back (the law may notice, and a caught dodger feeds the law's heat). Dated levies take more on top: the poll taxes of 1377-1381, the Hussite and Thirty Years' War contributions, the French crown's debts, the hut tax, war taxes, requisitions and collectives. A levy weighs on every town of the land, and a crushing one brings riots and risings. A seat in a tax office hides the collector's own dodging. Each line gets a hook for what taxes mean to it.
