Referee ticket (B3520-B3523): a player ticks "ask for a ruling" in Ask the Storyteller for an action the rules do not settle. The Storyteller's client builds one ticket in the approval queue from both sheets: the pool, the crossover referee's case for a named power, and the risks by line and era book (Paradox, a sin against Humanity or the Road, Banality, Rage, Angst, witnesses). The card has Change difficulty (1 to 3 either way) and Complication (a foreshadowing from the story aids). Approving whispers the player a Roll button at the ruled difficulty; a ticket never passes on its own. House rule "Pause the game while a ruling waits", off by default.
