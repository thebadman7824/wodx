bump: minor
effect: every ghoul, human or animal, loses its month's vitae on the sheet when it goes a month without a drink from its domitor, then a Discipline dot a month (Potence last), and rolls against the craving; an animal ghoul with no vitae left ages back to its years, dies past its span and turns to dust past twice that; a drink from the domitor starts the month again
visible: red "-1 vitae" rises from the ghoul's token each month unfed, an empty ghoul's token greys, a ghoul dead of age takes the dead mark and one turned to dust fades to a faint grey shape; feeding brings the colour back

A ghoul's monthly need, carried out by itself (B5050 to B5054), for human and animal ghouls alike, the Storyteller's as well as the players'. Before, the Clan features card only asked someone to take a blood point away; now the sheet changes, the craving is rolled (V20 Dark Ages p. 401: Self-Control at difficulty 7, Willpower for an animal) and a botch sends out the wodxGhoulCraving hook. New world setting "Ghouls need vitae each month", on by default; with it off the old card comes back. Animal ghouls on Creature sheets carry the ghoul row.
