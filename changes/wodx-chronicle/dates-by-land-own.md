Persia's solar year and a character's own count (B5874-B5875): from 1925 a scene in Persia tells the date by the solar Hijra year (Farvardin to Esfand, the year from Nowruz at the spring equinox), and 1976 to 1978 by the Shah's count. A player's character counts the days by its own people's year when the lifepath's people keep another faith than the land (Jewish, Sephardi and Mizrahi, Arab, Turkish, Persian, Amazigh, Tuareg, Moro, Greek, Russian, Serbian, Romanian, Armenian, Tamil, Hindi, Tibetan, Chinese, Khmer, Thai), or by the count the player picks on the Chronicle Clock; the land's holy day name is left out then.
