The Rothenstein saga (setting podSaga, off by default; B3660-B3668): What Sleeps Beneath Rothenstein is told over years and ages instead of ten nights. Nineteen books from a Great Moravian prologue (880) to 2030, plus one on the World of Future Darkness branch, each a story chain of dated chapters, interludes and world steps. The first book runs from October 1197 to 1202 with an interlude between each chapter, and the three sequels keep their own years. Four long clocks (the Red Husbandman, the Ashfield, the Church's eye, the Charter) carry from book to book, the first book's ending (sealed, severed or taken) changes the words of later books, and books the clock passes without play still move the world. The next book that fits the year goes to the approval queue; steps fire hook wodxSaga with an omen-shaped signal. The old session pages are not changed: each chapter links to its notes. api.pod.saga {state, start, skip, ending, clock, tick, rules}; the window has a saga section.
