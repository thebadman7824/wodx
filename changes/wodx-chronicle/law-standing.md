Standing before the law: a character's social rank now counts before the law of the land. Until their privileges or bars end on each land's dates, the unfree face harder trials and sentences and may hold no office of the state above the lowest; the clergy may claim the church's lighter hand; nobles are tried by their peers. In a modern age the wealthy buy the best advocates. A world setting chooses as history (the default), softened (no bar on offices) or off. A player character's gender never counts.
