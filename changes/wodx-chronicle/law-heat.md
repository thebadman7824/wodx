The law's heat: a crime that witnesses see raises the law's heat on the culprit in that land, by how grave the deed is under that land's law and how well its law catches people. Heat cools by the age's means (weeks with a watchman, months with a police file, a year with cameras and databases). The Storyteller gets a card at Asked after, Wanted and Manhunt, with what it means for that kind of character and buttons to make the arrest or let it go. A player with a dot of Law, Politics or Streetwise sees their own standing on the Law here card. A new world setting turns it off.
