Papers and the undying: each land and age keeps its own records of everyone (rolls, parish registers, household registers, censuses, passports, fingerprints, identity cards, citizen's numbers, computer records, biometrics). A new self on paper is a downtime project whose difficulty and goal grow with the records; when it is done the new name goes on the character. On a clock jump, a Kindred, ghoul, mummy or revenant who has kept one self for ten years or more may be noticed, on the Storyteller's card only. The Law here card lists the records kept for anyone with two dots of Law, Politics or Streetwise.
