Law of the land, South Asia (B3495): kings who rule by dharma with Brahmin counsel, caste councils and village assemblies, ordeals, being put out of caste; the Delhi Sultanate's and the Mughals' qadis and the poll tax (lifted by Akbar in 1564, back in 1679, gone after 1720); Company courts, the ban on widow burning (1829), Crown rule and the Indian Penal Code; independence and the 1950 constitution, and the Emergency of 1975 to 1977.
