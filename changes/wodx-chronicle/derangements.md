---
bump: minor
effect: A derangement in the sheet's box now does something in play. When one is set off, the character rolls Willpower at its difficulty (or spends a point) to hold it off for the scene; on a failure the Deranged status goes on and its rolls get harder for the scene (Paranoia +2 on social rolls, Phobia +2 on every roll with a flee-or-freeze held move, and so on). A Virtue loss can write a new one into the box.

Derangements in play (B3913): api.derangements {of, hold, gain, end, boutOf}, a table of fourteen derangements (the V20 list plus Phobia and Perfection) with what sets each off, the Willpower difficulty and the rolls it makes harder, and the Deranged status.
