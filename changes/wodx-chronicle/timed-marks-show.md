Timed marks always show on the token (B6023): a mark lasting the scene or the night had no rounds or seconds, so Foundry did not draw it on the token.
Crippled limb counts +2 difficulty on attacks and defence (B6027), and Branded gives +1 difficulty on social rolls (B6029); both had no rule behind them before.
