The story engines hold no story of their own (Chronicle Packs Desk, B7491-B7492): the saga, fronts, twists, reveals and encounters read the chronicle in play from chronicle-data.mjs, which a chronicle module (WoDX Rothenstein) fills. The saga setting is now "The chronicle's saga over the years"; the chronicle's city default, the saga's name on cards, the judgement card's answer and the place that turns cold after the murders come from the chronicle's data. The four Future Darkness world events set in Rothenstein moved to WoDX Rothenstein with their ids, and join the event list only while that chronicle is in play.
