Regime change: when the clock passes a turn of rule in the homeland's law (a new form of government, or a new ruler), the institutions sweep out many of the old officers, and a new form of government lets the old warrants, outlawries and banishments lapse. A town's rising asks the Storyteller whether it wins; if it does, the town's officers are driven out. Where a parliament is chosen by vote, each player character on a council faces the voters at each election (Manipulation + Politics), and a lost election costs the seat. Each line gets a hook for what a turn means to it. What a turn does is a house rule, marked as speculation.
