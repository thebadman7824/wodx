The night's work (B6220-B6225, Ben's desk pick hd1): a new tool in the Storyteller's hand lists every piece of work the Hand did by itself since the last session ended, the last hour or day, or a time the Storyteller picks. Each proposal carried out (approved on its own or by the Storyteller), each burst of work the clock sets going and each choice the Lone Chronicle seat makes is written down with its document changes. Tick any of them and Undo takes back every change newest first. A field someone changed since is warned of and kept as it is now unless the Storyteller chooses to put it back anyway. An approval taken back this way is marked so its own card's Undo cannot take it back twice. Other modules can wrap their own automatic work with api.nightwork.record.
