Trials: after an arrest the Storyteller gets a card with the forms of trial the land allows that year: ordeal, trial by combat (fought with WoDX Combat), compurgation, an inquiry that needs a confession, a judge, a jury, or a show trial. The roll goes through WoDX Storyteller's call for a roll, open to the table, with the accused's seat on a court and any bribe counted. The verdict is posted and passed on for the sentence.
