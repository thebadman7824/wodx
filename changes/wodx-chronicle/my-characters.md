My characters (B3534-B3536): a roster window for each player, from the Clock or a key: the main character and every ghoul, Kinfolk, retainer, apprentice, thrall, follower and ally they own, each awake, in torpor, dead or away. One click plays another and selects its token. When the played character falls into torpor, dies or goes away, the roster opens to pick someone else. A player asks to add a character; the Storyteller approves and picks the sheet. The Storyteller marks a character away for a long journey.
