Off means off (B6763). While WoDX Voices is switched off, its settings other than the on switch, its voice cache menu and the Voiced and Not voiced entries in the actor menu no longer show, and it no longer browses its voice files when the world starts. Turning it on reloads the world and shows the voice settings again.
