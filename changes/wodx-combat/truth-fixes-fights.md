Fixes from the done-claims audit (B6150-B6154). A player's attack and damage now reach the Storyteller's listeners (fomor blows, Witch's Nimbus, oaths, Rötschreck, a gunshot overwhelming heightened senses, evidence), fired once on the active Storyteller's client. A failed stand-up from a knockdown now loses the action: attacks by that character are refused that turn and the turn conductor skips it; the knockdown lasts until they stand; the -2 Initiative after standing applies whether or not Initiative is rolled again each turn. A token WoDX NPC put on Defend makes no attack that turn and its defences show as full defence (V20 p. 275). When Numbing or another body condition ends, the health track shrinks back at once. With the turn conductor off, Foundry's own Next at the round's end now gives declared, Rage and Hasten extra actions their pass. A gun with no Ability on its sheet rolls Firearms, not Archery. An attack after moving more than half the running speed warns that by the book no attack follows; with moves stopped, a player's is refused. Carrying pace and the walk limit are shown, not enforced, with the default "Show the pace" setting.
