Final audit second pass (B6787 to B6789): the close-combat rules that the cards only described are now applied. Tackle sets an off-balance flag on both fighters for the next round. Riposte is refused without a successful defense earlier in the turn. Hold, Grapple and Clinch put a grip on the victim that lasts until a resisted Strength + Brawl roll at the start of the victim's turn breaks it. A held player cannot attack; the Storyteller is warned instead. A stake that pierces a mortal's heart with 3 or more successes kills. A disarmed weapon is set down as a pile the given number of yards away from the attacker. wodxFed is relayed to the Storyteller's computer, so feeding by a player reaches every listener there.
