---
bump: minor
effect: every shot and throw adds the cover on the map between the shooter and the target to its difficulty, and the attack card says which cover and how much
visible: the attack card shows a "Cover:" line naming the table, counter or wall and the difficulty it added
---
Cover on shots (B4671): a shot or a throw reads WoDX Interactables' cover between the two tokens (a table, a counter, a barricade, a low wall, a table tipped over) and adds it to the difficulty, by the V20 cover table (pdf p. 286), with a line on the card.
