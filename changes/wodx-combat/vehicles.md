Fighting in and around vehicles (B4791 to B4796), from Mage: The Ascension 20th Anniversary pp. 458-463. Those inside a closed vehicle (a coach, a car, a lorry) are protected by it unless the attack scores 4 or more successes, which finds a window; inside an armoured vehicle or an aircraft every blow from outside goes into the vehicle. Damage against a vehicle: up to its Durability bounces off, the rest comes off its Structure, and at its Structure it is wrecked, all written on its tile. At the wheel of a land vehicle the attack menu offers "Ram with the ...": Dexterity + Drive (Ride for a cart) held to the vehicle's Maneuver, at 6, for its Durability + a die for every 10 mph covered this turn + its mass (cars +1, lorries +3, military +5) in bashing. Ramming another vehicle strikes its body, and everyone inside both takes the crash less the Durability (and a lorry's +3), halved when strapped in (aircraft always, cars and lorries from 1970). The one at the wheel swerves in place of dodging (Dexterity + the driving Ability held to the Maneuver, +2 against shots); anyone else sat inside a vehicle cannot dodge. A hit with 3 successes to hit and 6 levels of damage on a motor vehicle sets off the fuel: 12 dice of aggravated fire to all aboard, the vehicle burnt out, and a fire hazard on the map over it. quickAttack takes a weaponId. World setting "Fighting in and around vehicles", on by default.
