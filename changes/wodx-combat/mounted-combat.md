Fighting from the saddle (B4770-B4776), the rules the riding book check left unbuilt. A blow from the saddle may use the mount's Strength and Dexterity when higher (V20 Dark Ages p. 399; a riding horse is the book's large horse, a mule the small one). The mount takes one defensive action a turn for its rider at no cost to the rider: its Dexterity + Athletics, the Athletics held to the rider's Ride, offered in the attack window and chosen by the automatic defense when it is nearly as good as the rider's own. A rider struck rolls Stamina + Ride (Drive on a motorcycle) at the damage successes, 3 at least; thrown, the token is taken off the mount, set down beside it and Knocked down, and takes 2 to 4 dice by the ground, lethal on a botch (Dark Ages: Vampire pp. 244-245, Dark Ages Companion p. 38). The lance needs Ride 3 and Melee 3, or a hit jars the user with bashing dice equal to the damage successes; a botch is 4 lethal dice to the user and a saddle roll; a lance charge that hits makes the target roll Strength + Athletics at 6 or go down, and a rider is unhorsed. A bow shot from a moving horse is +2, +4 at a trot or faster (half the mount's pace or more this turn), in place of the saddle's +1, and nothing with a Mounted specialty (Dark Ages: Vampire p. 244). World setting "Fighting from the saddle", on by default.
