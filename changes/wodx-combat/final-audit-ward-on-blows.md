bump: patch
effect: a Ward or a soak Boost from a power or ritual adds its dice to the soak of a weapon or unarmed blow, not only to a power's harm
visible: the attack card's Soak line counts the Ward's dice and names it ("Warded +3 armour")

A power's Ward now stands against blows (B6732). WoDX Combat read Wards only when a power did harm through soakAndApply; the soak of an ordinary attack left them out.
