Gear in the fight (B3800-B3803): WoDX Combat reads the system's own bonus rows (attack dice and difficulty by weapon type, initiative, soak) on every item and active Bonus, so installed cyberware and worn wonders count in Combat's own rolls. Shields and parrying weapons work as V20 Dark Ages p. 350 and Sorcerers Crusade p. 202 give them: a carried shield sets the parry difficulty (Small Shield 4) and adds to the attacker's difficulty (Standard +1, Buckler +1, War Shield +2); a dagger parries at 5 and a sword adds +1 against close attacks. Installed cyberware from WoDX Library: the Pain Editor eases the wound penalty, a cyberarm adds its Potence to bare-handed damage, and a cyber limb, cowl or faceplate soaks a blow aimed at it with its own dice. Heavy Fire (macro and api.heavyFire) rolls a heavy gun's penetration dice against an armour's difficulty (still target −2, moving +2; 1, 3 and 5 successes harm the crew, stop or destroy it), a naval gun's rating plus crew successes against a ship's armour, or a blast's dice on each targeted token through Soak & Apply.
