New characters, new NPCs and a kept figure or look ask the PC sprite drawer for their sprite by themselves, half a minute after the last change, and the tokens put it on when it is drawn (B8100, #2491; world setting "New and changed characters get their sprite by themselves", on). The Storyteller can also ask for everyone's sprites (B6144): "Draw sprites now" in Paperdoll's settings writes the roster and a request, the PC job runner draws the new and changed characters, and the game learns them when the PC says it is done; the solo world on the New PC is the one that draws (B8101). A token made from a prototype that carried a sprite is fitted at once (B6140; the rest of B6140 is B8053's). Striking, knocking over, tearing or desecrating a thing plays a blow; examining and searching a hiding place play the looking-about move. A sprite is drawn upright whatever way the token was turned (B6145), so a figure walked in by a routine sits on its stool instead of lying at a slant. Riders are drawn astride (B6146): thighs forward round the beast, shins hanging, knees spread when seen from front or back; a rider coming toward the viewer sits behind the horse's head. A walk seen in large jumps on a slow screen still shows its steps and its facing (B6147)
