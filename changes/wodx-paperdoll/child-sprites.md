Children on the map: a character under 18 walks as a child, the same figure drawn shorter with a larger head, in four heights (2 to 6, 7 to 11, 12 to 14, 15 to 17) worked out from the birth year, or from the age at the Embrace or at death. The figure menu has a "Grown or a child" pick and dresses a child the same way as anyone else; "Grown" is not offered under 18, and a child figure picked for an older character marks them a minor, so nothing romantic can involve them (B3480-B3483).
