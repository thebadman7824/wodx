Mounts and vehicles as sprites (B4970-B4975): 257 drawn looks for the 22 kinds of mount (horses, mules, camels, the five supernatural steeds, carts, carriages, bicycles, motorcycles, cars, lorries, armoured cars, tanks, rowing boats, motorboats, balloons and airships, aeroplanes, helicopters, jets), each in the shapes of its era and several colour schemes. A mount shows the look of the chronicle's year, or of the year a vehicle was built; riders and passengers are drawn in its seats with their own sprites, facing as it faces. The palette button on a mount picks a shape and colours or gives it its own picture back. New setting "Mounts and vehicles are drawn as sprites". Gate gatemounts.
