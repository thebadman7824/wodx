bump: minor
what: Sprite tokens: every character and creature as a drawn pixel figure that walks, fights, sits, sleeps and works by its routine; children drawn as children; beasts with hooves, paws or flat feet that graze and lie down; mounts and vehicles.
effect: a character with a routine takes the pose of what it is doing (asleep, at rest, at work, at prayer, drinking, on an errand) and beasts graze or lie down; the token's pose button offers the new poses to whoever may move it
visible: on the map people sit on benches or stools, sleep lying down under a blanket, work with moving hands, hoe the field, kneel and carry sacks; children have short legs and large heads; cattle, horses, sheep, pigs and deer stand on hooves, graze with the head down and lie with folded legs

Everyday life on the map (B5660-B5665). Beasts' legs are built by kind: hooves with a low knee and a high hock, paws, or the flat feet of bears, and they walk in a four-beat walk. Every drawn figure has a standing frame, so one that is not walking stands on all its feet. Children are drawn with their own share of leg, trunk and head for each age band. People are drawn joint by joint sitting on a seat, on a stool, on the ground, kneeling, drinking, working with the hands, tending a hearth, hoeing, carrying a load and asleep; beasts graze and lie down. A new setting, "Figures live their routine on the map" (on by default), makes each character with a WoDX NPC routine take the pose of its hour by itself and get up when it walks, getting to its feet rather than jumping up; a seat or a bed beside it is used when there is one. Sprites drawn before this keep working and stand as before until the next batch.
