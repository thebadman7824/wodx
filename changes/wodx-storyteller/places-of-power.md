Places of power (B3450-B3456): a register of Mage Nodes, chantries and Changeling freeholds under Upkeep, Places of power. Nodes fill with free Quintessence and Tass week by week at the year's rate (M20 pp. 320-321) up to four weeks of output; a mage meditates there through the Avatar (Prime 1 for more than the Avatar a turn) or gathers Tass, which a mage with Prime 3 can absorb later (M20 p. 332). Chantries keep their pooled points by member, take the size and the era's name from the pool and year, and shrink when a member leaves (M20 pp. 308-309). Freeholds give Glamour for a night by the balefire, shared in an oathcircle and to the first sleeper only in a Glade; Reaving rolls Banality and costs ranks; lost ranks come back by the month or by the season; an untended balefire in a dull place fades; a destroyed freehold gives its keepers Banality (C20 pp. 169, 311-313). Node, Chantry and Holdings backgrounds are now ties that need tending, and drawing on the place tends them.
