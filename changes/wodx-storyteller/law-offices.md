Offices of the law and the state: towns now hold a court, the ruler's officers, a tax office and a garrison, and where the law of the land keeps them, the Holy Office and the secret police. Their ranks and names follow the town's law family and year (a qadi's court, a yamen, Soviet first secretaries), and they close and open again with the law. The Storyteller sees a line on what each office means to the Kindred, the Garou, hunters and the Technocracy.
