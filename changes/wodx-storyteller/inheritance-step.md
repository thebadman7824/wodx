The Inheritance step (B3530-B3533): when a character dies (or from the Storyteller's hand for one who leaves play), pick the heir and tick what passes on: items and fetishes, havens and places held, the money the estate card did not divide, the Ledger's boons, debts and oaths, rivals' grudges, and ties (marked as known through the dead). A Garou heir of the blood with Ancestors, or a mage with Past Lives or Avatar, keeps the dead as a past self that WoDX NPC can recall.
