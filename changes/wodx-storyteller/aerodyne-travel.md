Aerodyne and aerocab travel (B6628, review desk 2026-11-16): a new way of travelling from 2023 on the World of Future Darkness branch, on every road kind and across the country map in a straight line, door to door, at the aerodyne's chart speeds less a tenth (203 km an hour safe, 319 flat out; 253 and 398 from 2040). Only the sky slows it. It goes when asked for, and counts as the fastest way when the character's player owns an aerodyne vehicle sheet (or one names them). Flights may set down at up to two places on the way. The aerocab is a hired air taxi: one ride from where the fares stand, ten minutes' wait, a fare ten times a bus's.
