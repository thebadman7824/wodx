Aerodyne travel (B6628): a new way of travelling from 2023 on the World of Future Darkness branch, on every road kind and across the country map in a straight line, at 150 km an hour door to door (an aerodyne's safe 100 mph, Cyberpunk 2020 Concrete Jungle pdf p. 40). Only the sky slows it. Like the airliner for short hops, it is taken only when asked for: a dear hire (an AV taxi at 10 to 15 eb a mile against 3 on the ground, Night City pdf p. 11).
