bump: minor
effect: walking a token into a Way (a region with the behaviour WoDX: Way to) moves the walker and the company close by to the linked scene's far end, spends the Way's minutes on their own clocks and turns each player's screen to the new place; a gate shut for the night, a locked door or a place that is not built in the year refuses and says what can be done; the Ways window on the Storyteller toolbar makes a pair of Ways and lists places with no way in or out
visible: the tokens leave one map and appear at the far door of the next, and each player's view follows them there
---
Ways, the open world's doorways (B4780-B4783): a door, gate, stair, street, road's end, ferry or the edge of the land is a region with the new behaviour WoDX: Way to. Walking a token into it takes the character, the company within three squares and any follower set to follow, to the place it leads to, through the trip rules (own time, the dark, road encounters, places that ask first). Ways can shut at night, lock, or exist only between two years. The Ways window makes both ends at once and checks that every place can be reached and left.
