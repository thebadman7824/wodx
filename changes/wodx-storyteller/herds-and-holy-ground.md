Herd pools by district and holy ground shared by several claims (B3570-B3586). Each district with a hunting ground holds a month's pool of vessels by class, named for the age and land (nobles, clergy, merchants and the poor early on; executives, students and the street later), sized from its town's people. Feeding on a vessel or catching prey on a hunt draws it down; it fills again each month. Past the pool the district thins, then is over-hunted, then stripped: a warning card, Hunt +1 to +3 harder there, a hotter ground, a rumour and the region's hunters, through the hooks those systems already have. Upkeep has a Herds by district window. Holy ground ties a caern, a Node, a freehold or chantry and an Elysium on the places of power register to one strength that follows the age and the Gauntlet: meditating, gathering Tass, dreaming by the balefire and opening the caern take from it and give half when it is weak; a moot fills it a little; every other holder gets a card of each draw; and claims that draw on a weak site build a quarrel that goes to the approval queue. Upkeep has a Holy ground window; api.upkeep.herds and api.upkeep.sites.
