Tales from deeds (B3350-B3354): a player character's public deed (a standing won or lost, a boon or promise kept or broken) starts a tale in the rumour mill, with slots for who, what, where and how. Each retelling can twist it by the teller's leaning toward the character: a gossip makes it bigger, an enemy darker, a friend kinder, a stranger gets the details wrong. Every version is kept with who heard which. Fame and a crowd add first tellers. NPCs meet a character with the version they heard in mind (one step at most). Players get a What they say about you window, grouped by faction. World setting "Deeds become tales" (on). api.rumours.tale, toneFor, aboutView, openAbout.
