Towns that change over time (B3280-B3286): every market is a town with people, prosperity and unrest that move once a week of game time. Food short or gone, dear bread, taxes, closed roads, shut shops, sieges, plague years by region of the world and dated events (famine, war, a crash, a collapse, good times) push them; fires (timber towns in the dry season most), spring floods on rivers, storms on coasts, earthquakes on unsteady ground and failed harvests strike as disasters, and a failed harvest makes bread short in the market. Anger boils over into riots and, held at its height, a rising; a siege won by storm sacks the town; people leave an angry town for the linked town that does best. Auto lands each week with one Undo card (an event where a player character stands waits in the approval queue), Suggest queues everything, Off stops it. A Towns window (from Markets) shows and edits each town: people, prosperity, unrest, what it is built of, its part of the world and its ground, and what it has been through. Big events start rumours from the town's traders, go in the log and fire wodxTown; api.upkeep.towns.of(market) and ofScene(scene) give the condition and a yearly history for other systems.
