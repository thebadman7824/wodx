Threads of Fate, step 3 (B3650-B3656): 153 hook cards. 23 from Rothenstein's own encounters and its three ten-session sequels, two for each of the 44 world regions (the region's own kind of story, and its alternative), 30 doors that fold each line's own concerns into a storyline (Vampire, Werewolf, Mage, Changeling, Wraith, Hunter, Mummy, Demon from 1999, Kuei-jin, mortals and ghouls), and 12 anchors for a player's own hooks, lifepath threads, grudges, debts and tales. Every card has an opening line, what mortals see, what lies beneath for each line, pressure steps and endings that write back. Not yet used in Foundry.
