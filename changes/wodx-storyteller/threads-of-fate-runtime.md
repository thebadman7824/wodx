Threads of Fate, step 4 (B3650-B3656): storylines in play. A new setting, Threads of Fate, is Off by default; Suggest puts each woven storyline in the approval queue, and Open by itself opens it and tells the Storyteller. The weave runs once a week of game time, when a session ends, and at once for great events. An opened storyline gets a Storyteller-only page in the journal folder Threads of Fate, a progress clock with a segment for each step, and an opening rumour. Its steps come due by their days and are whispered to the Storyteller; the night's draw gives its next beat one time in three. The new Threads of Fate window (Storyteller toolbar) ends, locks or refuses storylines, takes back refused stories, takes a player's own hook to weave first, and shows what has happened on the signal board. An ending starts its rumour, goes into the Legends book, and lists what it writes back for the Storyteller to do. Session Zero lines cut cards that touch them, and romance never involves anyone under 18. Rothenstein saga steps go on the board so side stories can hang off them.
