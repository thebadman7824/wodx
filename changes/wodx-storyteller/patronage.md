Patronage of the arts (B3374-B3378): the Storyteller keeps a list of artists (named, or taken from the towns' NPCs with Expression or Crafts of 2 or more) in a new Artists and patrons window, which also shows what is in fashion. A player commissions a work as a downtime project of the new kind Commission a work of art, naming the artist, the form (from a portrait or an illuminated book to an opera from 1600, a novel from 1740, a film from 1900, a record from 1925, a video game from 1975) and the money spent. Finished, the work is made as an item on the patron; its quality comes from the artist's skill, a dot for every two of money, and one when the form is in the fashion of its year and way of life (thirty movements, from Romanesque, Gothic and the troubadours, icon painting, Persian miniatures, ink landscapes, Noh and the floating world, Mughal painting and the griots, through the Renaissance and Baroque, Romanticism and Modernism, to jazz, punk, hip hop and the internet). A fine work adds a Status die at gatherings, two for a masterpiece in fashion, is told to the Storyteller for the Toreador's eye, and a Toreador patron is asked whether it becomes a Muse. A fae patron makes the artist a Dreamer, or Ravages them by harsh handling. Artists without a patron for six months starve and may die or find one. The Storyteller can hold a salon (by era: a court of song, a salon, a gallery opening, a launch party) for a host: a kind tale starts and the host's artists may rise. api.patronage.
