Ventures: a character puts money from the purse into a caravan, a workshop or a trading house on a market, from Property and building or the Markets window. Each weekly market turn it pays a share into the purse or loses part of its value: a glut, nothing to sell, taxes, a monopoly, closed roads, a shut shop, a bad week, and at once a caravan lost on the road, a raid or a trader going broke. It is called what the year and the market's part of the world call it, from a commenda share, a qirad partnership or a share on hundi credit to joint-stock shares, the exchange, funds and a shell company in the World of Future Darkness, and money taken out early costs more the older the age. Resources sets how much goes in at once and, with Influence, how many ventures a character holds; Influence softens a loss. A large stake shows in the Markets window, draws a rival faction's notice and may earn a seat through the approval queue. World setting ventures; api.upkeep.ventures. B3110 to B3113.
