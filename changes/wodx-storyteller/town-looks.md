---
bump: minor
effect: a town turn's fire, flood, storm, earthquake, riot, rising, sack, blight or dread leaves marks on every scene of that town, which last for game time and go with the town card's Undo
visible: fire and smoke over the streets the night it burns, then burnt-out patches for weeks; water in the low ground after a flood; rubble after a quake; barricades after a riot; fog over a town in dread
---
Town events shown on the map (B4620): when a town event lands, every scene of that market gets its marks, through WoDX Chronicle's shared looks layer when it is there and plain locked drawings otherwise. Spots are seeded, so a turn always draws the same marks. They end by themselves with game time, and the town card's Undo takes them back. World setting "Show town events on the map", on by default.
