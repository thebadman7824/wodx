Crisis levers for a region (B3524-B3527): one window in the Storyteller's hand with a slider for each running system in a region: plague strength, Wyrm corruption spread, hunter pressure, and a Gauntlet, Shroud or Banality shift of up to 2 either way. Each slider writes into that system's own record (each outbreak's weekly attack, the hunters' regional pressure, the region's Wyrm spread rate in WoDX Otherworlds, drift at every place in the region), so nothing new is simulated. Every change is dated, noted in the chronicle log for the Legends book, and can be taken back from the window.
