A roll from the sheet itself (an Attribute or Ability clicked, rolled in the system's window) now counts what WoDX Actions' rolls count: merits and flaws, derangement bouts, drink and drugs, wards, powers on the character and conditions that make their own actions harder. The card adds a WoDX line naming each part. The world setting "WoDX on the sheet's own rolls" turns it off. Plain mortals, Hunters outside the Reckoning and every other sheet in the system's own layout now show the Nature button and a Set off link for the Derangements box (B6175, B6178).
