bump: minor
effect: fog makes a Perception roll by sight or hearing 1 harder, rain or falling snow 2 harder and Drive rolls 2 harder, out of doors only; the weather card names V20 as the source of those lines
visible: the roll card shows the fog or rain line on a Perception or Drive roll, and the weather card lists the V20 figures with their page
---
Fog, and rain or falling snow, take V20's Weather Control figures (V20 p. 229): fog +1 to Perception by sight or hearing, rain or snow +2 to those rolls and +2 to Drive, and both halve the reach of ranged weapons. The old house figures for fog (+2 to sight and to shots past short range) and for heavy rain on sight are gone; mist, storm noise, scent, tracks and footing stay house rules (B7054).
