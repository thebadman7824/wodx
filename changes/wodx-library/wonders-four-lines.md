Four more lines make their items. Changeling: gather dreamstuff, craft chimerical garb, weapons, armor, masterworks and forged chimera (made as companions), and imbue or craft Treasures that hold an Art and a store of Glamour (C20). Mummy: inscribe amulets (talismans that raise an Attribute while worn, wards, the scarabs) and effigies (ushabti and creature figures made as companions with their upkeep, the Sekhem Vessel), within the limit of active works (Mummy: The Resurrection). Demon: Enchant Object puts an evocation into a thing, used with Willpower or Faith or attuned, and cursed when the maker is monstrous; Imbue Object binds a soul into a vessel (Demon: The Fallen). Inquisitors bless water, salt and weapons for a scene, and their orders enshrine relics (a house rule from Ben's pick). Item powers can now move Sekhem a success at a time up to a rating, cost a point on a botch, and wait for attunement.
