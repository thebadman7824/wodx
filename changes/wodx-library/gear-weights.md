What things weigh (B6080 to B6082): api.weight gives an item, a good or a container line its weight, from the Storyteller's own figure, about 250 named figures, or the kind and size of the thing, with makes that change over the centuries (armor of iron before 1900, aramid after, Future Darkness composites from 2050) and counts ("silver pennies (7)").
