Bane fetishes: the six Black Spiral Dancer fetishes of W20 Book of the Wyrm (pdf 184-185), the baneklaive among them (Gnosis 7, Strength +3 aggravated, difficulty 7, silver). A Black Spiral Dancer binds Banes and sees only these; a Gaian Garou sees none of them. After the book check, Kinfolk no longer make fetishes unless the new world setting "Kinfolk with Gnosis may make fetishes and talens" is on (off by default) (B3680-B3682).
