Cookery by region (B3100-B3102): the brews, quids and dishes of other lands get recipes of their own (sorghum beer, maize beer, pulque, kava, foaming cacao, the betel quid, chuño, rice crackers, yak butter, tsampa, injera, pemmican, dried fish), with six new raw goods to make them from (sorghum, millet, maguey sap, kava root, areca nuts, betel leaves). Maize is old in the Americas and new elsewhere. The recipe rules take an optional place ({way, region}) for what is to be had there.
