Made supernatural items now do things when used: each power is a Use button that rolls, spends from the item's own pool (blood, Glamour, Faith, Sekhem, Quintessence) or the user's, counts charges and one-use items, adds drawbacks such as Paradox to the user, moves points in and out (a periapt), and posts a card with Undo. Attribute bonuses of a worn item switch on and off with the equipped box through the system's own bonus list. Every line's crafting kit fills the same flag (B3690-B3692).
