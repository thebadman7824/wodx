Mage can make things: a Talisman (a Device for the Technocracy) holding up to one Effect a dot from the Effect keywords the mage knows, one-use charms (gadgets), Quintessence periapts (matrices), trinkets, and fetishes from the Garou list bound with Spirit 4 or Prime 4, by M20 and the Book of Secrets (pdf 152-162). The work is an extended Arete roll; the finished Wonder carries its Effects with its own Arete, its Quintessence and its Paradox. World setting "Making Wonders, charms, periapts and fetishes (Mage)" (B3684-B3688).
