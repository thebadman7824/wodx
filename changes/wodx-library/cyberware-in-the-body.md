bump: minor
effect: ticking a piece of cyberware's equipped box turns on its sheet bonuses (Attributes, Ability dice, attack difficulty, initiative, soak) and takes its Humanity cost with a card; cyber weapons that were plain items now roll as weapons
---
Cyberware in the body (B3795-B3797): the 105 cyberware, 7 cyber limbs, 5 bioware and 3 exoskeletons of World of Future Darkness and Cyberpunk 2020 carry what they do. Each piece's rows on the system's own bonus list turn on while it is installed (equipped) and off when it comes out: Smartgun Link −2 attack difficulty with ranged weapons, Kerenzikov +1 Dexterity and +2 initiative, a Linear Frame sets Strength to 6, 7 or 8, the sense and link pieces add a die to their Ability. The first time a piece goes in, its printed Humanity cost is rolled into a Humanity Index (ten points to a dot of Humanity, or of Gnosis for a shapeshifter, as the Future Darkness article converts it), and a card tells the player and the Storyteller what it took; RealSkinn gives some back. Thirteen cyber weapons that were plain items (Popup Gun, Flame Thrower, Slice N' Dice and the rest) become Melee or Ranged Weapons. Shields, the two parrying weapons, the Great War's heavy guns and their tank and ship armour carry their parry difficulty, added attacker difficulty, penetration and blast dice as data, with a line on each item (B3798). The senses, limb armour, pain editor and Potence of the arms are kept on each piece for WoDX Senses and WoDX Combat to read. Gate: gatecyber.
