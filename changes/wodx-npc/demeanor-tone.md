bump: minor
effect: An NPC's Demeanor now sets the tone of their stock talk. A Bravo or a Curmudgeon speaks a step colder than they feel, and a Gallant or a Bon Vivant a step warmer, so the greeting and small talk a player gets change with the face the NPC shows. Their real attitude does not change.
