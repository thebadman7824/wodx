Final audit fixes (B6760-B6762, B6764-B6766). A player's feeding, social win or power now reaches WoDX NPC on the Storyteller's client, so the Kiss, a vessel's memory of who fed on it, loyalty drift and cult deeds also follow a player's own roll and no longer only the Storyteller's. An NPC servant's roll to resist restraint and a follower's Willpower roll to refuse an order now go to the Storyteller only. The table sees the outcome without the dice. In a family line nobody now marries under 18, in any era. A family member brought into play now keeps the line's gender, and one in a hundred is nonbinary. A player no longer sees the Talk, Orders or Who remembers you buttons while that feature is off. The Undo button on a settled faction boon card now works. Card text no longer shows a decision number.
