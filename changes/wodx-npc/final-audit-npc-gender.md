NPC gender rule everywhere (B6767-B6768). A child born in a family line, a childe, cub, apprentice or fosterling from the Fates brought onto a sheet, and a resident's partner now follow the NPC gender rule: one in a hundred is nonbinary. A brood sheet now has its sex, matching its name. A resident's partner is still the same sex as the head of the house one time in ten. A player's feeding, social win or power now reaches WoDX NPC once per event, also when a second Storyteller plays it.
