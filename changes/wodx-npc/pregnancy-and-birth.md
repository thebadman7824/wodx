Pregnancy and birth (B3220-B3222): the Storyteller starts a pregnancy between two adults (or approves a player's ask in the approval queue), the clock counts about nine months, and the birth comes by itself as a roll of Stamina plus the attendant's Medicine against a difficulty by century, from 8 before 1700 to 4 in a modern hospital. A failure is a hard birth, a botch a loss told gently to the Storyteller only, and a safe birth has no roll. Rest after the birth follows the custom of the time and place. Ghouls, mages, Garou, changelings, Kinfolk and hunters add story hooks to the Storyteller's card, never rules. api.family and the hooks wodxExpecting and wodxBirth.
