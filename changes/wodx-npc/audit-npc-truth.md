Audit fixes (B6170, B6172, B6173): Grieving is known on every client from the start, so a character still Grieving after a reload loses the social die and shows the mark; in Auto mode an NPC whose best option is a power it has picked uses it on its own turn (without a pick it still waits for the Storyteller); a mortal fed on through an unlinked token wakes from the Daze when its time runs out, and a hurt NPC on an unlinked token goes to be tended when the fight ends, when its world actor keeps a routine and it is that actor's only token on the scene; in Suggest mode (the default) the routine card lists each guard's next stop on the beat, and Accept walks it there; freeing a prisoner (the Untie them button) is a deed, so the player's character gains standing in the town and a tale starts. Corrections to earlier notes: every "walks to" in the aftercare, follow-through and motives notes (the hospital, church, tavern, home, a guard's beat, the faithful, servants, a sentry) moves the token at once only with routines on Auto; on the default Suggest the walk is on the routine card and happens on Accept. Overheard talk (the Philippines' galleon and martial-law talk, the town topic) is off by default: switch on "NPCs talk among themselves (overheard)" in WoDX NPC's settings to hear it.
