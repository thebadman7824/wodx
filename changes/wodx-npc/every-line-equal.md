bump: minor
effect: NPCs of every line now get the tools the Kindred had. After a fight a Kuei-jin mends with Chi, a wraith with Pathos, a demon with Faith and a ghoul with blood. A Garou or Fera regenerates instead of healing at a mortal's pace. When a loved one is hurt or dies, the cards give a Kuei-jin, wraith, demon or mummy their own line's check. A Kuei-jin's or Garou's household counts toward their own secret. Kuei-jin NPCs keep a blood cult instead of ghouls, and the cult keeps their house by day. Fates no longer has them Embraced, sired or committing diablerie. A household can be seeded to serve a master of any line, as Kinfolk, a cult, thralls, the enchanted or conditioned agents. A wraith can find a hidden loyalty with Lifesight.
visible: a floating "+2 wounds (Chi)", "(Pathos)", "(Faith)" or "(regenerates)" rises over the NPC's token as it mends, in its line's colour; the servant ring shows on a resident bound to a Garou, mummy, demon or changeling master; the residents window lists each master with the right words ("Kinfolk of", "Blood cult of", "Thralls of").
