bump: minor
effect: NPCs of every line now get the tools the Kindred had. After a fight a Kuei-jin mends with Chi, a wraith with Pathos, a demon with Faith and a ghoul with blood. A Garou or Fera regenerates instead of healing at a mortal's pace. When a loved one is hurt or dies, the cards give a Kuei-jin, wraith, demon or mummy their own line's check. A Kuei-jin's or Garou's household counts toward their own secret. Kuei-jin NPCs keep a blood cult instead of ghouls, and the cult keeps their house by day. Fates no longer has them Embraced, sired or committing diablerie. A household can be seeded to serve a master of any line, as Kinfolk, a cult, thralls, the enchanted or conditioned agents. A wraith can find a hidden loyalty with Lifesight. NPCs of every line now refill their own pool each night as their book says: a changeling takes Glamour from its freehold or a Dreamer, a wraith Pathos from its Passions and Memoriam, a demon with no thralls reaps Faith from a mortal, and shapechangers, mages and mummies are marked for WoDX Actions' own daily Gnosis, Quintessence and Sekhem rolls. When a loved one is hurt, taken or killed, each line's check is rolled and a failure lands on the sheet (frenzy, Angst, Torment, Willpower or Banality). A secret-keeper's household that talks starts a true rumour. Looking someone over rolls the viewer's line's sense, and a find reveals a hidden loyalty in the Relationships tab.
visible: a floating "+2 wounds (Chi)", "(Pathos)", "(Faith)" or "(regenerates)" rises over the NPC's token as it mends, in its line's colour; the servant ring shows on a resident bound to a Garou, mummy, demon or changeling master; the residents window lists each master with the right words ("Kinfolk of", "Blood cult of", "Thralls of"); "+2 Glamour", "+1 Pathos" or "+1 Faith" floats over a refilling NPC and the Storyteller gets one "NPCs refill their pools" card a night; the loved-one card shows the roll and "+1 Angst", "Frenzy" or "It holds"; the household card says a rumour has started; the finder gets a card naming whom the spy serves, and the tie appears.
