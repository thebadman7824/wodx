After a fight, an unlinked NPC that has no routine of its own to carry the duty (a passer-by, a placed generic NPC, or one of several tokens sharing a world actor) is now tended as itself: its token walks into a hospital or house of worship on its scene, or with none there is hidden while away, and comes back once no longer laid up; a passer-by fed on goes off to rest the same way after the Daze and is back in the morning (B6665).
