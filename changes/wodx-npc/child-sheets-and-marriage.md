Children as characters (B3223-B3228): a newborn is a child sheet, marked so the age floor always holds, with Attributes, Abilities and Willpower held to caps by age. A skip of whole years brings one approval card per child: a lifepath scene from the childhood or teen years (never one about love or marriage), schooling or work by century, region and way of life, a Nature from 13, and coming of age at 18, when the mark comes off. Players may not own or make a character under 18 unless the world setting "Players may play children" is on (off by default). Player-asked marriage (B3229-B3231): propose from a lover or friendly tie; an NPC answers by warmth (Devoted yes, Fond rolls Charisma + Empathy), a player answers on a card; the courting custom names who must agree, a rank gap draws an objection and a feud between the families blocks the match; the banns, contract or civil notice run on the clock; then the wedding card goes to the Storyteller, and approving it marries them. Hooks wodxGrownUp and wodxProposal.
