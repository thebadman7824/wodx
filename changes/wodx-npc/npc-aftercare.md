effect: an NPC taken by the law is held at the watch-house until the verdict, serves a sentence off the maps (back home when it ends), is banished or put to death; a hurt NPC heals with blood if Kindred, goes to the hospital, church or home to be tended and mends on the books' healing clock; a rival who backs down walks off with a grudge and the next meeting of the two is likelier to come to blows; a mortal fed on without consent is Dazed and rests at home until morning; an NPC who loses someone close mourns by faith (days at home, evenings at prayer) and remembers who did it; a robber who got money spends the night at the tavern, and the victim can report it to the watch (theft heat and a rumour); married NPCs share a home and move back on a divorce; a follower brought by a fetch goes back to its day after an hour
visible: the held NPC stands in the watch-house; the hurt walk to the hospital or church and stay there; the token of a fed-on vessel shows Dazed and walks home; mourners walk to the church each evening; the robber walks to the tavern; the one who gives way walks off the map; a moved spouse walks to the new home; the health track empties as the clock runs
