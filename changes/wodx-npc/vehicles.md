---
bump: minor
what: NPCs get into a mount or vehicle by themselves: to flee faster, or to give chase to a foe beyond a run; at the wheel they move at its pace and ram
effect: an NPC whose best choice is to flee, with a free horse, car or other vehicle it can drive within a run, goes for it and takes the reins; an aggressive NPC with every foe out of reach does the same to give chase; at the reins it flees and closes in at the mount's or vehicle's pace, and its attacks may be a ram
visible: the NPC's token runs to the vehicle on the map and takes the reins, then the vehicle tile moves with it; the turn card offers "Get into a vehicle" with why, and says it drives off
---
NPCs and vehicles (B4797, house rule). The fight reading lists the free mounts and vehicles on the scene in the year (nobody at the reins, a seat left, not wrecked) with the distance and whether the NPC has the dots to drive it. A new option, "Get into a vehicle", scores just above flight when flight is the best choice, or just above the best attack when every foe is out of reach and further than a run (aggression a half or more). Auto mode carries it out: the token walks beside the tile and takes the reins through WoDX Interactables' own Mount it action. At the reins the NPC's flight and approach use the mount's or vehicle's pace, so it drives off or runs a foe down, and the ram is among its attacks.
