---
bump: minor
effect: a failed simple or teamwork roll at the same task and target makes the next try in the scene +1 difficulty a failure, and at 10 the Action window says the task cannot be attempted now; a power or Action roll past 10 needs one more success a point
visible: the Action card names the try ("Retry (third try) +2 diff") and the extra successes needed; the Storyteller's Clear failed tries button sits on the Scene row of every line's menu
---
Trying again gets harder (B7121, V20 Dark Ages pdf 324; Demon: The Fallen pdf 223; Hunter: The Reckoning pdf 172): a failed simple or teamwork roll is remembered per character, task and target for the scene, and each new try is +1 difficulty a failure until the task cannot be tried; extended and resisted rolls are left out. A success, a new scene or the Clear failed tries button starts it again, and the world setting "Trying again gets harder" switches it off. Power windows no longer hold their difficulty at 10 before the roll, so past 10 each point needs one more success (B7120).
