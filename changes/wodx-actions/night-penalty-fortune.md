Feeding against a clan's law (the Dark Ages Salubri and their warriors, V20 Dark Ages pdf pp. 102, 104) now takes 2 dice from every action roll for the rest of the night, not only an icon; soak, damage and Initiative keep their dice. Lucky, Destiny and Fate now offer "roll again if it fails" in every power window and in the frenzy, Rötschreck and sin windows, rolled before the effect is worked out. A player cannot move the token of a character who is asleep for the day, Asleep, in torpor or staked (B6370-B6372).
