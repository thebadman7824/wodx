The day's sleep (B4890-B4897). As the clock moves, every Cainite the clock covers falls asleep by day and rises at their own hour (V20 p. 311, V20 Dark Ages p. 113): the sleep marker shows on the token and a word floats over it. "At dawn, try to stay awake" in Sun and haven rolls the Road by itself at dawn (V20 pp. 262-263, Deep and Light Sleeper count); the turns run down in a fight and by the clock, then sleep takes them. A sleeper who is hurt rolls to wake by itself, and a botch sleeps until sundown. Rising heals bashing and lethal with blood above the hunger line (setting "Heal on rising"). New Night at dusk and the sun's burns are now automatic by default, and a world on the old buttons is moved over once with a note to the Storyteller. Rolls for the Storyteller's Cainites go to the Storyteller only. Settings: "The day's sleep", "Heal on rising".
