A carried ward or Magic Resistance now makes every line's power one harder when it is aimed at its bearer, as it already did for magick, Gifts, cantrips, evocations and Arcanoi: Kuei-jin Chi powers, Dark Ages Mage spells, Dark Ages Fae cantrips, Mummy Hekau, Orpheus Horrors, Hunter edges, Numina, hedge magic and Inquisitor Blessings, and the fomor powers aimed at someone (harm, a hold, sway or fear, or a drain on the target's sheet). The card names the ward. A power with no one else targeted, or on the user's own body, is not touched (B6662).
