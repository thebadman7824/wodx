The boon scale's source is corrected (B7636): V20 Dark Ages has no prestation or boon scale, so the Prestation journal now calls the scale a WoDX house rule in the Dark Ages, taken from the later Camarilla custom. A settled boon (repaid, called, refused or void) now goes on the History pages of the two it binds. Before, it went on the pages of whatever names were left in the window's "Record a boon" fields, so the debtor's page could miss the refusal.
