Dark Ages fae and Changeling Treasures (B6550-B6566). The Dominions' full cantrip lists (Dark Ages: Fae pp. 123-162) are presets: "Add from the book" on the fae sheet picks them up to the sheet's dots, and Cast a cantrip takes each one's difficulty (a number, or the target's Willpower), the successes it needs, its Weaving or Willpower cost, once-a-day and once-a-scene limits, the age floor for the romantic ones, and what it does past the card (the Sword's aggravated blade for the scene, Freeze Death, Pure Form, and the Charm, Persisting Charm, True Enchantment and Dominion Writ cantrips, which make a Treasure holding a known cantrip). Sprites have the seven Charms of p. 97 (Animate Tools, Blast and Healing bought by the level, Paralyzing Stare, Reform, Speech, Swift Flight) in the Spirit Charm window. Treasures for every era: faerie dust, the invested and oathcrafted Treasures of Dark Ages: Fae (pp. 187-191), Changeling 20th Treasures holding any Art's cantrip (a Glamour point from the Treasure's store first, pp. 324-325), the named ones and the two Treasures of Banality; Use a Treasure, Keep a Treasure's oath and Give a Treasure (GM) are on both fae menus. Also: a Fortune's own shift and a Hasten's extra actions now reach the roll (the Dark Ages rotes Antics, Slowdream, Berserk and Snowglide give their actions too), a Boost can reach every roll or Willpower rolls alone, and a Ward against the mind adds its dice when a target resists a power that reaches into the mind.
