Drained and the other registered conditions now count on the rolls of "With this person" (Persuade and the other social acts, Look them over and Ask about them, Lift and Plant) and on the feeder's own rolls when feeding (spotting a sickness, resisting it, a clan's feeding law, the Hunger roll, Kindred-blood addiction), the same way the Action window counts them: each condition's difficulty is added and named on the card, and a sight-only condition counts only on a Perception roll (B6663).
