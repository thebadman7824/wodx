bump: minor
effect: a character on one of nine Dark Ages Paths sees that Path's own sins in the sin window and the Road dialog, and a Path of Journeys, Chivalry or Devil walker rolls the Virtues the book gives that Path for sin, frenzy and Rötschreck
visible: none, a rules and data correction with no map change; the new sins and Virtues show on the sin card, the Road dialog and the roll cards
---
Dark Ages Paths follow the book (B7630-B7632): nine Paths add their own sins to their Road's chart at the levels V20 Dark Ages gives (Hunter, Journeys, Liberation, Breath, Community, Illumination, Thorns, Veils, Making); the Paths of Journeys, Chivalry and the Devil roll their own Virtues; the Path of Rebellion is named the Path of the Devil, and the Path of Screams takes the chart WoDX had filed as a separate Path of Damnation. Saved characters on the old names keep their Path.
