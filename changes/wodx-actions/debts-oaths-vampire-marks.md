Debts and oaths, and the vampire's marks (B3560 to B3565). The Storyteller can call a Blood Hunt in the form of its age and sect (a lord's hunt, the Camarilla's Blood Hunt, the Sabbat's Wild Hunt, the barons' hunt): it goes on the Prestation journal, sets the Kindred NPCs hunting, starts a rumour, takes the target's Status, and ends at Final Death or when lifted (V20 pp. 24, 28, 493, 510). Changelings swear and break oaths with each C20 oath's own cost (pp. 204, 264-267), and Dark Ages fae oaths break with the punishment they name; everyone bound is told. A player's vampire in frenzy or Rötschreck gets a card each turn of what the Beast wants, or the NPC planner takes the token, with a Willpower point to take a turn back (V20 p. 310). Low Humanity shows in the bearing toward mortals and the blood it takes to seem alive (V20 pp. 269, 310), and the Nightfall card gives the hour each vampire rises (V20 p. 311). Waking after a torpor longer than a lifetime brings a card of what changed, old tongues and Abilities behind the age (V20 torpor only). Diablerie leaves black veins that Aura Perception sees and a taint other Kindred may feel (V20 pp. 294-295), with an optional rule, off by default, for a mighty victim's soul pushing through.
