Binding card (B3528): when a Fear, Sway, Bind, Possess or Hold result lands on a player character (from Dominate, Presence, Mind, the Arts or any power with those keywords, applied on its own or approved), that character's players get a card naming the command, who laid it, how long it lasts and the resist roll where the power's book allows one. Nothing holds the token. Other modules can send the same card through the effects api (bindingCard).
