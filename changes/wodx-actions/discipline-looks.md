Disciplines can be seen on the map (B7641-B7643): when a power is rolled, the worker's token glows, darkens, blurs or tints for six seconds by its Discipline and family (Majesty's gold, Dread Gaze's dark red, Arms of Ahriman's wide shadow, Lure of Flames' fire), for all seventeen Disciplines the Dark Ages clans start with; Auspex shows only to its user and the Storyteller, and Obfuscate shows nothing.
