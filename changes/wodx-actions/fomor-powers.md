Fomor powers from the book (B5610-B5619). Every power on W20 pp. 430-439 has a summary in WoDX's own words and a page reference, and it does something in play. Always-on powers come from the sheet: Armored Hide's soak, Exoskeleton and Unnatural Strength, Footpads, Nimbleness, Claws and Fangs and the other natural weapons, Darksight, Armored Skin's lethal soak, Berserker's Rage, Spirit Ties' Gnosis, Regeneration, Extra Speed, Immunity to the Delirium, Triatic Sense, and the splash of Poison Tumors, Toxic Secretions, Tar Skin and the Venomous Bite. The others roll from the Fomor power window: Mind Blast, Eyes of the Wyrm, Roar of the Wyrm, Webbing, Fiery Discharge, Brain Eating, Homogeneity, Infectious Touch, Wings, Invisibility, Shadowplay, Size Shift and the rest. Each uses the book's cost and roll, counts its uses a scene or a day, and puts its result on the fomor or its targets. NPC fomor pick them by themselves through the NPC power choice.
