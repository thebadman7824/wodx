Other-world sights on the player's own map (B3540-B3547). A successful peek across the Gauntlet lays the Penumbra over the peeker's map: the map's own reflection, Banes from the scene's Wyrm corruption, a web veil for the era, and the spirits there marked friendly, watching, wary or hostile from the peeker's standing. Mages get Prime, Time, Entropy and Correspondence sights at the first rank of each Sphere (Dark Ages mages through the Pillar that fits); turning one on rolls the casting roll and the successes set how long it holds. Prime shows places of power, Tass carried and vulgar strain; Time shows echoes of what happened at the place (map events, wounds, crossings, magick); Entropy marks the hurt; Correspondence the ways to other places. Changelings and the enchanted see other changelings' fae mien, freeholds and the Dreaming dressed, and Banality greying the dull. Wraiths see the living scene from the Shadowlands, with their Fetters lit and other wraiths plain. A new token tool lets the Storyteller enchant mortals, a wraith tie Fetters to things on the map, and a changeling set a mien picture.
