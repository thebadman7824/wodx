Cyberware reaches the senses (B3799): the sense pieces installed on a character (WoDX Library, api.cyber.installedFx) count in every Senses roll. Low Lite, Thermograph and Infrared optics, the radar sensor and the sonar implant take away the darkness step as Eyes of the Cat does; Amplified Hearing, Image Enhancement, the boosts and the like make their sense one step easier each. A power that already sees in the dark keeps its own name. Gate: gatecyber step 7.
