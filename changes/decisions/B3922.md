## B3922. More events move the factions (2026-11-14)
No-effect audit w1. Several dated events in the chronicle passed with only a line in the history. WoDX NPC now offers a new power for each of them when the clock reaches it: changelings in hiding at the Shattering, the Giovanni at the fall of the Cappadocians, a Justicar's archons at the First Conclave, a freehold at the Resurgence, Kuei-jin envoys after the Week of Nightmares, a Fallen and their faithful at the escape from the Abyss, the Children of Carthage at the Venice Conclave, a Cyberpunks crew and a corporate hunter team in Future Darkness. Events that wipe out a line also put to sleep any faction named for it: Croatan, Bunyip, Ravnos, Cappadocian and Doissetep. A test checks that every classic event, except the Time of Judgment and the Avatar Storm (which have rule effects of their own), has at least one effect. Reverse: remove the entries.
