## B7311. gatetruthactions step 6 keeps the fight to itself and logs who moved it (2026-11-18)
Gates. Step 6 (the frenzy check held turn by turn) failed after other gates in jobs 1944, 2006 and 2014, and passed alone. Each time the hold read 1 turn left right after the first roll, where it should read 2. The hold only goes down on the bearer's own turn (beastTurn), so one extra turn of the bearer ran before the step looked. The step moves the turns by hand, so for the step the turn conductor is set to off and WoDX NPC is off on the gate's scene (the other fighter is an NPC, and a world at Balanced runs NPC turns by itself); both are put back after the step. The step's detail now carries a log of every turn move and every change to the hold, each with the first frames of the code that made it, so a failure names what moved the fight. Not yet run in Foundry.

Reverse: take the conductor and scene mode lines, the hooks and the log out of step 6 in tools/gate/gatetruthactions.mjs.

## B7312. gate7b's Auto steps say what the NPC read (2026-11-18)
Gates. "Auto: the NPC moves round the wall" failed after other gates in jobs 1944 and 2006 with the runner choosing to flee ("faces what it fears": a guard fears the supernatural once it shows), and "a lost action" failed in 2006 with a plan card instead of "cannot act". No pass condition changed. Not yet run in Foundry.

## B7313. gate7b's hero passes for human (2026-11-18)
Gates. The touched-gates run on this change (job 2016) gave the reason for the Auto step's failure: the hero was "Ada (6d, wraith)", a Wraith, so the guard saw an obvious foe it fears ("Flee (4.1): faces what it fears") and fled instead of walking round the wall. The hero was the first player's actor in the world's order. In the testbed's known-good world (private/cloud-foundry/testbed) the player's actors by id are Ilse (Kindred), Ada (Wraith), Promethean Urchin, a deprecated sheet, Brother Stanislaw and Ulf (Garou), so a pick by id takes Ilse, and a run that does not gets Ada next. gate7b now takes the first player's actor by id that passes for human and was not made by another gate (a gate's name, or owned by everyone) (not a spirit or wraith, no obvious effect or flag: WoDX NPC's own rule), then the first one. A first try that preferred Mortals by name took Brother Stanislaw and broke two other steps (job 2021), so it was replaced. Not yet run in Foundry. Why Ilse was not first on some runs is still open.

Reverse: put back `game.actors.find(a => a.hasPlayerOwner && a.type !== "Group")` for the hero in cast() in tools/gate/gate7b.mjs.
