B6920. No Angst from Pandemonium in the Labyrinth (owner's ruling on the Final Audit Desk, 16 Nov: follow the book). Wraith 20th p. 204 gives the Shadow a point of temporary Angst per 1 rolled on Pandemonium in Stygia and the Tempest, and says the Shadow never gains Angst from Pandemonium in the Labyrinth. `realmAngst` in WoDX Actions gave it for the Labyrinth too; it now gives it only for the Tempest or Stygia realm, and the card's reason line says so. Harness phase6c (the realm Angst case), gate gate6c step "Pandemonium in the Labyrinth gives no Angst".
effect: a Pandemonium roll in the Labyrinth adds no Angst to the sheet and its card shows no Angst line; in the Tempest each 1 still adds a point.
visible: none, Angst is a sheet track and a card line; nothing on the map changes.

Reverse: put `|| realm === "labyrinth"` back in realmAngst.
