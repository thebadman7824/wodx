## B6330. One library of map objects (2026-11-15)
Tools. Placed objects kept getting their size, their detail and their facing against walls wrong (ovens facing walls, bare-looking rooms, shutters clipping into walls). tools/pixelmap/library now defines every object a pixel map may hold once: what a player calls it, its true size in feet (a range where real ones vary) with the period source of that size, whether it has a front and how much room the front needs, how it stands against a wall, the years it belongs to, its lands, the rooms and trades it serves, and its drawing seen from straight above with its front to the south. 97 objects to start with: the furniture, fittings, fires, containers, goods, trades, outdoor pieces, plants and church furnishings the maps already used.

Reverse: delete tools/pixelmap/library and return maps/farmhouse.py and scene.py to B5748.

## B6331. Objects are placed only through the library (2026-11-15)
Tools. A map places an object with library.put(rec, id, x, y, face) or library.by_wall(rec, id, wall, at, toward), which sets its back a hand's breadth off the wall's face and turns its front to the room. Doors and windows go in with library.door() and library.window(), drawn at the wall's own thickness so their frames fill the wall exactly. Every object is drawn twice, on a light and a dark floor, so the object, its shadow and the light of its fire are told apart and laid on the real floor cleanly. A crate's lid, taken off, now leans in front of it, toward the room.

Reverse: as B6330.
