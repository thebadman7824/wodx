## B6460. Every Cataclysm world object checked (2026-11-15)
Tools. All 1,791 kinds of world object in Cataclysm: Dark Days Ahead, the base game and every mod, were checked against the object census one by one: 638 it already held, 219 are states or variants of another, 426 are walls, floors and ground, 228 are Cataclysm's own (alien and nether growths, starships, game mechanics), and the rest gave 153 objects the census lacked, from the air compressor and band saw to the standing stone, ritual circle and Umbral rift, plus two World of Darkness ones the check suggested (blighted fungal growth, fleshcrafted growth). The census now holds 645 kinds of object. New rooms: beach, lighthouse, silo, reactor, swimming pool, oilfield, golf green and aircraft cabin. Only ideas were taken; every name is our own.

Reverse: remove the Cataclysm check rows and the new rooms from census.py.
