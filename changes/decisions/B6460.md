## B6463. A colour cast no longer passes as a new era (2026-11-15)
Tools. The gate's "each era its own look" check also compares each variation with its twin one era earlier with colour, tint and brightness taken out (the light-and-dark pattern only). A twin that differs by less than 0.08 there counts as the same look, so an era that differs from the one before only by a colour cast fails, and the era must change how the thing is made: its shape, parts, fittings or detail.

Reverse: remove _shape and SHAPE_SAME from vcheck.py.

## B6464. One shared worn and broken look; no stamped marks; details must line up (2026-11-15)
Tools. The owner's review of the seating desk found the details were the trouble: patches that do not look like patches, strange dark circles, random cracks, and misaligned details. Wear is no longer a variation: every variation is drawn sound, and an object in a damaged or broken state gets one layer shared by every object, as the owner asked: damaged shows one clear crack across it, the same shape on everything; broken replaces it with a pile of rubble in its own colours. DRAWING.md now forbids stamped stains, rings, cracks and patch squares, asks that every detail sit centred and aligned on its part, and puts the parts that name an object from above first. The gate fails a variation far less symmetric than the rest of its object, which is how a stray or off-centre detail shows.

Reverse: restore the condition axis in variants.py and remove wear_layer from core.render and _asym from vcheck.py.
