## B6620. Four flying kinds on the Future Darkness branch (2026-11-16, desk 2026-11-16)
WoDX Interactables. The aerodyne (a private flying car, 4 seats, Pilot), the aerocab (the hired air taxi, a pilot and four fares, its own kind at the desk's pick), the armoured AV (police, corporate or military, a crew of three and ten aboard, as Mage 20th's military utility helicopter; Pilot 3 as a military aircraft, Mage 20th p. 462) and the hover bike (1 seat, Drive on the ground, Pilot in the air, Concrete Jungle pdf p. 40), from 2023 only, so they exist only past the main timeline's last year. Earlier, a tile of one becomes a helicopter or a motorcycle.

Reverse: drop the four kinds from MOUNTS, WAYS and BODIES.

## B6621. Storyteller figures, as every other vehicle (2026-11-16, desk 2026-11-16)
WoDX Interactables. At the desk the Cyberpunk 2020 numbers were not kept: like every other row in chase-rules, each kind stands on the Mage 20th chart row (pp. 460-462) of the vehicle that does its job, and the Cyberpunk books only say what the kind is and does. Aerodyne: the News Copter, safe 140, top 220 mph, Maneuver 6, Durability 4, Structure 6. Aerocab: the Large Helicopter, 150 / 250, 6, 5 / 6. Armoured AV: the Military Utility Helicopter, 180 / 280, 7, 8 / 10. Hover bike: the ATV on the ground (30 / 70, 5), the aerodyne's speeds in the air with Maneuver 7, and the Light Motorcycle's frame, 2 / 3, which like any cycle covers nobody (p. 460). From 2040 every one goes a quarter faster and handles one better (the desk's pick, house reading).

Reverse: change the rows in chase-rules.

## B6622. Height for everything that flies (2026-11-16)
WoDX Interactables. A flying mount or vehicle has a height: the elevation of its tile or token and of everyone aboard, set by the one at the controls through their own token's elevation. Held to the kind's ceiling (house figures, high enough not to matter on a map, but the hover bike's 20 metres) and to the Ability that flies it (no dots, no take-off). A hover bike off the ground is an aerodyne in every rule (its air figures, Pilot, where it can go).

Reverse: setting flight off is not offered; remove setupFlight from main.mjs.

## B6624. A craft that falls (2026-11-16)
WoDX Interactables. Wrecked in the air, a craft falls and everyone aboard takes the fall's dice (V20 core pdf p. 304: a die every 10 feet, 10 at most, lethal from 100 feet), less its Durability when its body covers them, halved when strapped in (Mage 20th p. 459; aircraft strap everyone in). A craft that drops unwrecked also takes the fall's levels past its Durability off its Structure.

Reverse: bringDown in flight.mjs.

## B6626. Flying low in a chase (2026-11-16)
WoDX Interactables. A flier below the rooftops takes the ground's difficulty as a car would, +1 more flat out (the fast low flight of Forlorn Hope pdf p. 67), which an armoured AV's terrain-following radar takes off. A quarry flying above the rooftops has no cover: the +1 to keep sight in cover does not apply. House readings.

Reverse: lowFlight() in flight-rules.

## B6627. Height on the map (2026-11-16)
WoDX Paperdoll, WoDX Interactables. A flier's picture rises with its height, a square and a half at most, and its shadow stays on the ground, shrinking and fading to 300 feet; a ring of dust blows out at take-off and landing. Map events "takeoff" and "landing" (Lift-off, Touch-down).

Reverse: liftOf() in flight-rules and the shadow layer in mounts.mjs.

## B6628. Travel by aerodyne and aerocab (2026-11-16, desk 2026-11-16)
WoDX Storyteller. A way of travelling from 2023 on the Future Darkness branch, door to door over any road or water and straight across the country map, at its chart's safe speed less a tenth for lifting off and landing (203 km an hour, 253 from 2040), or flat out at its top speed (319, 398), which makes trouble on the way half again as likely. Only gales, fog and storms slow it. At the desk both of its options were kept: it goes when asked for, and it counts as the fastest way when the traveller has one of their own (a vehicle sheet of that kind their player owns, or that names them). To travel at a finer grain, a flight (by aerodyne or aerocab) may set down at up to two places on the way, chosen in the travel window. The aerocab is a hired ride that picks the fares up where they stand: one ride, a ten-minute wait, a fare of a three-hundredth of a month's wage a km (ten times a bus; Cyberpunk 2020 puts an AV taxi at four or five times a ground cab, Night City pdf p. 11).

Reverse: drop MODES.aerodyne, "aerodyne" from OWN, HIRED["hire:aerocab"], CRUISES and the via and cruise fields of the travel window.

## B6629. A hover bike lifts no load (2026-11-16, desk 2026-11-16)
