## B6620. Three flying kinds on the Future Darkness branch (2026-11-16)
WoDX Interactables. The aerodyne (a personal or hire flying car, 4 seats, Pilot), the armoured AV (police, corporate or military, 12 seats: the E-Van's ten troopers and its crew, Cyberpunk 2020 Forlorn Hope pdf p. 96; Pilot 3 as a military aircraft, Mage 20th p. 462) and the hover bike (1 seat, Drive on the ground, Pilot in the air, Concrete Jungle pdf p. 40), from 2023 only, so they exist only past the main timeline's last year. Earlier, a tile of one becomes a helicopter or a motorcycle.

Reverse: drop the three kinds from MOUNTS, WAYS and BODIES.

## B6621. Cyberpunk 2020 figures turned to V20 terms (2026-11-16)
WoDX Interactables. Speeds: the hover bike 35/80 mph on the ground and 100/195 in the air (Concrete Jungle pdf p. 40); the aerodyne the same air figures; the armoured AV top 200 mph (the A-20 panzer built on the AV-4 line, Forlorn Hope pdf p. 42). Maneuver from Mage 20th's nearest aircraft (news copter 6, military utility helicopter 7), and one more for the hover bike in the air (the book's +1 there). Bodies: Durability = SP / 6 and Structure = SDP / 10, rounded (house reading), which puts the AV-6's SP 45 and SDP 100 (Night City Stories pdf p. 86) exactly on Mage 20th's military utility helicopter, 8 and 10; the hover bike's SP 5 and SDP 35 give 1 and 4, and like a jetpack its body covers nobody. No book held gives a private aerodyne's figures: 4 and 6, Mage 20th's news copter.

Reverse: change the rows in chase-rules.

## B6622. Height for everything that flies (2026-11-16)
WoDX Interactables. A flying mount or vehicle has a height: the elevation of its tile or token and of everyone aboard, set by the one at the controls through their own token's elevation. Held to the kind's ceiling (house figures, high enough not to matter on a map, but the hover bike's 20 metres) and to the Ability that flies it (no dots, no take-off). A hover bike off the ground is an aerodyne in every rule (its air figures, Pilot, where it can go).

Reverse: setting flight off is not offered; remove setupFlight from main.mjs.

## B6623. Bands of height (2026-11-16)
WoDX Interactables. On the ground; low (under 50 feet, among the rooftops); over the rooftops (to 300 feet); high above. House figures.

Reverse: change BANDS in flight-rules.

## B6624. A craft that falls (2026-11-16)
WoDX Interactables. Wrecked in the air, a craft falls and everyone aboard takes the fall's dice (V20 core pdf p. 304: a die every 10 feet, 10 at most, lethal from 100 feet), less its Durability when its body covers them, halved when strapped in (Mage 20th p. 459; aircraft strap everyone in). A craft that drops unwrecked also takes the fall's levels past its Durability off its Structure.

Reverse: bringDown in flight.mjs.

## B6625. Nobody at the controls (2026-11-16)
WoDX Interactables. An aerodyne, an armoured AV or a hover bike drops at once ("AVs don't glide", Forlorn Hope pdf p. 72). An aeroplane or a jet glides, a helicopter autorotates, a balloon drifts, a gryphon flies on: a card says so and the Storyteller plays it (house reading). Someone who leaves a craft in the air falls, unless their token flies.

Reverse: unpiloted() in flight-rules.

## B6626. Flying low in a chase (2026-11-16)
WoDX Interactables. A flier below the rooftops takes the ground's difficulty as a car would, +1 more flat out (the fast low flight of Forlorn Hope pdf p. 67), which an armoured AV's terrain-following radar takes off. A quarry flying above the rooftops has no cover: the +1 to keep sight in cover does not apply. House readings.

Reverse: lowFlight() in flight-rules.

## B6627. Height on the map (2026-11-16)
WoDX Paperdoll, WoDX Interactables. A flier's picture rises with its height, a square and a half at most, and its shadow stays on the ground, shrinking and fading to 300 feet; a ring of dust blows out at take-off and landing. Map events "takeoff" and "landing" (Lift-off, Touch-down).

Reverse: liftOf() in flight-rules and the shadow layer in mounts.mjs.

## B6628. Travel by aerodyne (2026-11-16)
WoDX Storyteller. A way of travelling from 2023 on the Future Darkness branch: 150 km an hour door to door over any road or water and straight across the country map; only gales, fog and storms slow it; taken only when asked for.

Reverse: drop MODES.aerodyne and "aerodyne" from OWN.
