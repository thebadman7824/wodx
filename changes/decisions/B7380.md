## B7380. The recheck gets the lane's stop check passed in (2026-11-18)

inside laneWork, so from 12:25Z on 18 November every gates job with a failing gate ended that lane with "Runner error
on lane N: stopped is not defined" and no result (#2042, #2043, lane 3 of #2044). laneWork now passes its stopped
check to recheck(). The unit tests had covered recheckStep but never ran recheck() itself.
