## B7210. gateadaptive sets aside other player characters' frenzy and hiding (2026-11-17)
Gates. The adaptive tension reads every player's character. In job 1926 the tension read frenzy before the gate's own vampire had any condition ("before":"frenzy"), and the recheck on a fresh lane failed the same way, so a player's character in the testbed world carries a frenzy condition. The gate now disables the effects that give another player's character a frenzy or hiding condition for the run, enables them again at the end, and its clean-up step checks that they are back. The tension rules themselves are unchanged.

Reverse: remove the `aside` list from tools/gate/gateadaptive.mjs.

## B7211. gateupkeep step 7 counts the region's hunters (2026-11-17)
Gates. A place's hunting difficulty is its heat plus the stage of the region's hunters, capped at 3 (hunters.mjs `rulesHuntExtra`). The gate expected the heat alone (2), and the testbed's region is already watched by hunters, so it read 3 (job 1926). Step 7 now expects the smaller of 3 and 2 plus `hunters.stageDiff` for the scene, and records the hunters' share.

Reverse: put `h.extra === 2` back in step 7 of tools/gate/gateupkeep.mjs.
