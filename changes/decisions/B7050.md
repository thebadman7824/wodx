## B7050. Every working rule's numbers are checked against its own page (2026-11-17)
Repository tools. The audits checked that rules run, not that their numbers match the book.
`tools/books/numbers-check.mjs` opens each power's cited page in the private book text, finds the power by its
heading, takes its text up to the next power, and compares the row's roll pool, difficulty, cost and effect numbers
with it. A row it cannot match is flagged, never changed: a person reads the page and gives each flagged row its
verdict. Rules a book gives once for a group (V20 Thaumaturgy: a blood point and Willpower at the power's level + 3,
V20 pdf 221) count for every row of the group, though a difference in a power's own text is still flagged. The
report lists row ids, files, lines and pdf pages, never the book's words (rule 5). Findings with full paths:
/mnt/project-files/numbers-check/findings.md.

Reverse: delete the tool and its test; nothing in the game reads it.
