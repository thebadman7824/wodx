## B6698. What the Fall ledger does in play (2026-11-16)
WoDX Chronicle, Actions, NPC. Ben's G2 note: the ledger must do something, not only say a number rose. Every social roll the victim makes to resist the corrupter is one harder a step (the ledger has always said so; nothing read it before). Crossing step 3 moves the victim's attitude to the corrupter up one through the NPC attitude queue. Crossing step 5 asks the Storyteller to bind the victim as the group's servant at full strength: a cultist (Setites, Nephandi, Apophis), a fomor with one to three powers serving the corrupter (the Wyrm), or a pact (infernal, Yama Kings, Defilers). The Defilers keep their Longing on the same ledger.

Reverse: drop the attitude and bind steps from applyCorrupt in signatures.mjs and the fallShift in wodx-actions social.js.

## B6699. Scheme clocks (2026-11-16)
WoDX NPC. G5 as built: a faction is an antagonist group by its name, or its leader's sheet, or a key the Storyteller sets. Four steps, one asked for each weekly faction turn through the approval queue. Steps 1 to 3 post a sign to everyone; step 4 gives the faction one dot in the Background its plan builds and a new goal, and Wyrm groups add a corruption source to the first place they hold. The plans are house readings of each group's aims in its books.
