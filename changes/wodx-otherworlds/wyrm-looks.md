Wyrm corruption shown on the map (B4621): whenever a place's corruption changes (the weekly turn, its Undo, the corruption window, a cleansing), its scene is drawn again to match, through WoDX Chronicle's shared looks layer when it is there and locked plain drawings otherwise. World setting "Show Wyrm corruption on the map", on by default.
