Ghouled animals, step three (B5058 to B5064): traits for every animal kind from the V20 Dark Ages animals list (pp. 396 to 399), with house figures for the kinds it leaves out. Three steps of training: trained gives the traits the list puts in brackets, war-trained (horses, camels, elephants, guard dogs and hounds) adds a dot of Brawl and is the mark the mounts rules read to stop a horse shying. Training costs 5 experience, then 10, or runs as a long task (Manipulation + Animal Ken, difficulty 7, a roll a week). Breeds by year and land, from destriers and alaunts to Dobermanns and salukis. In the wizard's Backgrounds page a vampire picks the kind, land, breed and training for each Retainers dot; dots put together make one tougher beast with the Storyteller's leave.
