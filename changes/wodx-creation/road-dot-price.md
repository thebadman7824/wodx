bump: minor
effect: A vampire's Road dot is priced with the Road's Mentor and Moment of Truth.
visible: The experience window shows a lower price for the next Road dot, and why, with a Mentor or a Moment of Truth.
---
A Road dot's price follows the Road (B7644, B7645): a Mentor with more dots on the same Road takes a point off per dot between them, to half; a Moment of Truth this story halves what is left, and buying the dot spends it (V20 Dark Ages pp. 111, 115).
