Names by year (desk nm1, B6250 to B6253): data/name-customs.mjs gives each people the stages its names went through (byname, father's name, settling, family name, or one name alone) with the year of each law or change and its source; people-rules applies the stage for the chronicle's exact year, draws a family name from the next age's list where a law fixed one early, and marks whether the name passes down. Seeds keep their people: only the name changes.
