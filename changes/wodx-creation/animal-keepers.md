Ghouled animals, step four (B5130 to B5137). Clan marks after V20 Dark Ages p. 401, only the weaknesses a beast can show. The szlachta and vozhd after V20 pp. 502 to 503; a vozhd is immune to Animalism, Dominate and Presence and its making goes to the Masquerade ledger. Kennels breed in each kind's season, once a year, from two or more ghouls of the kind; the young is born trained and its first drink fills it. Households: Gangrel, Tzimisce, Nosferatu and any vampire with Animalism keep one to three animal ghouls, more in the Dark Ages and the countryside, of the kinds the age and the clan fit; they live at the haven on the animal routines. An animal ghoul's tokens are linked to its sheet, so its blood, bond and mark are the ones on the map.
