# Future Darkness rules, settled 2026-11-10

Ben chose option A, the recommended one, on all 30 cards of the Rules Desk (draft 2026-11-10d) with no notes. These are house rules for the Future Darkness branch. Cards marked "book check" wait for the PC to confirm names and pages before they are built.


## Every line

- **g01 Machines and concealment.** Live neural-linked optics count as eyes, so powers that work on the mind (Obfuscate, Dominate, the Delirium, the Mists) work on whoever is looking. Recordings and unlinked sensors are not minds, so they show the truth, except that the fae never appear on recordings. (WoFD p. 21 (pdf 8). Matches the timeline's recordings.)
- **g02 Money and Resources.** Use the article's starting cash by Resources dot, and Resources as monthly income at a tenth of that. (WoFD p. 22 (pdf 9).)
- **g03 Who can work in the Net.** Everyone uses the article's Net rules. Only named powers act inside it: Technica, the wraiths' machine Arcanos, Fallen bound in the Net, Net Spiders and mages' Correspondence and Data effects. (WoFD p. 22 (pdf 9); the WoDX Net (Otherworlds 0.4.0).)
- **g04 Braindance.** A braindance is a recording of a whole person. Powers that reach the dead or dreams can touch it: wraiths ride it, changelings draw a little Glamour from a dreamer's recording, projectors can use a flatline rig. Each line's card sets the cost. (CP2020 p. 178 (pdf 181) for its invention.)
- **g05 Ghouls and mortals with chrome.** Mortals and ghouls pay the same Humanity cost as Kindred. (WoFD p. 20 (pdf 7).)

## Vampire

- **g06 Paths of Enlightenment and chrome.** Paths pay half the cost, rounded down. Metamorphosis pays nothing, since it seeks to remake the body. (WoFD p. 20 (pdf 7). Path names need a book check on the PC.)
- **g07 Cyberware that stops after the Embrace.** Dead implants stay as inert weight until removed by surgery or by Awakening the Flesh. They cost no Humanity while dead. (WoFD pp. 20-21 (pdf 7-8).)

## Werewolf

- **g08 Gnosis cost.** The Humanity Index rule with Gnosis: 1 permanent Gnosis per 10 points of Humanity Cost. (WoFD p. 20 (pdf 7).)
- **g09 Changing shape with chrome.** Only metal and plastic cyberware blocks the change. Bioware grows with the body and does not. (WoFD p. 20 (pdf 7).)
- **g10 The Net and the Umbra.** The Net has a reflection in the Umbra, a Weaver realm. Garou can step sideways into it near a node and fight Net Spiders there with Umbral rules. (WoFD p. 19 (pdf 6) compares Technica 5 to Net Spiders.)

## Mage

- **g11 A chromed mage and Paradox.** Each implant adds +1 difficulty to vulgar magick worked on the mage's own body. Coincidental magick is unaffected. (Timeline 2020 (m1).)
- **g12 Cyberware or Enhancement.** Street cyberware is mundane in the 2020s and never an Enhancement. Technocratic Enhancements stay a separate, Paradox-bearing thing. (Mage rules need a book check on the PC.)
- **g13 What is coincidental in 2020.** Anything a 2020 corp could plausibly sell is coincidental: cyberware, drones, the Net, AI, braindance. Orbital weapons and clones are coincidental only near corp sites. (CP2020 pp. 176-179.)

## Wraith

- **g14 Which Arcanos rides the Net.** Use whichever Arcanos the Wraith books give for entering machines and lines. Spooks stays as the name the living use for Net ghosts. Book check first. (Wraith 20th pages need a check on the PC.)
- **g15 Riding a braindance.** Riding a braindance uses Puppetry against the viewer, at the usual cost, while the recording plays. (Timeline 2010 (wr3). Arcanos rules need a check on the PC.)
- **g16 Shroud in the glassed lands.** Shroud rating there is 2 lower than the area's normal, and cyberoptics with thermographics see wraiths at Shroud 4 or less. (Timeline 1997 and 2025 (wr2).)

## Changeling

- **g17 Banality from chrome.** Every 10 points of Humanity Cost adds 1 permanent Banality. (House rule throughout.)
- **g18 Nocker optics.** The wearer sees chimera as if enchanted, and gains 1 Banality each scene they wear them. Recordings through them show nothing. (Timeline 2016 (c2).)
- **g19 Glamour from braindance.** Playing a rockerboy's braindance can yield 1 Glamour by Reverie once a day, never Rapture. (Timeline 2007 (c2).)

## Hunter

- **g20 Mortal hunters with chrome (1990-2022).** Hunters Hunted II rules with the mortal Humanity cost (card g05). (WoFD p. 18 (pdf 5).)
- **g21 Imbued and chrome (from 2023).** An imbued can carry cyberware and keep Edges. Each implant raises the difficulty of Second Sight by 1. (Hunter: The Reckoning pages need a check on the PC.)

## Demon

- **g22 Fallen bound in the Net.** Treat a Net-bound Fallen like an Earthbound whose reliquary is the Net: it acts through linked systems and draws Faith from users who believe in it. (Timeline 2005 and 2013 (d3).)
- **g23 Hosts with chrome.** Implants in the host work. Each 10 points of Humanity Cost adds 1 temporary Torment. (House rule throughout.)

## Mummy

- **g24 Ba cost.** 1 permanent Ba per 10 points of Humanity Cost. (WoFD p. 20 (pdf 7).)
- **g25 Rebirth on scanners.** Rebirth always shows on medical scanners; mummies must die and return where no scanner watches. (Timeline 2025 (mu2).)

## Kindred of the East

- **g26 Chi on thermographics.** A Kuei-jin's heat follows its balance: Yang-heavy runs hot, Yin-heavy runs cold, balanced passes for living. (Kindred of the East pages need a check on the PC.)
- **g27 Dharma cost.** Each 10 points of Humanity Cost lowers Dharma by 1 when the next Dharma roll fails. (House rule throughout.)
- **g28 Dragon nests under arcologies.** A tapped nest yields 1 less chi for each year it is tapped, to a minimum of 1. (Timeline 2018 (k3).)

## Orpheus

- **g29 Braindance projection.** A flatline rig works like Orpheus's crucible projection, with the rig's quality as a bonus to the roll and a risk of real death on a botch. (Orpheus pages need a check on the PC.)
- **g30 Projectors with chrome.** Cyberware stays with the body; the projected self has none. (House rule throughout.)
