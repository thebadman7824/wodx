# Ending scenarios: story summaries (items 8 to 13)

Summaries in our own words, read from the OCR text. Pages are printed (pdf). These scans have no spaces between words in places, and some headings are garbled, so a name or page marked "about" is a best reading. Most of these endings leave the final outcome to the characters, so "who wins" is often open.

## 8. Gehenna

- **Wormwood**, 40 (pdf 43). God ends His patience with Caine's line. A red star called Wormwood appears and its poison falls on the Kindred, killing most and stopping the rest from Embracing. Churches become the only shelter. The few who are spared face judgment and a last chance at redemption. God wins; the Kindred as a race lose.
- **Fair Is Foul**, 88 (pdf 91). The alternative myths come true: Lilith is real and has planned her revenge on Caine for millennia. She uses the Apostates and her own brood to lure Caine to a mortal city and ambush him. The characters decide whether Caine lives or dies, and Lilith's side may win.
- **Nightshade**, 124 (pdf 127). The Masquerade collapses and mortals learn that vampires exist. The characters cross a West in panic, trying to delay or stop Gehenna. In the climax, the Antediluvians gather at a basalt throne; Saulot asks to atone, an angel accepts, and sunlight burns the Ancients to ash. Afterwards the Curse of Caine keeps weakening and elders die off within weeks. Humanity survives, and vampires fade.
- **The Crucible of God**, 168 (pdf 171). The Antediluvians try to become gods through long metamorphoses; the Ravnos founder's failure caused the Week of Nightmares. As the Red Star brightens, the others wake, but the Curse fails before they finish. To claim godhood they must devour rivals, childer and mortal blood. Humanity and its supernatural allies are the last line against them.

## 9. Apocalypse

- **The Last Battleground**, 34 (pdf 38). The mildest scenario. The Final Battle is fought mostly in the Umbra and spills into the physical world without wrecking it. The Garou can win, at grave cost.
- **A Tribe Falls**, 76 (pdf 80). The Garou Nation is destroyed from within: one tribe, totem and all, turns to the Wyrm (the Storyteller picks which) and fights for it in the Final Battle. Most falls start with good intentions.
- **Weaver Ascendant**, 116 (pdf 120). The Weaver is the enemy. It takes control of Pentex and its subsidiaries and does more damage in months than the Wyrm managed in years. It threatens to cut the physical and spirit worlds apart, which would kill both. The Fera play a bigger part.
- **Ragnarok**, 144 (pdf 148). The loudest ending. A destroying claw strikes, Luna is mortally wounded, and the world falls into Fimbulwinter. Most higher life dies, and the Wyrm's hordes break loose in such numbers that only a miracle lets the Garou hold.

## 10. Ascension

- **Judgment**, 50 (pdf 53). The nearest thing to an official ending. Old metaplot schemes come due. Voormas tries to stop the Wheel of Ages and defeat death, while the Tenth Sphere of Judgment brings the Final Days. The story runs from the streets after the Reckoning to the realms beyond the Horizon.
- **The Revolution Will Be Televised**, 96 (pdf 99). The Technocracy decides how the world ends and imposes its own version of the future. The Union wins.
- **The Earth Will Shake**, 120 (pdf 123). Reality fights back: a meteor heads for Earth, in the physical world and in the Umbra at once. Surviving it takes Sleepers and mages working together.
- **A Whimper, Not a Bang**, 144 (pdf 147). The end happens out of sight. Magick slowly dies, and Enlightened Science with it, leaving a purely mundane world. The question is whether any wonder can be saved.
- **Hell on Earth**, 166 (pdf 169). The worst case: Descent, not Ascension. The Nephandi start Armageddon and even the united Traditions cannot stop it. The story is about surviving and fighting on in the hell that follows.

## 11. Time of Judgment (World of Darkness)

Chapter starts: Changeling about 16 (pdf 18), Demon 50 (pdf 52), Hunter 94 (pdf 96), Kindred of the East 144 (pdf 146), Mummy 176 (pdf 178).

**Changeling: The Dreaming** (five scenarios; the names are not clear in the scan):
- The Fomorians return. The White Court escapes, the Thallain and adhene come back, and both sides race for the Triumph Casque of Sorrows. Losing means the Endless Winter. pdf 24, with an epic war variant on pdf 33.
- Meilge's plot. Meilge steers Faerilyth to the throne, then has her killed by David, takes the crown, and gives Concordia to the Shadow Court. A quiet, secret ending. pdf 30.
- Science wins outright. Dreams die, the Dreaming fades, and changelings must reconnect with mortals or perish. pdf 39.
- The Great Purge. The Tuatha de Danaan send scouts back and enforce an old oath against mixing fae and mortal blood, which outlaws every changeling. pdf 41.
- Exodus. The fae leave the Autumn World for the Dreaming and a possible new home, giving up everything they knew. pdf 43.

**Demon: The Fallen** (three outlines):
- The Earthbound unite against the fallen. The world is shattered whoever wins; the best the fallen can do is pull down the Dread Kings' reliquaries at a cost that leaves humanity a scattered remnant. 60 (pdf 62).
- Lucifer's return. The Morningstar's plan drives the later scenes, and the fallen must decide whether to reveal themselves to the world. About 69 (pdf 71).
- The third outline was not identified clearly in the scan. The Earthbound strike everywhere at once, and Lucifer's stratagem is shown to have worked. About 81-82 (pdf 83-84).

**Hunter: The Reckoning** (one per Virtue):
- Cleansing Fires (Zeal). Civilisation collapses and monsters act openly, running death camps; the imbued rise up and purge their city. 112 (pdf 114).
- Glimmer of Hope (Mercy). A power appears that can cure monsters and make them human again; the imbued guard it or try to destroy it. 116 (pdf 118).
- Winds of Change (Vision). Ordinary people side with the imbued, and a united hunter movement can change the world. 120 (pdf 122).

**Kindred of the East** (names as read; outlines only):
- Rise of the White Devil. Thrace and the Tremere move against the Middle Kingdom. About 155 (pdf 157), with a Quincunx counterstrike option on pdf 159.
- Zero Hour, led by a secret organisation, and the rise of a Demon Empress. pdf 169-171.
- Endings for the Sixth Age range up to "None Return." pdf 177.

**Mummy: The Resurrection:**
- Apophis Ascends. The Amenti face Eurydice, a wraith filled with the Serpent's power, who plans to consume the Apepnu and bring on the Corruptor. 182 (pdf 184).
- The World Goes Westward. The Teomallki of South America are tied to how the end plays out: are they bound by fate or free to choose? Between 182 and 200.
- Dawn of the Age of Sorrow. The Wu T'ian make a last stand against Yomi and the Demon Emperor, trying to keep knowledge alive for later ages. About 195-200.
- Long Day's Journey into Night. Eurydice tears down the Shroud to merge the living world with the Underworld. 201 (pdf 203).

## 12. Ends of Empire (Wraith), Epilogues and Final Fates

The appendix starts at about 144 (pdf 145); the fates list is at 148-151 (pdf 149-152).
- A nuclear blast in the Labyrinth, set off early by the Smiling Lord's device over Enoch, creates the Sixth Great Maelstrom.
- Stygia falls. Charon returns and stands with the Deathlords at the Onyx Tower, and Charon's Transcendence leaves the Isle abandoned.
- The Guilds come back into the open. The Chanteurs break apart, the Pardoners return to tending the wounded, and Lord Ember dies fighting a Hekatonkhire.
- Persephone's crossing at the moment the storm hits blows thousands of wraiths into the Skinlands.
- Stygia's laws, the Dictum Mortuum among them, lose their hold.

After the Fall, 152 (pdf 153), suggests chronicles afterwards: rebuilding an isolated Necropolis, being stranded in the storm, restoring a Guild, salvaging the ruins of Stygia, and more.

## 13. Orpheus: End Game

- **Breach**, 16 (pdf 17). Grandmother shows her power. To keep the rebel Malfeans off her biggest anchor, she turns a Spectre hive inside out into the living world and breaches the Stormwall. The city floods with the Maelstrom for days, with epidemic illness and quarantine, and the crucible has to cope with the fallout and perhaps help seal the breach.
- **Sturm und Drang**, 146 (pdf 147). Several ways to end the chronicle, as linked episodes:
  - Relic Recovery, 147 (pdf 148): a raid into the Labyrinth for a relic.
  - The Battle for the Nameless City, 149 (pdf 150), where the Spectre war opens in earnest.
  - Grandmother's assault on a Malfean, about 151 (pdf 152).
  - God's Arrow, about 154 (pdf 155): seize the key to her downfall from her Memory Tower.
  - Grandmother Withdraws, about 172 (pdf 173): she devours the Malfeans and sinks back toward Oblivion for millennia.
  - Grandmother may be beaten or may not; the chapter also covers carrying on afterwards.
