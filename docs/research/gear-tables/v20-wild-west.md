Notes:
- * marks rifles: with a scope and at least 1 dot of Firearms, the difficulty drops to 5 (pdf 141). On the pistols and rifles, the * after a rate of 1 is printed the same way.
- Coach Gun: the figures in brackets are for firing both barrels at once.
- Range is the practical range in yards. Fire out to twice the listed range at +2 difficulty and −1 damage die (pdf 139). With a scope, a rifle can fire at Extreme Long-Distance, four times the range, at −3 damage dice.
- Rate is operations per turn. Celerity can double it; trying more than double jams the gun for 1 turn, and clearing it takes an Intelligence + Firearms roll at difficulty 5 (pdf 141).
- Reloading takes a full turn and a successful Dexterity roll. Spending a Willpower point reloads two guns in one turn (pdf 139).
- Conceal codes in this book: V vest, C coat, L long coat, N can't be hidden. The chart prints J for the two oldest pistols, which isn't in its legend.
- Other modifiers (pdf 140): point blank (under 1 yard) is difficulty 4; within 1 yard on a walk-down it's 5. Cover adds +1 to +4. Target speed adds +0 to +3. Off-hand fire with two guns is −2 dice. Quick-draw order is Wits + Firearms at difficulty 4.
- Damage type isn't marked on the chart. Bullets are lethal, by the V20 rules.
