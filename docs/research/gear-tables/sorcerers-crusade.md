Notes:
- Damage on these charts doesn't mark the type. Blades and points are lethal and blunt weapons bashing, by the general rules.
- Rate: 1/2 means one shot every two turns. Bows can fire at double rate on half the dice pool; crossbows and firearms can't.
- Range is in yards. Shooting out to double range adds +2 to difficulty. For thrown weapons the difficulty is the distance in yards divided by the thrower's Strength.
- Firearms: some designs have two to five barrels, each fired every other turn (wheellocks every turn). Reloading takes five turns per barrel. Matchlocks need a dry, lit match cord; wheellocks fire wet or dry.
- Conceal codes: P pouch, C tunic or cloak, L long cloak or robes, N can't be hidden. The chart prints T for the Mace and Morningstar, which isn't in its legend.
- The armor penalty reduces Dexterity and Perception dice pools (pdf 212). The pdf 212 text names the armor types; the ratings are on the Combat Summary chart.
- Longbow: the printed row puts N under Rate and 1/2 under Concealment. I swapped them to match the other bows. The damage reads 6 as printed.
