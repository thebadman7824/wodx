# Cyberpunk 2020 core rulebook: cyberware (names, Humanity Cost, surgery code, SP)

Source: Cyberpunk 2020, 2nd edition core rulebook (R. Talsorian Games, CP3002), the PDF edition with a text layer; the "v2.01 (new art)" printing has no text layer. Same 2nd edition rules either way. The Cyberware List is on four image-only pages, read from page images.
**Page numbering:** printed page = pdf page − 3 (pdf 85 is printed 82). The four chart pages carry no page number, so their printed numbers come from that offset: pdf 79–82 = printed 76–79.
Only the names, the Surg. code, the H.Loss (Humanity Cost) column and SP figures are given here. Prices, ID codes and descriptions are left out as asked.

Surgery codes (pdf 78 and 83 / printed 75 and 80): N = negligible (walk-in, about 1 hr, 1 point of surgical damage), M = minor (clinic, 2 hrs, 1D6+1), MA = major (full hospital, 4 hrs, 2D6+1), CR = critical (full hospital, 6 hrs, 3D6+1).
Humanity Cost bands (pdf 78 / printed 75): very low 1D6/2, low 1D6, medium 2D6, high 3D6, very high 4D6. Humanity loss is subtracted from Empathy × 10. In WoD, per the World of Future Darkness article, it comes off Humanity × 10.

Notes:
- The two extra columns (surgery, Humanity Cost) were added for this book, as asked.
- SP figures appear only on Subdermal Armor (torso SP 18), Skin Weave (body SP 12), cyberlimb Armor (SP 20) and Body Plating (Cowl, Faceplate and Torso Plate, each SP 25). Hydraulic Rams, Thickened Myomar and Reinforced Joints change the limb's SDP (structure damage points), not its SP. The chapter text (pdf 88 and 93 / printed 85 and 90) repeats the skinweave and limb armor SP.
- The WoD conversion in World of Future Darkness is 1 soak die per 4 SP (see world-of-future-darkness.md). That gives Subdermal Armor 18 SP ≈ +4 soak dice and Skin Weave 12 SP = +3 soak dice, which matches the article's list.
- Kerenzikov's H.Loss is printed "1D6/2D6", most likely one value per boost level. Check the item text (pdf 87 / printed 84) if it matters.
- Damage figures for the cyberweapons are in the chart's description column (for example Rippers 1D6+3, Wolvers 3D6, both AP as a knife). I left them out because they are CP2020 d6 values; WoFD has the WoD conversions.
