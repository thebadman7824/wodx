# World of Future Darkness (White Wolf Magazine article): cyberware, weapons, armor

Source file: `oWoD\v20 Sourcebooks\Misc\World of Future Darkness.pdf` (11 pdf pages, an article in White Wolf Magazine). **Printed magazine page = pdf page + 13** (pdf 5 = 18, pdf 7 = 20, pdf 9 = 22, pdf 11 = 24). OCR text is in `tools-env\text\wofd`; I checked the cyberware lists against page images.

The article has **no firearm or armor stat chart**. It adapts Cyberpunk 2020 cyberware to V:tM 2nd edition (TN = target number, i.e. difficulty). "Smartguns" appear only as the Smartgun Link and the cyberoptic Targeting Scope. For anything it doesn't list, it says to use the Cyberpunk 2020 entry unchanged.

## Confirmations asked for (pdf 7–11)

- **Subdermal Armor:** +4 soak dice (pdf 9 / printed 22).
- **Armor implants in general:** Subdermal Armor and Skinweave add to soak dice. The conversion is one soak die per 4 SP (pdf 8 / printed 21).
- **Skinweave:** +3 soak dice (pdf 10 / printed 23).
- **Cyberarm:** 4 damage boxes and 4 soak dice. It counts as Potence 1 for punching and Potence 2 for crushing, and is bought at a set Strength (pdf 11 / printed 24). Potence doesn't add to a cyberarm's strength, and Fortitude doesn't protect a cyber limb (pdf 9 / printed 22).
- **Reflex boosters:** these act as Celerity of their level. Kerenzikov 2 is Celerity 2 with no blood cost, and Dexterity gains the boost level −1 (pdf 7–8 / printed 20–21). List entries: Kerenzikov +1 Celerity per level and +1 Dexterity at level 2; Speedware +3 Celerity and +2 Dexterity for 5 rounds; Boostmaster +1 Celerity and +1 Dexterity on any boosterware (pdf 9 / printed 22).
- **BOD boosts in general:** add half their Cyberpunk level to Strength or Stamina (pdf 7 / printed 20).

Other numbers from the article:
- Humanity cost (pdf 7 / printed 20): it works as in Cyberpunk 2020, against Humanity × 10. Garou use Gnosis instead, mummies Ba. To roll d10s instead of d6s, multiply the d6 count by 3 and divide by 5, keeping the remainder as a flat add. Fixed-cost items stay out of that total.
- Awakening the Flesh (Thaumaturgy ritual level 4, pdf 7 / printed 20): a two-hour ritual that lets cyberware stay in a Kindred body. It costs 3 blood points and a piece of amber. Afterwards the patient makes a Wits + Medicine roll; a failure heals the cyberware out, and each botch is one aggravated wound.
- Starting Resources as cash (pdf 9 / printed 22): 1 dot 2,000eb, 2 dots 5,000eb, 3 dots 10,000eb, 4 dots 20,000eb, 5 dots 50,000eb.
- Task conversion (pdf 11 / printed 24): Cyberpunk Easy, Average, Difficult and Very Difficult map to difficulty 3, 5, 7 and 9.
- Organ replacements (T-Maxx liver, Sport Heart and the like) do nothing for Kindred. Anything that works through the nervous system keeps working (pdf 8 / printed 21).
