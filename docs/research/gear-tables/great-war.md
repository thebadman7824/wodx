# Wraith: The Great War: weapons and armor

Source file: `oWoD\Wraith (c)\Wraith - The Great War.pdf`. The book has no text layer; I read it from the OCR text in `tools-env\text\greatwar` and checked the charts against page images. **Pdf page = printed page** in this book (the page footers match).

**There is no personal weapons chart (pistol, rifle, bayonet and so on) in this book.** The skirmish rules (pdf/printed 69) use "the weapon's damage rating" and point to Wraith: The Oblivion for weapon stats. What the book does have is heavy-weapon systems and armor-penetration tables, listed below. Rifle, pistol and melee stats will have to come from Wraith 20th, or W:tO 2nd edition, which isn't in this job's list.

Notes:
- Skirmish fire (69): Perception + Firearms (or Archery) at difficulty 5 plus the Maelstrom's level, adjusted for range and aim. Damage is the weapon's damage rating plus successes. There's no Dodge in skirmish combat.
- Skirmish melee (69): a resisted Dexterity + Melee/Brawl/Martial Arts roll at difficulty 6. The winner does the weapon's damage rating + Strength + net successes. Shields add to the opponent's difficulty.
- Armor (70): any armor, relic plate or flak jacket, gives its normal protection against everything that doesn't ignore armor.
- Close-formation fire (68): roll 1 die (scattered fire), 3 dice (rifle, musket or arrow barrage) or 5 dice (artillery or machine gun) at difficulty 6. Each success deals one die of damage, each soaked on its own.
- Tank penetration (73): roll the weapon's penetration dice against the armor's difficulty. 1 success harms crew or kit, 3 disable the vehicle, 5 destroy it. A still target is −2 difficulty; firing from a moving tank is +2.
- Naval shells (77): dice = the shell's damage rating + crew successes, against the ship's armor as difficulty. Living chain shot is 8 + 1 die aggravated per chain, Dexterity + Dodge at difficulty 8 to avoid.
- The text on pdf 73 points to "the Weapons Chart, p.77", but the penetration chart is on page 74.
