# Dark Ages: Mage Grimoire: rote mechanics by fellowship

Source: Dark Ages: Mage Grimoire (PDF on the PC trove). Read from page renders, since the OCR text was unusable.
Every page is given as "pdf N / printed N-2", so pdf 23 is printed page 21. "p" in a line means the printed page.
Mechanics only, in our own words. No book text is copied. Where a line says CHECK or flag, the book itself is unclear.

## Batini (Ahl-i-Batin)
- Foci note pdf 22/p20: Anbiya trance (exercise, prayer, fasting, dance); Fatihah written word, calligraphy, gestures, gems/lenses; Hajj drawings, plans, maps, sandals, saddle; Layl darkness, veils, smoke, hooded cloaks, absence of light/sound. All Pillars: mirrors/still water scrying; gems/crystals store a spell; weapons/pens direct; clothes/jewelry for spells on a person.
- Dhikr (Fatihah 1) pdf 22/p20. Passive: per success one full answer about what is being recalled, or one forgotten relevant detail. Active (mantra or amulet): per success fix ~15 min of action/talk, ~50 pages, one panoramic view, one long speech or piece of music, or all about one person; 5 successes = whole scene. Failure: nothing. Botch: memory blocked until a full night's sleep (or ST). ST rules if other Pillars can act on recalled memories.
- Closet Dance (Hajj 3, Layl 3) pdf 23/p21. Per success one jump to the nearest similar adjacent space; extra successes add one step of range each; 5 ≈ run of a large building, market or forest. Spaces must match in size, shape, build and purpose and be in one structure unless the far one is memorized. Failure: stay. Botch: land in the middle of the nearest large open area. Layl part: slip unnoticed through open repeating spaces (trees, columns), untraceable. Occupied target (oil, grain, a person): Hajj 4 to swap that mass back.
- Tears of Allah (Hajj 4, Anbiya 1) pdf 23-24/p21-22. Needs a prepared conduit to a raw elemental place (journey there or to a vantage point; one place per element; fixed in memory, Dhikr may help; someone else's coordinates rarely work and may need a horoscope or geomancy; Anbiya offsets changes over time). Per success 1 bashing + 1 lethal (heat, cold, pressure, debris). Matter stays after. Caster still exposed to heat/cold brought. Failure: error or a change at the source. Botch: hits caster or allies, sucks things in, or will not close (uncontrolled spew for ST-set time).
- Solomonic rules pdf 24/p22: advanced murshids only; a week of purification before and after; each spell takes most of a day, all three a day and a night. Works on any spirit, tuned to djinn.
- Summoning (Fatihah 2, Hajj 4) pdf 24-25/p22-23. Extended, target djinn Gnosis + 1 successes, base diff 10: −1 per major component (spoken name, written name, sigil, true image), −1 per two lesser (colour, shape, tone, scent, formula), −2 knowing the High Umbrood lord over it. Djinn appears physically in natural form, stays 1 hour per extra success. Can't resist in the material world; one in the Umbra may ignore it.
- Binding (Fatihah 4, Hajj 2) pdf 25/p23. Extended, Rage + 2 successes, base diff 10 cut as Summoning. One success within 3 turns of the Summoning holds the djinn in the object until the spell ends. Extra successes raise the diff to free it (magic or breaking the container). Names and sigils inscribed on the circle or container. Lasts until released; then no other mage can Summon it (auto-fail).
- Commanding (Anbiya 5, Fatihah 3, Layl 5) pdf 25-26/p23-24. Willpower + 3 successes, base diff 10 cut as above. One task, +1 task per extra success. Lasts until done; each extra success holds it one more step (hour, day, week, month, year, decade, century). Unprolonged ends at caster's death. A Commanded djinn can't be Bound by another; answers another Summoning only if no conflict. ST plays loopholes.

## Messianic Voices ("Miracles of Faith")
- I Am Come for Your Souls (Gavri-El 3, Uri-El 3) pdf 44-45/p42-43. One target in normal range per success, plus one per Quintessence point spent. Each target rolls Willpower, diff 6 +1 per caster success (max 9), and must match or beat the caster's successes or flee in terror for at least one minute per caster success (may drop held items).
- Swords into Plowshares (Gavri-El 2, Repha-El 3) pdf 45/p43. Everyone in sight (out to the normal range from successes) who means violence makes a contested Willpower roll against the mage, each side's diff = the other's permanent Willpower. Mage wins by 1-2: some aggression, won't act without new provocation. By 3+: rage gone, friendly. Subject wins: free to act. Each success past the first gives the mage +1 die on the contest. Large mobs: roll an average Willpower; a narrow win leaves a few stragglers, a wide win ends the bloodlust.

## Order of Hermes ("Spells of the Order")
- Degrees sidebar pdf 58/p56: Neophyte, Zelator, Practicus (Modus Foundation 1, chooses a Word), Initiate (1 in a Pillar), Initiate Exemptus (2 Pillar + 2 Modus), Adept (3 Pillar + 3 Modus + 1 other), Adept Major (4 + 4 Modus, 3 in another, 1 in another), Magister Scholae (5 + 5 Modus, 3 in two others), Magister Mundi (5 in every Pillar and Modus). Fuller than DA:M p.37.
- Revoking the Gift of Adam (Corona 3) pdf 59/p57. On success the subject can't voice thoughts coherently for a time set by successes; each attempt to say one thought needs a Willpower roll, diff 6 +1 per caster success (max 10), or it comes out as garble. Telepathy still works. Corona 5 version also blocks telepathy; cast as an Ongoing spell it is permanent (until undone by magic): no use, understanding or learning of language at all.
- Utterance of the 72 Names (Anima 5, Corona 5, Primus 4, Vires 3) pdf 59-60/p57-58. Long purification first; body of untouched soil and pure water (or alchemical clay), 8 ft+. Best as an Ongoing spell (much Quintessence and Willpower). Free-willed mute golem: Str 10, Dex 2, Sta 8, Cha 1, Man 1, App 0, Per 2, Int 2, Wits 2; 10 health levels (−0 ×4, −1 ×2, −2 ×2, −5, Incap, Destroyed); 4 armor dice soak any damage; Willpower 3. Eats 1 Quintessence a day or sleeps; creator can switch it off and wake it with 1 Quintessence (a paper with a Name in its mouth). Heal with Anima 4, Primus 4 (1 level per success). Refuses cruel or immoral orders. Botch: Scouring Backlash (DA:M p.139) or a destructive spirit animates it. Mispronouncing the Name can kill the speaker (flavour, no roll given).

## Old Faith ("The Fruit of the Tree"): rotes vary by place and witch (pdf 71/p69)
- Bewitching Glamour (Spring 3, Summer 2) pdf 71/p69. Herbal infusion (rose, jasmine). Until next sunrise: +1 Appearance per success, and +1 die per success on Subterfuge to seduce or sway. Partly illusion (viewer sees their ideal).
- Bitter Fruit (Winter 3) pdf 71/p69. Poisons a ripe fruit; it rots within a day, so can't be prepared far ahead. Taster rolls Stamina diff 7 each round: fail = 1 unsoakable lethal level. Repeats for rounds = caster successes, or until cured by magic; mundane care can't save them.
- Circe's Enchantment (Spring 4, Summer 2) pdf 71-72/p69-70. 1 success + 1 Quintessence turns the subject into the animal its nature fits at that moment (subject's nature picks; caster can set up the circumstances). Becomes a normal animal, no human memory, only instinct; gear falls (may be damaged). Several subjects: needs Summer 3 and +1 success and +1 Quintessence each. Lasts the spell's duration unless cast Ongoing (hard on unwilling).
- Dowsing (Spring 1) pdf 72/p70. Forked yew or ash rod. 1 success finds the best place to dig a well if any water is there. Autumn 3 digs the well; Winter 3 raises a lasting spring.
- Eternal Slumber (Winter 3) pdf 72/p70. Potion (valerian, mushrooms, poppy) in food, drink, a blade or needle prick, or candle wax (first to breathe the fumes). Extended: 20 successes, 35 Quintessence, 4 temporary Willpower. Victim rolls Willpower diff 7; needs 2 successes to resist, else a deathlike sleep. Medicine diff 8 to tell they are alive. No ageing or want while asleep. Caster sets one waking condition; lasts until met or undone by magic.
- Eye of the Hunter (Summer 1) pdf 72/p70. +1 die per success on hunting Survival rolls; senses any beast (birds, fish; not people or unnatural beings) within the hurried-spell distance. Shapeshifters in beast form read as beasts; Perception + Awareness diff 6 to notice they're more.
- Faerie Gateway (Spring 4) pdf 72-73/p70-71. At an in-between place (mound, ring, glade, shore, cave mouth, hilltop). Dusk −1 diff, night normal, day +1. 1 success opens it for about six people; each extra success doubles that. No safe welcome promised; leaving needs another casting; time may run oddly.
- The Giant's Dance (Autumn 3, Spring 5) pdf 73/p71. Open ground or many loose stones (not worked stone like walls). Stone giant: Str 8, Dex 1, Sta 8, Wits 1, Brawl 1; health −0 ×8, −1, −2, −5; soaks any damage that doesn't strike its pattern (much aggravated too). Every 3 successes: +1 to an Attribute or one more −0 level. Follows simple orders. Crumbles at Incapacitated. Compare Ancient Warder of the Land (DA:M p.119).
- Healer's Sight (Spring 1 or Winter 1) pdf 73-74/p71-72. Spring senses well-being and works out the condition; Winter senses the malady, where and how bad. 1 success: general nature and the subject's state (health level etc.); more successes, more detail. +1 die per success on Herbalism, Hearth Wisdom or Medicine to treat that subject.
- The Hollow Hill (Spring 5) pdf 74/p72. Ongoing extended: 10 successes doubles inside size; each further 10 adds ×1 (×3, ×4...). One roll a day, tracing the lines with a potion. To seal: 5 Quintessence + 1 per success, and 1 temporary Willpower per 10 successes. ST option: part of a Chantry Background (DA:M pp.83-84); each Background point spent shrinks the outside one step on the table while the inside stays.
- Magic Mirror (Autumn 2) pdf 74/p72. Scrying via pool, cauldron or mirror. Range by normal casting distance: 3 successes miles, 4 a countryside, 5 a distant land. Others may sense it. Spells through the mirror need normal (not hurried) casting with enough successes for the distance plus the effect, so more Backlash risk.
- Merlin's Ride (Spring 4, Summer 2) pdf 74/p72. Distance travelled is multiplied by successes, for one day's travel; renew each day. Long talk with outsiders or more than a brief stop breaks it.
- Mother's Bounty (Autumn 2) pdf 74-75/p72-73. Simple, 1 success, but needs the right sacrifice (small offering normally; in famine greater, even a human; a dying land needs a sacred king). Wrong sacrifice: fails, no Backlash if 1 success. Success: crops and stock in the area healthy. Each extra success +~10% yield, or widens area one step (town, county, shire or barony, nation).
- Oracular Head (Winter 3) pdf 75/p73. Ongoing extended, 10 successes, one roll a day; then 13 Quintessence and 2 Willpower to fix it. Head answers all questions as best it knows (mostly what it knew in life, some insight from death) and must obey. Valdaermen have the same art (Mimir's Head p.99 printed).
- Storm Brewing (Summer 4) pdf 75/p73. Extended, roll once per hour of simmering. 20 successes: storm over a small village; 30: a country; 40: a region 100 miles each way. Caster can't leave more than about an hour (a helper must keep the fire). Emptying the cauldron or dousing the fire breaks it. Storm lasts while maintained; minor damage to buildings and ships.
- Stray Sod (Autumn 3) pdf 75-76/p73-74. Simple, 1 success needed. Each success +1 diff on victims' Survival to avoid getting lost; immediate Survival roll. Above 9 stays 9 but +1 success needed per point over. Fail: lost. Botch: hopelessly lost, maybe into a hazard.
- Witch's Wind (Spring 3) pdf 76/p74. Successes = wind Strength, any direction, up to 100 yards away. Use Feats of Strength table (DA:V p.228). Strength + Athletics to move against it.
- Withering Curse (Winter 3) pdf 76/p74. Needs a piece of the victim burned with bitter herbs. Each success = 1 lethal level, soaked with Willpower, dealt 1 per day until done or dead. Caster can lift it; killing the caster ends it. If victim absent, extra successes for distance (DA:M p.104). Winter 5: aggravated.
- Elixirs pdf 76/p74: Flying Ointment (Autumn 3, 1 dose per success): trance, spirit roams one night with all powers, invisible and ghostly to the material world, can deal with the Umbra. Four Thieves Vinegar (Summer 2, 1 dose per success): drunk and rubbed on, immune to disease for some hours.
- Love Philter (elixir; Spring 2 + Summer 2, 10 successes total to brew) pdf 77/p75. Drinker rolls Willpower, diff = caster's lower of Spring or Summer + 4; fail = desire for the next suitable person seen. Fleeting unless reinforced.

## Spirit-Talkers (rotes start pdf 85/p83; they roll Sensitivity + a Pillar)
- Animal Speech (Wise One 2, or Wise One 2 + Chieftain 2 to give orders) pdf 85-86/p83-84. Header says Chieftain 2, system text says Chieftain 1 (flag). Each success = talk with one animal for the scene (2 successes: two animals). Successes also set how complex orders can be: 1 simple, 5 very complex. Dream version (sleep near the animal): Wise One 1 only.
- Banish the Spirits of War (Chieftain 3, Warrior 2, Wise One 3) pdf 86/p84. Clears line of sight of war-aligned spirits for one scene. Everyone there rolls Willpower diff 7 to stay aggressive, +1 diff per success on Sensitivity + Chieftain; over 9 needs extra successes. Defending needs no roll; striking back needs one. Changing Breeds there can't regain Rage this scene unless they leave.
- Bind Spirit (Chieftain 3, Wise One 3) pdf 86-87/p84-85. Makes a fetish: needs a top-quality vessel suited to the spirit (doesn't find or subdue the spirit). Extended, one roll per in-game hour of fasting and chanting, 1 success per point of the spirit's Essence. After 24 hours, a Stamina roll before each roll, diff 5, +1 every 6 hours.
- Curse Shifting (Wise One 4) pdf 86/p84. Sensitivity + Wise One. Moves a curse to another person, animal, plant or place (kill the goat, curse gone). Small curses 1 success; most curses removed with ~5; an ancient strong curse 20+. ST sets the number.
- Dead Words (Wise One 2, 3 or 4) pdf 86-87/p84-85. Sensitivity + Wise One; each success = one turn of talk with a ghost in sight, at its grave or a place important to it. Wise One 3 calls the shade from anywhere; Wise One 4 makes it manifest. Only the mage perceives it.
  Long talk: cast as an extended ritual. Dream version (sleep on the grave): Wise One 1 only. (pdf 87/p85)
- Deny the Charm of Death (Chieftain 2) pdf 87/p85. Immune to a number of Dominate or Presence uses (by dead or undead) equal to successes on Sensitivity + Chieftain.
- Exorcism (Wise One 3 or Chieftain 4) pdf 87-88/p85-86. Wise One: contested Willpower with the spirit, +1 die to the mage's Willpower per success on Sensitivity + Wise One. Chieftain: the spirit gets no roll, but the mage needs 1 success per 10 Essence (1 drives out Essence 10 or less, 2 up to 20, and so on). Works on a person, place or thing.
- RULE (sidebar pdf 88/p86): DA:M's spirit Power = DA:W's Essence; halve the Power of DA:M chapter 6 sample spirits.
- Horse Bringer (Chieftain 2, or Chieftain 3 + Wise One 3) pdf 88/p86. One wild horse per success on Sensitivity + Chieftain, arriving within a day, fully obedient; other animals too (deer for food). Higher version calls rare magical beasts (gryphons), best at a shallowing; Bygones obey only one day per success.
- Iron Glare (Chieftain 1, Warrior 1) pdf 88/p86. Sensitivity + Chieftain (or Warrior). Add successes to Intimidation successes for the scene. Target of the mage's anger: 1 success most foes (Willpower 6 or less) back down; 2 even strong-willed back down; 3 apologetic; 4 flees; 5+ collapses. Eerie aura rating adds.
- Irresistible Summons (Chieftain 4) pdf 88/p86. Calls one person the mage has met. Sensitivity + Chieftain; each success = one full day the target does their best to come (may bring company). Ends within 10 feet after greeting. Not there in time: wears off, target forgets why. Extended casting for far targets. No resistance within a mile; beyond, contested Willpower diff 6.
- Lords' Bane (Trickster 2) pdf 88-89/p86-87. Sensitivity + Trickster; each success cancels one success on the target's Charisma rolls; if the mage has more successes, treat as a botch (the speaker looks a fool).
- Otherskin (Trickster 3) pdf 89/p87. Mage changes sex until next dawn; needs 2 successes; extras add one-for-one to Charisma, Appearance, Leadership or Subterfuge for the duration.
- Sacred Antics (Trickster 3) pdf 89/p87. Two turns per success on Sensitivity + Trickster. Athletics diff for outrageous stunts drops to 5 (4 if playful or funny). Can't botch (ones count as twos). Not for combat.
- Self Possession (Chieftain 1) pdf 89/p87. Sensitivity + Chieftain; for the scene, subtract successes from every attempt to possess or mentally control the mage.
- Sheltering Winds (Chieftain 2, Warrior 2) pdf 89/p87. Each success removes one success from archers' attacks on the mage for the scene. Missiles only.
- Slip the Shadow (Trickster 3, or Trickster 3 + Wise One 3) pdf 89/p87. Sensitivity + Trickster; shadow roams free one scene per success, fully controlled; with Wise One 3 it reports what it sees and hears. Mage casts no shadow meanwhile. Botch: shadow rebels; mage loses half permanent Willpower (round down), back 1 per day, then a new shadow grows; the old one stays loose.
- Slow Dream Curse (Trickster 3, Warrior 2) pdf 89-90/p87-88. Sensitivity + Trickster; each success slows one target for the scene: initiative 1, −1 diff to hit them, quarter movement. Celerity users lose one more action per turn per success (continued next page).
  Celerity example: 3 successes vs Celerity 4 leaves 1 extra action; 5 successes, treated as anyone else (initiative 1). (pdf 90/p88)
- Spirit Army (Chieftain 2, Warrior 5, Wise One 3) pdf 90/p88. Sensitivity + Warrior; two soldiers per success from enough like vessels (trees, corpses, armor, stones, sand; prepared statues or constructs). Mental and Social 1. Skeletons Str 2 Dex 3 Sta 2 Brawl 2 Melee 4; Stone elementals 5/2/6, Brawl 4 Melee 1; Oak 4/2/5, Brawl 3 Melee 2; Willow 3/5/3, Brawl 3 Melee 3; Animated armor 3/3/6, Brawl 2 Melee 4. Slavishly loyal; leave when duration ends or the spell drops. Best as an extended spell.
- Spirit-Guided Arrow (Warrior 2) pdf 90/p88. Each success on Sensitivity + Warrior adds one success to the next Archery roll; extras roll into damage.
- Welcoming Waters (Chieftain 3, Warrior 3, Wise One 3) pdf 90/p88. Water bigger than a pond. Sensitivity + Wise One: one arm per success, each arm Str and Dex = successes. Mundane and even magical bladed weapons don't harm the arms. A small boat needs 5+ arms. Orders can be precise.
- Wield the Dreamblade (Warrior 4) pdf 90-91/p88-89. Three turns of waking dream, ready on the fourth. Invisible melee weapon of any familiar kind; damage = Strength + successes on Sensitivity + Warrior, aggravated; dodging it +1 diff. Lasts one turn per permanent Willpower dot; recall later in the scene in one turn.
- Willow Body (Trickster 3) pdf 91/p89. One turn per success on Sensitivity + Trickster. Bend long bones 120°, stretch limbs 6 inches per success, squeeze through a 3-inch square hole. If it lapses mid-squeeze: up to 4 dice aggravated (ST sets by how much was stuck).

## Valdaermen (rotes pdf 98/p96 on; roll Blót + a Pillar)
- Baldur's Blessing (Forlog 4, Hjaldar 4) pdf 98/p96. Names of all things that swore not to harm Baldur written on the target's bare skin (nearly a day's work). ## Wonders
The Grimoire gives these items no dot ratings and no Quintessence pools. Rating, Pillars and Quintessence are listed only where the text sets them.
- Fae treasures, pdf 142/p140 (list starts on the page before; check):
  - Red and White Apples of Avalon: one apple per visitor ever. Red: pain and hallucinations (not lethal in itself). White: heals the mind (night terrors, madness, lost speech), not the body.
  - Chalice of Rain: fill and pour as an offering to the Fae; rain before the next moonrise.
  - Brooch of Scrying: ask one yes/no question, smash it; the stone stays white (yes) or turns black (no). One use.
- Magical Treasures, pdf 142-143/p140-141:
  - Brazier of Summoning: Solomonic mages (Batini, Messianic, Hermetic) who read Sanskrit. Extended Foundation + a spirit Pillar (Al-Hajj, Uri-El or Primus), 10 successes; the item supplies the power, so only 1 dot in that Pillar needed. Calls a random spirit of a chosen class (demon, elemental or djinn), not a named one; any temper; no protection given.
  - Dowsing Rod: yew; spend 1 temporary Willpower and concentrate; leads to anything sought within 200 yards; wanders if focus slips.

## Rule changes and additions
- Moon Quintessence pdf 113/p111: an offering to the Moon in a night casting gives 1-3 Quintessence (prayer in own blood 1, a calf 3, a person more, ST call); use at once, unused fades 1 per 5 minutes. Only for magic fitting the phase: new = beginnings and blessings; waxing = health and growth; full = all magic gets +1 point; waning = banishing.
- Sunday pdf 113/p111: Messianic Voices −1 diff on instantaneous spells on Sundays (not extended or ongoing; ST may penalise those). ST option: devout Christian mages praying daily for a full week get +1 Aura modifier until a day is missed. Skipping holy days can cost a Pillar or all magic until atonement (fasting, pilgrimage, vow of silence).

- Certámen pdf 122-124/p120-122:
  1. Declaration: the challenger states the stake plainly; the other accepts or yields.
  2. Contest of wills: one Manipulation + Intimidation (or Subterfuge) roll each, diff = the other's permanent Willpower. More successes: +1 die next stage. Botch: −1 die (both botch = both just fail). Either may yield.
  3. Locus: Foundation + any Pillar, diff 6; each success puts 1 Quintessence (from what is at hand, ignoring the channel limit) in the locus. One fails or has no Quintessence: loses. Both fail: reroll at 7, then 8; a third fail calls it off and the aggressor yields.
  4. Aegis and gladius: pick a Pillar for each. Balanced: attack and deflect diff 7. Offensive: attack 6, deflect 8. Defensive: attack 8, deflect 6 (chosen each turn). Reusing the locus Pillar: +1 to all rolls per extra use (all three the same: +2).
  5. Duel: normal initiative; winner may strike first or make the foe go first. One attack and one deflection each turn. Attack: gladius Pillar alone (no Foundation) vs the chosen diff; defender rolls aegis Pillar. If attack successes beat deflection: damage dice = attacker's Foundation + 1 per extra success; defender soaks with Foundation dice; each unsoaked success knocks 1 Quintessence out of the locus (unusable until the duel ends). Empty locus loses.
  6. Resolution: winner takes the stake, gets back all their locus Quintessence plus half (round down) of the loser's; the loser gets the rest. Etiquette: no rematch for a year and a day.
