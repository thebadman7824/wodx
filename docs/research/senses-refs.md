# Page refs for WoDX Senses (2026-11-09)

Mechanics and page numbers only. Every page is marked pdf or printed.

## V20 core (printed = pdf − 8)
- **Heightened Senses (Auspex 1).** pdf 142–143 / printed 134–135.
  - Sight, hearing and smell double in clarity and range. Taste and touch get sharper but reach no farther.
  - It takes a reflexive action to turn on. There is no roll and no cost.
  - While it is on, sense rolls (such as Perception + Alertness) can drop in difficulty by the character's Auspex rating.
  - A sudden bright light or loud noise can blind or deafen the character for an hour or more.
  - The Storyteller may secretly roll plain Auspex to see whether a threat is sensed. The book's examples are difficulty 5 for a gun pointed at the character and 9 for a hidden plot.
- **Difficulty chart and degrees of success.** pdf 257 / printed 249.
  - Difficulties run from 3 (Trivial) up to 9 (nearly impossible), with 6 as Standard.
  - Successes: 1 is Marginal, 2 Moderate, 3 Complete, 4 Exceptional, 5 or more Phenomenal.
  - A 10 always counts as a success, whatever the difficulty.
- **Lowest difficulty.** I found no explicit floor in pdf 255–262. The chart starts at 3 (Trivial). A difficulty of 2 does appear in play, for example attacks on a vampire committing diablerie (pdf 302 / printed 294). So treat 2 as the practical minimum. A floor of 3 for ordinary tasks would be a house rule.

## Wraith: The Oblivion 20th (printed = pdf − 65)
Lifesight and Deathsight are Wraith 20 abilities, not W20. The W20 core has neither term.
- **Lifesight.** pdf 191 / printed 126.
  - Reads a living being's aura (mood and health) with Perception + Empathy at difficulty 5.
  - 1 success gives a general feeling; 4 is close to reading their mind.
- **Deathsight.** pdf 191 / printed 126.
  - Sees the Oblivion in things: weak points and how much health a living being has.
  - Discern Weakness or Sense Health is Perception + Awareness at difficulty 5, scaled by successes.

## Hunter: The Reckoning (printed = pdf − 1)
- **Second sight.** pdf 133–134 / printed 132–133.
  - Monsters that anyone could see need no Conviction.
  - One Conviction point lets the hunter spot monsters hidden nearby within natural sight, including those disguised or hidden by powers.
  - Second sight marks every supernatural being as not human or not alive. It doesn't reveal what kind of being it is.
  - Spirits and ghosts in the physical world look solid to the hunter. The newly dead show signs such as pallor, a grave smell or a cold breeze.
  - A subtle power used on the hunter can be noticed with a Conviction reaction roll, resisted against the creature's Stealth or its power roll. The Storyteller may make that roll in secret.
  - Whether hunters may trigger second sight in reaction, rather than keeping it on, is the Storyteller's call, and the players should know in advance.
