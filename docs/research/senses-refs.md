## V20 core (printed = pdf − 8)
- **Auspex, the opening.** pdf 142 / printed 134.
  - A sudden or dynamic event can distract an Auspex user. Willpower roll at difficulty 4 or more, higher for a stronger source.
  - A failure leaves the character oblivious to the surroundings for a turn or two.
- **Heightened Senses (Auspex 1).** pdf 142–143 / printed 134–135.
  - Sight, hearing and smell double in clarity and range. Taste and touch get sharper but reach no farther.
  - It takes a reflexive action to turn on. There is no roll and no cost. It lasts as long as the vampire wants.
  - While it is on, sense rolls (such as Perception + Alertness) can drop in difficulty by the character's Auspex rating.
  - An especially sudden or strong stimulus (a spotlight's glare, a thunderclap) can blind or deafen the character for an hour or more.
  - The Storyteller may secretly roll plain Auspex to see whether a threat is sensed. The book's examples are difficulty 5 for a gun pointed at the character and 9 for a hidden plot.
  - One sense alone may be heightened: that sense's difficulty drops by one more, and the difficulty to avoid distraction rises by one.
  - It does not see in pitch darkness, but it lowers the penalty for acting there from +2 to +1, and the character can make ranged attacks in pitch darkness at a foe heard or smelled.
- **Difficulty chart and degrees of success.** pdf 257 / printed 249.
  - V20 sets every difficulty from 2 to 10, most often 3 to 9. So V20's lowest difficulty is 2.
  - The chart names each level: Trivial is 3, Easy 4, Straightforward 5, Standard 6, Challenging 7, Difficult 8, Extremely difficult 9. A roll with no stated difficulty is 6.
  - Successes: 1 is Marginal, 2 Moderate, 3 Complete, 4 Exceptional, 5 or more Phenomenal.
  - A 10 always counts as a success, whatever the difficulty.
- **Correction.** The first version of this page said V20 states no floor. It does (pdf 257), so WoDX Senses' floor of 3 was a house rule against the book and is now 2 (B6570).

## Werewolf: The Apocalypse 20th (printed = pdf − 2)
- **Heightened Senses (Gift, Level One).** pdf 160–161 / printed 158–159.
  - A Gnosis point turns it on for a scene.
  - Homid and Glabro: Perception difficulties drop by two, and Perception + Primal-Urge allows uncanny feats such as tracking by scent.
  - Crinos, Hispo and Lupus: Perception difficulties drop by three (not added to the ordinary Lupus Perception bonus), and Primal-Urge pools gain a die.
  - Sudden loud noises, bright lights or strong scents can disorient.

## Wraith: The Oblivion 20th (printed = pdf − 65)
Lifesight and Deathsight are Wraith 20 abilities, not W20. The W20 core has neither term.
- **Lifesight.** pdf 191 / printed 126.
  - Reads a living being's aura (mood and health) with Perception + Empathy at difficulty 5.
  - 1 success gives a general feeling; 4 is close to reading their mind.
- **Deathsight.** pdf 191 / printed 126.
  - Sees the Oblivion in things: weak points and how much health a living being has.
  - Discern Weakness or Sense Health is Perception + Awareness at difficulty 5, scaled by successes.
  - A weak spot found this way, on a building or a person, gives -1 difficulty to hit it.
- **Heightened Senses.** pdf 191 / printed 126. At the Storyteller's call, Perception + Alertness at difficulty 5 picks out a detail. Too much stimulus causes discomfort but no Corpus loss.
- **Spectres.** pdf 413 / printed 348. Deathsight rolls at -1 (lowest 2); Perception rolls +1 except Deathsight and Lifesight; Lifesight on the living +2; no Sharpened Senses.

## Hunter: The Reckoning (printed = pdf − 1)
- **Second sight.** pdf 133–135 / printed 132–134.
  - One Conviction point gives the benefits for the scene, a reflexive action. Without it the hunter counts as a normal person.
  - Monsters that anyone could see need no Conviction.
  - One Conviction point lets the hunter spot monsters hidden nearby within natural sight, including those disguised or hidden by powers.
  - Second sight marks every supernatural being as not human or not alive. It doesn't reveal what kind of being it is.
  - Spirits and ghosts in the physical world look solid to the hunter. The newly dead show signs such as pallor, a grave smell or a cold breeze.
  - A possessing spirit shows over its host (pdf 134 / printed 133).
  - The hunter sees through illusions: a false sight, sound, smell, taste or touch is plainly unreal (pdf 134 / printed 133).
  - Another hunter looks wholly human unless using an edge (pdf 135 / printed 134).
- **Reacting with Conviction** (sidebar, pdf 134 / printed 133).
  - Intelligence, Perception or Wits + primary Virtue at difficulty 6 (or Alertness, Awareness or Intuition in place of the Virtue) to spend Conviction in time.
  - A subtle power used on the hunter can be noticed with a reaction roll, resisted against the creature's Stealth or its power roll. The Storyteller may make that roll in secret.
  - Whether hunters may trigger second sight in reaction, rather than keeping it on, is the Storyteller's call, and the players should know in advance.
