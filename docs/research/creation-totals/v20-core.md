## (c) Linguistics and languages per dot
V20 has **no Linguistics Ability**. Languages come from Merits instead:
- **Language** Merit: 1 point, one language per purchase. pdf 492 / printed 484.
- **Natural Linguist** Merit: 2 points. +3 dice on language rolls, and each Language Merit gives two languages. pdf 493 / printed 485.
- Linguistics also appears as an Academics specialty. pdf 116 / printed 108.

So "languages per dot" does not exist in V20. Language counts come from Language Merit purchases (doubled with Natural Linguist).
