# Hunter: The Reckoning: creation totals

Book: Hunter - The Reckoning (1999).pdf. This comes from WoDX's OCR (tools-env\text\hunter). Page offset: printed = pdf − 1. Every ref below gives both.

The summary is on pdf 85–86 / printed 84–85. The full steps are on pdf 89–91 / printed 88–90.

## Attributes and Abilities
- Every Attribute starts at 1 dot. Physical, Social and Mental get 6 / 4 / 3 dots, as for mortals.
- Talents, Skills and Knowledges get 11 / 7 / 4 dots, with no Ability above 3.

## Backgrounds
- 5 dots.
- The list is Allies, Arsenal, Bystanders, Contacts, Destiny, Exposure, Fame, Influence, Mentor, Patron and Resources. (pdf 86 / printed 85)

## Virtues and edges
- There are three Virtues: Zeal, Vision and Mercy, each rated 1 to 10. They start at 0, and the player spends 3 points among them. (pdf 90 / printed 89)
- Each creed has a primary Virtue:
  - Zeal: Avenger, Judge and Defender.
  - Vision: Visionary.
  - Mercy: Redeemer, Martyr and Innocent.
- No other Virtue may be higher than the primary Virtue, at creation or in play.
- Edges are bought with the same Virtue points, at a cost equal to the edge's level. A level 2 edge costs 2, so level 1 plus level 2 together use all 3 points. (pdf 91 / printed 90)
  - Edges in a path must be bought in order.
  - Edges from another creed's path need points in that path's Virtue.
  - No edge from another path may be higher than the character's best edge in their own primary path.
- Freebies can't buy Virtues or edges. (pdf 90 / printed 89)

## Conviction and Willpower
- Willpower is 3. (pdf 85 / printed 84)
- Starting Conviction depends on creed. Freebies can raise it, up to 8 at most for a new character. (pdf 91 / printed 90)

## Freebie points
- 21 freebie points. Traits may be raised to 4 or 5 at this step. (pdf 85 / printed 84)

### Freebie costs (pdf 86 / printed 85)

| Trait | Cost |
|---|---|
| Attribute | 5 per dot |
| Ability | 2 per dot |
| Background | 1 per dot |
| Willpower | 1 per dot |
| Conviction | 1 per dot, 8 at most to start |
| Virtue or edge | not allowed |

## Note for WoDX
House rule: Hunter: The Reckoning is the hunter rule set from 1999 on. Earlier eras use Hunters Hunted II, or inquisitors in the Dark Ages.
