# Changeling: The Dreaming 20th Anniversary Edition: creation totals

Book: Changeling the Dreaming 20th Anniversary.pdf. Page offset: printed = pdf − 1. Every ref below gives both.

## Attributes
- Every Attribute starts at 1 dot. Physical, Social and Mental get 7 / 5 / 3 dots. (pdf 143 / printed 142)

## Abilities
- Talents, Skills and Knowledges get 13 / 9 / 5 dots. (pdf 143–144 / printed 142–143)
- No Ability above 3 at this step.
- C20 adds Abilities to the usual lists, among them Kenning, Gremayre, Enigmas and Technology. The full lists are in the chart. (pdf 149 / printed 148)

## Advantages
- Backgrounds: 5 dots, and a single Background can take all 5. (pdf 144 / printed 143)
- A noble character needs the Title Background. (pdf 143 / printed 142)
- Arts: 3 dots. Realms: 5 dots. (pdf 144–145 / printed 143–144)

## Tempers (pdf 145 / printed 144; seeming changes on pdf 149 / printed 148)
- Willpower 4, Glamour 4 and Banality 3. No points are spent here.
- Seeming changes:
  - Childling: +1 Glamour.
  - Wilder: +1 Glamour or +1 Willpower, the player's choice.
  - Grump: +1 Willpower.
- Permanent Willpower and Glamour can't go above 10. Permanent Banality 10 means the changeling is Undone.

## Birthrights and Frailties
- These come from kith. Bonuses from them can take a Trait past 5, and raise that Trait's cap to match. For example, the satyr Birthright gives +1 Stamina, so a satyr's Stamina cap is 6. (pdf 145–146 / printed 144–145)

## Freebie points
- 15 freebie points. (pdf 145 / printed 144)
- Freebies can't raise a Trait above 5 (except through a Birthright or a house Boon), can't raise Glamour or Willpower above 10, and can't raise Banality.
- Merits and Flaws are optional, with up to 7 points of Flaws, for a total of 22 freebies at most. (pdf 146 / printed 145; pdf 177 / printed 176)
- Specialties are given to any Attribute or Ability at 4 or more. Crafts and Performance need one at any rating. (pdf 146 / printed 145)
- Threshold (Musing or Ravaging) and antithesis are chosen after the freebies. The Threshold can wait until play. (pdf 146 / printed 145)
