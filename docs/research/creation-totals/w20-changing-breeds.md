# W20 Changing Breeds: starting Rage, Gnosis and Willpower

Built from OCR: the private dump book-text/item-ocr/w20cb.txt. Pages are the dump's pdf pages, and printed = pdf + 7 (pdf 43 carries printed 50). Written in WoDX's own words. Code: FERA_STARTS in modules/wodx-creation/scripts/derived-rules.mjs (B7831).

The book gives each Breed its own starting Rage, Gnosis and Willpower. It gives no Attribute, Ability, Background, Gift or freebie figures of its own, so a Changing Breed is built with W20's (w20.md).

Notes:
- Every Corax also starts with one more dot each of Subterfuge, Enigmas and Dodge ("Raven's Gifts", pdf 88).
- The Bastet tribe rows in the dump carry no tribe heading beside the figures. Each row was matched by the tribe's Yava that follows it and its starting Gifts (Faerie Light and Satyr's Wisdom for the Ceilican, for example).
- The Mokole's Archid form takes as many Characteristics as the starting Gnosis (pdf 139).
- The lost Breeds (Apis, Grondr, Camazotz) are not playable and are left out.
