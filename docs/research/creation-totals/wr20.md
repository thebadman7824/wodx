# Wraith: The Oblivion 20th Anniversary Edition: creation totals

Book: Wr20 - Corebook - Rules.pdf. Page offset: printed = pdf − 65. Every ref below gives both.

## Attributes
- Every Attribute starts at 1 dot. Physical, Social and Mental get 7 / 5 / 3 dots. (pdf 176 / printed 111)

## Abilities
- Talents, Skills and Knowledges get 13 / 9 / 5 dots. (pdf 177 / printed 112)
- No Ability above 3 at this step. Before XP is earned, only freebies can raise an Ability to 4 or 5.
- Specialties can be taken for any Trait above 3. (pdf 177 / printed 112)

## Arcanoi
- 5 dots, with no Arcanos above 3 at creation. (pdf 177 / printed 112)
- Each Arcanos has Common powers, which any wraith can learn, and Initiate powers, which are mostly kept to its Guild. Joining a Guild is done through the Guild Background.

## Backgrounds
- 7 dots. (pdf 178 / printed 113)

## Passions and Fetters
- Passions: 10 dots. Each Passion has a statement, a core emotion and a rating from 1 to 5. (pdf 178 / printed 113)
- Fetters: 10 dots, each rated from 1 to 5. (pdf 179 / printed 114)
- The player may let the Storyteller assign the Fetters' importance in secret.

## Corpus, Pathos and Willpower
- Corpus is 10. It can't be raised with freebies. (pdf 179 / printed 114; pdf 180 / printed 115)
- Pathos = 5 + Memoriam, up to 10 with freebies. (pdf 179 / printed 114)
- Willpower = 5, up to 10 with freebies. Temporary Willpower starts equal to permanent. (pdf 180 / printed 115)

## Freebie points
- 15 freebie points. (pdf 180 / printed 115)
- The player can spend freebies one for one to shrink the Shadow's freebie pool.
- The player can also take up to 7 extra freebies by adding the same number to the Shadow's pool.

### Freebie costs (pdf 180 / printed 115)

| Trait | Cost |
|---|---|
| Attribute | 5 per dot |
| Arcanos | 5 per dot |
| Willpower | 2 per dot |
| Ability | 2 per dot |
| Passion | 2 per dot |
| Background | 1 per dot |
| Fetter | 1 per dot |
| Pathos | 1 per 2 dots |
| Corpus | cannot be bought |

## Shadow creation (Chapter 6)
- Step One: pick a Shadow archetype. The list runs from about pdf 292 to pdf 295 (printed 227–230).
- Step Two, Angst: roll dice equal to Willpower at difficulty 6. Ones don't cancel successes on this roll. Each success is 1 permanent Angst. The minimum is 1, and permanent Angst can't start above permanent Willpower. (pdf 295 / printed 230)
  - Temporary Angst can go above permanent Angst, up to 10.
  - When temporary Angst passes Willpower, the Shadow can try for Catharsis (printed 236).
  - When temporary Angst reaches 10, it turns into 1 permanent Angst.
  - Permanent Angst 10 means the wraith becomes a Spectre.
- Step Three, Dark Passions: 7 dots. (pdf 295 / printed 230)
- Step Four, Shadow freebies: 10 freebies, plus or minus whatever the player traded. (pdf 296 / printed 231)
  - They buy Angst (permanent or temporary), Dark Passions and Thorns.
  - Permanent Angst still can't start above Willpower. Temporary Angst can be bought up to 9.
  - Total Dark Passion dots can never be more than the wraith's total Passion dots.
- Thorns cost what each entry says, from 1 point per level (Spectre Prestige, Dark Allies, Tainted Relic, Infamy) up to 7 (Vampiric Nature). (pdf 296–299 / printed 231–234)
- The book gives no per-dot table for the Shadow's Angst and Dark Passions. The worked example spends 3 on Pact of Doom, 3 on Trick of the Light, 2 for a Dark Passion at rating 2, and 2 for 1 dot of temporary Angst. (pdf 299 / printed 234)
  - That suggests 1 per Dark Passion dot and 2 per temporary Angst dot. This is inferred from the example, not stated as a rule.

## Special item (a): a mortal becoming a wraith in play

### What the book gives
- **The optional Crucible scene** (sidebar, pdf 346 / printed 281). The book suggests it for exactly this case: characters played as mortals for a session or more, whose deaths happen during play.
  - The new wraith relives the moments around the death, with the Shadowlands bleeding into it.
  - It ends when the wraith accepts the death.
  - The strongest Passions and Fetters, Eidolon, Memoriam and the Shadow should all feature.
  - Under this rule, a wraith can't remove their own Caul until they complete a successful Crucible. The Storyteller may call it a success and play the scene later as a flashback.
- **The Caul.** New wraiths ("Enfants") arrive wrapped in a Caul, and see the Shadowlands distorted until it comes off. (pdf 345 / printed 280) There are three ways out:
  1. **Removing it yourself** (pdf 345 / printed 280).
     - The wraith must accept the death, spend 1 Willpower and roll Willpower at difficulty 4.
     - Each success adds 1 Pathos on top of the starting total, and may push Pathos past 10.
     - While above 10 Pathos, the wraith glows. Perception rolls to notice or track them are at −1 difficulty, unless an Arcanos hides them.
     - The extra Pathos lasts until it is spent.
  2. **A Reaper guiding the wraith out** (pdf 346–347 / printed 281–282).
     - The Reaper spends 1 Willpower to link to the Enfant. The process takes at least 10 minutes, and can take hours.
     - The Reaper rolls Charisma + Empathy at difficulty 9 − the Enfant's Memoriam.
     - Success frees the Enfant with little trauma.
     - The Reaper gains Pathos equal to the Enfant's Memoriam + 2, but not past the Reaper's maximum, and gets −2 difficulty on Empathy rolls for the rest of the session.
     - Several Reapers can work together, using teamwork rules, and split the Pathos evenly.
  3. **Harvested: the Caul torn off** (pdf 347 / printed 282). This has no mechanical cost, but the wraith comes out in a far worse state of mind.
- **Starting the wraith.** The book then builds the wraith from the normal creation totals above: Pathos 5 + Memoriam, Willpower 5, Corpus 10, Passions 10, Fetters 10, Arcanoi 5, wraith Backgrounds 7, and the Shadow (Angst roll, 7 Dark Passion dots, 10 Shadow freebies).

### What the book does not say
- It never says how a living character's existing traits carry over. Do the mortal's Attributes, Abilities and Willpower stay, or is the wraith rebuilt?
- WoDX needs a house rule here. One option is to keep the mortal's Attributes and Abilities, then add the wraith Advantages, Pathos, Corpus and the Shadow fresh. Ben should decide.
