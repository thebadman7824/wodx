# Mummy: The Resurrection: creation totals

Book: Mummy - the Resurrection.pdf. It has no text layer, so this comes from WoDX's OCR (tools-env\text\batch3\mummy-ocr). Page offset: printed = pdf + 4; the pdf has no cover pages. Every ref below gives both.

The summary is on pdf 52 / printed 56. The rebirth steps are on pdf 56 / printed 60.

Creation happens in two stages: the mortal Second Life, then the Rebirth as a mummy.

## Stage one: the Second Life (the mortal)
- Every Attribute starts at 1 dot. Physical, Social and Mental get 6 / 4 / 3 dots.
- Talents, Skills and Knowledges get 11 / 7 / 4 dots, with no Ability above 3.
- Backgrounds: 3 dots.
- 5 freebie points.

## Stage two: the Rebirth (pdf 52 / printed 56; pdf 56 / printed 60)
- **Attributes:** add 2 dots, either both in one Attribute or one each in two.
- **Abilities:** add 5 dots. These may raise an Ability above 3.
- **Hekau:** 3 dots, and one must go in the Amenti's primary Hekau path.
  - No Hekau path can be higher than Balance.
  - Each dot in a path gives one level of spells or rituals in that path.
- **Backgrounds:** add 2 dots, from the stage one list or the mummy-only list. The mummy-only Backgrounds are Arcane, Ba, Companion, Ka, Legacy, Memory, Vessel and Tomb.
- **Merits and Flaws:** chosen here.
- **Balance:** starts at 1, and freebies can raise it to 3 at most.
- **Willpower:** 5.
- **Sekhem:** equal to the final Balance. The summary chart says it starts at 1.
- **Freebies:** 10 more.

## Amenti and Hekau
- Amenti: Kher-minu, Khri-habi, Mesektet, Sakhmu, Sefekhi and Udja-sen.
- Hekau paths: Amulets, Alchemy, Celestial, Effigy, Necromancy and Nomenclature.
- The OCR jumbled which primary path goes with which Amenti. See Chapter Two of the book.
