# Vampire: The Dark Ages 20th Anniversary Edition: creation totals

Book: (VtDA 20) Vampire the Dark Ages 20AE.pdf. Page offset: printed = pdf − 1. Every ref below gives both.

## Attributes
- Every Attribute starts at 1 dot. Nosferatu start with Appearance 0. (pdf 153 / printed 152)
- Rank Physical, Social and Mental as primary, secondary and tertiary: 7 / 5 / 3 dots. (pdf 153 / printed 152; quick reference pdf 157 / printed 156)

## Abilities
- Rank Talents, Skills and Knowledges: 13 / 9 / 5 dots. (pdf 154 / printed 153)
- No Ability above 3 at this step. Freebies can raise them past 3. (pdf 154 / printed 153; pdf 157 / printed 156)
- Commerce and Crafts each need a field of expertise picked at creation. Working outside that field counts as 0 dots at +1 difficulty. More fields cost freebies or XP. (pdf 168 / printed 167)

## Disciplines
- 4 dots among the clan's three Disciplines. This is one more than V20, which gives 3. (pdf 154 / printed 153)
- Disciplines from outside the clan cost freebies only.
- Caitiff may choose any Disciplines, with the Storyteller's approval, or from a list the Storyteller sets.

## Backgrounds
- 5 dots. (pdf 154 / printed 153)
- The coterie may pool these Backgrounds: Allies, Contacts, Domain, Herd, Influence, Mentor, Resources and Retainers. (Pooling rules on printed 182.)
- The Storyteller may limit Backgrounds, or require some. The book suggests giving required ones for free. (pdf 154–155 / printed 153–154)
- The Background list is Allies, Alternate Identity, Contacts, Domain, Fame, Generation, Herd, Influence, Mentor, Resources, Retainers and Status. (pdf 159 / printed 158)

## Virtues
- Each Virtue starts at 1 dot, plus 7 more dots to spread. (pdf 155 / printed 154)
- Two Virtues come from the chosen Road: Conscience or Conviction, and Self-Control or Instinct. The third is always Courage.

## Road, Willpower and blood pool
- Road rating = the two Road Virtues added together, on a scale of 1 to 10. Most new characters start between 5 and 7. (pdf 155 / printed 154)
- Willpower = Courage. (pdf 155 / printed 154)
- Starting blood pool = 1d10 + Domain + Herd, capped at the Generation maximum. This differs from V20, where it is a plain 1d10. (pdf 155–156 / printed 154–155)
- Generation maximums are on printed 181.

## Freebie points
- 15 freebie points. (pdf 156 / printed 155)
- Merits and Flaws are optional, with up to 7 points of Flaws. (pdf 157 / printed 156)

## Differences from V20 core, for WoDX
- 4 Discipline dots, not 3.
- Starting blood pool adds Domain and Herd.
- Road replaces Humanity, with the same cost of 2.
- The cost table lists Blood Sorcery paths and rituals separately.
