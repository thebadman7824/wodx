# Dark Ages: Werewolf: creation totals

Book: WOD - Werewolf - The Dark Ages - Core Rulebook.pdf. It has no text layer, so this comes from WoDX's own OCR of pdf 78–145 (tools-env\text\daw). Page offset: printed = pdf − 1. Every ref below gives both.

The summary pages are pdf 81–82 / printed 80–81.

## Attributes and Abilities
- Every Attribute starts at 1 dot. Physical, Social and Mental get 7 / 5 / 3 dots.
- Talents, Skills and Knowledges get 13 / 9 / 5 dots, with no Ability above 3.
- Every werewolf must start with at least 1 dot in Primal-Urge.

## Advantages
- Backgrounds: 5 dots, restricted by tribe.
- Werewolves may take Allies, Contacts, Mentor and Resources from Dark Ages: Vampire (pp. 153–156 of that book), plus these: Ancestors, Fetish, Hunting Grounds, Kinfolk, Pure Breed, Rites and Totem. (pdf 82 / printed 81)
- Hunting Grounds is new compared with W20.
- Gifts: three Level One Gifts, one each from breed, auspice and tribe.
- Rank 1 (cliath). Renown: 3 points, split by auspice.

## Rage, Gnosis and Willpower
Rage comes from auspice, Gnosis from breed and Willpower from tribe.

## Bonus points
- 15 bonus points. This line calls them "bonus points", not freebies.

### Bonus point costs (pdf 82 / printed 81)
The OCR split the labels from the numbers. This pairing follows the order on the page:

| Trait | Cost |
|---|---|
| Attribute | 5 per dot |
| Ability | 2 per dot |
| Ability Specialty | 1 each, up to 3 per Ability |
| Background | 1 per dot |
| Gift | 7 per Gift, Level One only |

| Gnosis | 2 per dot |
