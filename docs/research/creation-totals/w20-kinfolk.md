# W20 Kinfolk: creation totals

Books: Werewolf: The Apocalypse 20th Anniversary Edition, chapter nine (page offset printed = pdf − 2), and W20 Kinfolk: A Breed Apart (offset printed = pdf − 1). Read from the private text dumps on 23 Nov 2026 (B7820).

From W20 pdf 382–384 / printed 380–382:
- Attributes 6 / 4 / 3. Abilities 11 / 7 / 4, none above 3.
- Backgrounds: 5, from the Garou list less Totem and the pack-only ones.
- Willpower 3.
- Freebies: 21. Flaws up to 7 points.
- No Gifts can be bought at creation; a Kinfolk learns them in play from a teacher, at 15 experience for a breed or tribe Gift and 20 for another (pdf 385 / printed 383).
- Gnosis comes only through the Gnosis Merit (5 to 7 points for 1 to 3 Gnosis).
