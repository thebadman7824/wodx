# Kindred of the East: creation totals

Book: WOD - Kindred Of The East - Core Rulebook.pdf. This comes from WoDX's text dump (tools-env\text\batch3\kote; file names are pdf pages). Page offset: printed = pdf + 8; the scan has no front matter. Every ref below gives both.

The summary is on pdf 70–71 / printed 78–79. The full steps are on pdf 78–86 / printed 86–94.

## Attributes and Abilities
- Every Attribute starts at 1 dot. Physical, Social and Mental get 7 / 5 / 3 dots.
- Talents, Skills and Knowledges get 13 / 9 / 5 dots, with no Ability above 3.
- Martial Arts costs 2 Skill dots per dot at creation, and 3 freebies per dot. (pdf 75 / printed 83)

## Disciplines
- 3 dots, and one must be a Demon Art. (pdf 70 / printed 78; pdf 78 / printed 86)
- The Discipline groups are Shintai (Blood, Bone, Jade, Flesh, Flame), Chi (Equilibrium, Yang Prana, Yin Prana, Tapestry) and Soul Disciplines.

## Backgrounds
- 5 dots. Generation and Status are not allowed. (pdf 78 / printed 86)
- The book adds Kuei-jin Backgrounds, among them Artifact, Nushi (bought by the whole wu, with its own cost chart) and Rites. A starting character should not know a Level Four or Five rite. (pdf 79 / printed 87)

## Virtues
- Chi Virtues (Yin and Yang) start at 1 each, plus 4 dots. (pdf 70 / printed 78)
- Soul Virtues start at Hun 1 and P'o 3. Freebies can raise them. P'o can't start below 3. (pdf 78 / printed 86)
- Balance:
  - If one Chi Virtue is 2 or more above the other, the character is attuned to it, as Yin or Yang.
  - If the two are within 1 of each other, the character is Balanced. (pdf 72 / printed 80)
- A Virtue pair normally totals 10 at most; for example, Yin 6 caps Yang at 4. A Chi Virtue above 5 is extraordinary for a new character. Higher Dharma allows more: Dharma 6 allows a pair to total 12. (pdf 80 / printed 88)

## Finishing touches
- Willpower = Hun. It must be raised to at least 5, either by raising Hun or by buying Willpower. (pdf 70 / printed 78; pdf 86 / printed 94)
- Starting Chi equals the permanent Yin and Yang ratings.
- Dharma starts at 1.
- Guanxi toward each other player character = 5 + that wu-mate's Charisma − the character's P'o (or Yin). (pdf 70 / printed 78)

## Freebie points
- 15 freebie points. (pdf 70 / printed 78; pdf 86 / printed 94)
