# V20 Ghouls and Revenants: creation totals

Book: V20 - Ghouls and Revenants.pdf. Page offset: printed = pdf − 1. Every ref below gives both.

The quick chart is on pdf 117 / printed 116. These rules are for starting characters. Elder ghouls need the Storyteller's agreement first. (pdf 116 / printed 115)

Character types: vassal (a ghoul with a domitor), independent ghoul, or revenant.

## Attributes and Abilities
- Every Attribute starts at 1 dot. Physical, Social and Mental get 6 / 4 / 3 dots. This is lower than a vampire's 7 / 5 / 3. (pdf 117 / printed 116)
- Talents, Skills and Knowledges get 11 / 7 / 4 dots, with no Ability above 3. A vampire gets 13 / 9 / 5.

## Disciplines
- Every ghoul and revenant gets Potence 1. (pdf 117 / printed 116)
- One more Discipline dot depends on the type:
  - Vassal: one dot in a Discipline that suits the character.
  - Independent ghoul: one dot in any Discipline, if the Storyteller agrees.
  - Revenant: one dot in a Discipline the family commonly uses.

## Backgrounds
- 5 dots. (pdf 117 / printed 116)
- The book adds new Backgrounds, such as Family Elder (revenants only) and Majordomo (a version of Retainers; one servant per dot). (pdf 125–126 / printed 124–125)

## Virtues, Humanity and Path
- Each Virtue starts at 1 dot. Revenants and Sabbat ghouls get 5 more dots; all other ghouls get 7. (pdf 117 / printed 116; pdf 126 / printed 125)
- Humanity = Conscience + Self-Control. (pdf 126 / printed 125)
- Revenants may follow a Path of Enlightenment instead of Humanity. Ghouls need the Storyteller's express permission for one. No Path can start above 5.
- Willpower = Courage.

## Blood pool
The book gives two different numbers for this:
- The quick chart says Blood Point 1, whatever the character's age. (pdf 117 / printed 116)
- The "Ghoul Blood Pools" section has the player roll a die for starting blood. The Storyteller may allow a reroll on a 1, and independent ghouls halve the result. (pdf 141 / printed 140)
- The same section says a starting ghoul can hold only 2 points, plus 1 per 200 years of regular feeding.
- The sample revenant has a maximum of 10, starts at 1, and makes back 1 point each night. (pdf 141 / printed 140)

WoDX should pick one. A reasonable reading: a ghoul starts at 1 and holds 2 at most; a revenant holds 10 and makes its own vitae.

## Freebie points
- 21 freebie points. (pdf 117 / printed 116; pdf 126 / printed 125)
- Merits and Flaws are optional. The quick chart says the sum of Merits and Flaws can't exceed 7 points. (pdf 117 / printed 116)
- The book lists which V20 Merits and Flaws suit ghouls and which are not allowed. (pdf 127 / printed 126)

### Freebie costs (pdf 117 / printed 116)

| Trait | Cost |
|---|---|
| Attribute | 5 per dot |
| Ability | 2 per dot |
| Discipline | 10 per dot |
| Background | 1 per dot |
| Virtue | 2 per dot |
| Humanity | 1 per dot |
| Willpower | 1 per dot |

### Experience costs (pdf 141 / printed 140)

| Trait | Cost |
|---|---|
| New Ability | 3 |
| New Discipline | 20 |
| New Thaumaturgy Path | 20 (Tremere ghouls only) |
| Willpower | current |
| Humanity | current × 2 |
| Virtue | current × 2 |
| Ability | current × 2 |
| Attribute | current × 5 |
| Thaumaturgy Path | current × 15 (Tremere ghouls only) |
| Clan or family Discipline | current × 15 |
| Other Discipline | current × 25 |

Ghouls get the cheaper rate for their domitor's clan Disciplines, and revenants for their family's. Independent ghouls pay more for Disciplines.

## Note for WoDX
House rule: ghouls use the V20 Dark Ages ghoul rules in every era. This file is the modern V20 reference, kept for comparison and for revenants.
