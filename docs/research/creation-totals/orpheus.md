# Orpheus: creation totals

Book: Orpheus 1 - Corebook.pdf. It has no text layer, so this comes from WoDX's OCR (tools-env\text\batch3\orpheus-ocr). Page offset: printed = pdf − 1. Every ref below gives both.

The summary is on pdf 123–125 / printed 122–124. The full steps are on pdf 129–131 / printed 128–130.

## Attributes and Abilities
- Every Attribute starts at 1 dot. Physical, Social and Mental get 6 / 4 / 3 dots, as for mortals.
- Talents, Skills and Knowledges get 11 / 7 / 4 dots, with no Ability above 3.

## Advantages
- The Shade's automatic Horror is recorded here.
- Backgrounds: 2 standard dots plus 2 Orpheus dots, the Orpheus dots only for characters in the Orpheus Group. (pdf 123 / printed 122)
- The Background list is Allies, Arsenal, Artifact, Contacts, Destiny, Detective License, Health Insurance, Influence, Mentor, Memorial, Patron, Personal Trainer, Reincarnate, Resources and Status. (pdf 125 / printed 124)
- Stains: pick 3. (pdf 130 / printed 129)
  - Freebies can't buy Stains.
  - Up to 2 extra Stains can be taken, for 1 more freebie each.
  - A character can call on a Stain on purpose, at a cost, only at Spite 4 or more.

## Vitality, Willpower and Spite
Each starts at the Shade's base plus the Nature's modifier. (pdf 123 / printed 122; pdf 131 / printed 130)

| Shade | Vitality | Willpower | Spite | Automatic Horror | Prohibited Horrors |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Banshee | 5 | 3 | 0 | Wail | Helter Skelter, Congeal |
| Haunter | 5 | 4 | 1 | Inhabit | Forebode, Wail |
| Poltergeist | 5 | 5 | 2 | Helter Skelter | Unearthly Repose, Storm-Wending |
| Skinrider | 4 | 6 | 2 | Puppetry | currently unknown |
| Wisp | 4 | 4 | 0 | Unearthly Repose | Puppetry, Juggernaut |

- Each Nature archetype adds a set of modifiers, for example Addict +1 / +1 / +3 or Defender +3 / +2 / +0. The full list is on pdf 124–125 / printed 123–124.
- Vitality can never go above 9 for most characters, or 7 for hues.
- Spite can't be raised with freebies. (pdf 131 / printed 130)

## Laments (pdf 123–124 / printed 122–123)

| Lament | Kind | Bonus | Weakness |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hue | ghost | can call on any of its 3 Stains without penalty for one scene | Vitality never above its starting rating, 7 at most; freebies can raise it, XP can't |
| Skimmer | projector | regains 1 Vitality per hour resting in the body | spends 1 Vitality per hour out of the body, or takes 1 bashing; soul damage lands on the body as bashing, soaked at difficulty 5; needs Meditation to project in one turn, or else it takes a minute |
| Sleeper | projector | can push unwanted Spite into the body and lose it while healing | can't return until the body is thawed and revived |
| Spirit | ghost | 5 extra freebies | a roaming Spectre nemesis that keeps coming back |
