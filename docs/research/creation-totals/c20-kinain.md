# C20 Kinain: creation totals

Book: Changeling: The Dreaming 20th Anniversary Edition, appendix II. Page offset: printed = pdf − 1. Read from the private text dump on 23 Nov 2026 (B7820).

C20 pdf 460–462 / printed 459–461 give the Kinain rules:
- Glamour: a Kinain holds 2 at most and gets 1 back each chapter from their own feelings. Changelings can Ravage them like any mortal.
- Heritage: the faerie parent's kith. One Birthright from that kith's two, the less overt one as a rule.
- Legacies: two, without Seelie or Unseelie sides; both Quests apply, only the expressed Legacy's Ban.
- Arts and Realms: 1 Art dot at creation; one dot of the parent's affinity Realm and one of the player's choice.
- Banality: starts at 3 and never harms them.
- Backgrounds: no Dreamers, Holdings, Remembrance, Retinue or Title. Faerie Blood (0 to 5) sets how many Arts they can learn, from one to six.

The book gives no Attribute, Ability, Background, Willpower or freebie figures for Kinain. WoDX takes the changeling's (7 / 5 / 3, 13 / 9 / 5, 5 Backgrounds, 15 freebies, C20 pdf 143–146 / printed 142–145) and leaves Willpower to the Storyteller (B7822, logged in docs/PENDING-REVIEW.md).
