# Werewolf: The Apocalypse 20th Anniversary Edition: creation totals

Book: W20 - Core Rulebook.pdf. Page offset: printed = pdf − 2. Every ref below gives both.

## Attributes
- Every Attribute starts at 1 dot. (pdf 118 / printed 116)
- Physical, Social and Mental get 7 / 5 / 3 dots. No Attribute can go above 5.
- The quick summary is on pdf 114 / printed 112.

## Abilities
- Talents, Skills and Knowledges get 13 / 9 / 5 dots. (pdf 118 / printed 116)
- No Ability above 3 at this step. Freebies can raise them past 3.

## Gifts
- Three Level One Gifts: one from the breed list, one from the auspice list and one from the tribe list. (pdf 118 / printed 116; pdf 116 / printed 114)
- More Gifts can be bought with freebies, but only Level One ones.

## Backgrounds
- 5 dots. (pdf 118 / printed 116)
- Each tribe restricts or discourages some Backgrounds, as listed under Tribe below. (pdf 115–116 / printed 113–114)
- The Background list is Allies, Ancestors, Contacts, Fate, Fetish, Kinfolk, Mentor, Pure Breed, Resources, Rites, Spirit Heritage and Totem. Totem is pooled across the pack. (pdf 116 / printed 114)

## Renown and Rank
- Starting permanent Renown is set by auspice, and temporary Renown starts at 0. Some pack totems are an exception to the 0. (pdf 118 / printed 116)
- Rank is 1 (cliath).

## Freebie points
- 15 freebie points. (pdf 119 / printed 117)
- Merits and Flaws are optional. They can only be taken at creation, with up to 7 points of Flaws, for a total of 22 freebies at most. (pdf 473 / printed 471)
- Metis can take some deformity Flaws (Deformity, Lame, Monstrous, One Arm, Deaf, Mute, Blind) as their metis deformity, for no freebies. (pdf 476 / printed 474)
