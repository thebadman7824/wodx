# Dark Ages: Inquisitor: creation totals

Book: WOD - Vampire - The Dark Ages - Inquisitor.pdf. This comes from WoDX's OCR (tools-env\text\inquisitor; file names are pdf pages). Page offset: printed = pdf − 1. Every ref below gives both.

The summary is on pdf 148 / printed 147. The full steps run to pdf 150 / printed 149.

## Attributes and Abilities
- Every Attribute starts at 1 dot. Physical, Social and Mental get 6 / 4 / 3 dots, as for mortals.
- Talents, Skills and Knowledges get 11 / 7 / 4 dots, with no Ability above 3.
- Every inquisitor must have at least Theology 1. Linguistics 1 (Latin) is recommended.

## Advantages
- One dot in the order's primary Superior Virtue. (pdf 150 / printed 149) Each Superior Virtue grows from a basic one:
  - Zeal, from Courage.
  - Wisdom, from Self-Control.
  - Faith, from Conscience.
- Order powers: either one Acolyte-level order Endowment (with its Curse), or the order benefit (with its drawback).
- Endowment types: Benedictions, Investitures, Legacies (House Murnau only) and Psalms.
- One Orison. The three are Sophia Patris, Sedulitas Filius and Boni Spiritus.
- Backgrounds: 5 dots. The list is Allies, Chapter-House, Contacts, Exposure, Flock, Holy Relics, Influence, Mentor, Rank, Resources and Retainers.
- Basic Virtues: each starts at 1 dot, plus 7 more, as in Dark Ages: Vampire. (pdf 150 / printed 149)

## Orders (pdf 148 / printed 147)
- Monastic orders: Knights of Acre, Sisters of St John, the Red Order.
- Lay orders: Oculi Dei, the House of Murnau.

## Finishing touches
- Conviction = the primary Superior Virtue + its basic Virtue, for example Faith + Conscience. The scale is 1 to 10.
- Piety = Conscience + Self-Control.
- Willpower = Courage + Zeal.

## Bonus points
- 21 bonus points.
- Endowments and Orisons can't be bought with bonus points.

### Bonus point costs (pdf 148 / printed 147)

| Trait | Cost |
