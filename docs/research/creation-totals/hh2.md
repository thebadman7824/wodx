# The Hunters Hunted II (V20): creation totals

Book: The Hunters Hunted II (V20 line). Page offset: printed = pdf − 1. Read from the private text dump on 23 Nov 2026 (B7820).

The summary chart is on pdf 36 / printed 35; the steps run to pdf 42 / printed 41.

- Attributes: 1 free dot each, then 6 / 4 / 3. The Storyteller may raise this to 7 / 5 / 3 so hunters stand closer to vampires.
- Abilities: 13 / 9 / 5, none above 3 before freebies.
- Backgrounds: 5. The book adds Armory, Base of Operations and Guide.
- Virtues: 1 free dot each, then 7 more. Conviction may stand in for Conscience with the Storyteller's leave (V20 p. 314).
- Humanity = Conscience + Self-Control. Willpower = Courage.
- Freebies: 21. Flaws up to 7 points.
- Numina get no free dots: each is bought with freebies, from paths that go together.

| Trait | Freebie cost |
|---|---|
| Attribute | 5 per dot |
| Ability | 2 per dot |
| Numina | 7 per dot |
| Background | 1 per dot |
| Virtue | 2 per dot |
| Humanity | 2 per dot |
| Willpower | 1 per dot |
