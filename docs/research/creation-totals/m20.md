# Mage: The Ascension 20th Anniversary Edition: creation totals

Book: Mage 20th Anniversary Edition.pdf. Page offset: printed = pdf − 1. Every ref below gives both.

## Attributes
- Every Attribute starts at 1 dot. Physical, Social and Mental get 7 / 5 / 3 dots. (pdf 256 / printed 255)

## Abilities
- Talents, Skills and Knowledges get 13 / 9 / 5 dots. (pdf 256 / printed 255)
- No Ability above 3 at this step. Freebies can raise them past 3.

## Backgrounds
- 7 dots, not 5 as in the other lines. (pdf 257 / printed 256)
- Every mage should have at least Avatar 1. The book gives this as advice, not as a hard rule. (pdf 255 / printed 254)
- The magickal focus (paradigm, practice and instruments) costs no points. (pdf 257 / printed 256)
- Backgrounds marked "$" on the list cost double, for example Sanctum/Laboratory and Totem. Totem is for shamanic characters only. (pdf 254 / printed 253)
- Requisitions and Secret Weapons are for Technocratic Union characters only.

## Spheres
- 6 dots. (pdf 258 / printed 257)
- The first dot placed is the Affinity Sphere. It must be one of the Affinity Spheres the character's group allows.
- No Sphere can start higher than Arete.

## Arete
- Arete 1 is free. More dots cost 4 freebies each. (pdf 258 / printed 257; pdf 254 / printed 253)
- The maximum at creation is 3.

## Willpower, Quintessence and Paradox
- Willpower starts at 5. (pdf 258 / printed 257)
- Quintessence starts equal to the Avatar Background. Paradox starts at 0.

## Freebie points
- 15 freebie points. (pdf 258 / printed 257)
- Flaws give up to 7 points. (pdf 254 / printed 253)

## Special item (b): Awakening in play

### What the book gives
- M20 treats the Awakening as story, not as a set of rules. The prelude should sit just before or just after the Awakening, and the book gives guidance on when in life people Awaken. (pdf 263–264 / printed 262–263) It has no procedure for turning an existing Sleeper character into a mage.
- **Arete.** A Sleeper has no Arete; the Arete scale starts at 0 for "Sleeper" and 1 for "Initiated". (pdf 329 / printed 328)
  - A new mage gets Arete 1 free. (pdf 258 / printed 257)
  - Raising Arete takes a Seeking. After a Seeking succeeds, the next dot can be bought. (pdf 330 / printed 329; Seekings are on printed 44 and 367–369)
- **Spheres.** The first Sphere dot is the Affinity Sphere, and no Sphere may be higher than Arete. (pdf 258 / printed 257)
- **Willpower limit.** If permanent Willpower is lower than Arete, both Sphere ranks and casting dice are limited to Willpower. (pdf 330 / printed 329)
  - This matters for a mortal who Awakens: the mortal may have Willpower 3, while a new mage character starts at 5.
- **Avatar and Quintessence.**
  - Every mage should have at least Avatar 1. (pdf 255 / printed 254)
  - Quintessence starts equal to Avatar, and Paradox at 0. (pdf 258 / printed 257)
- **Focus.** At Arete 1 and 2, every Effect must go through a paradigm, a practice and at least seven instruments. Mystics may start dropping instruments at Arete 3; technomancers only at Arete 6. (pdf 330 / printed 329)

### What WoDX has to decide
- For a mortal who Awakens in play, the book gives no rule for carrying traits over. A suggested house rule, for Ben to approve:
  - Keep Attributes, Abilities and Backgrounds.
  - Add Arete 1, one Affinity Sphere dot and Avatar 1 if the character doesn't have it.
  - Set Quintessence equal to Avatar and Paradox to 0.
  - Leave Willpower as it is, noting that the Willpower limit above will cap the new mage until it is raised.
  - Pick a paradigm, a practice and seven instruments.
  - Later Spheres and Arete come from experience points, with a Seeking needed for each Arete dot.
