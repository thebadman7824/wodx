# Common rolls: what the menus covered, what was missing (2026-11-09)

Players at the 8 November playtest could not find a plain Perception check, or other everyday rolls, in the WoDX actions menu. This note checks the menu against V20's own lists of rolls and records what WoDX Actions 3.13.0 adds. Pages are V20 printed pages (the pdf page is 8 more), read from the core book on the PC.

## What was there

- **Action / Dramatic system** already rolled any Attribute + Ability, or Willpower, Road or a Virtue, at any difficulty, as a simple, extended, resisted or teamwork roll. Its preset list held the V20 Dark Ages dramatic systems (V20 Dark Ages pp. 332-339): climbing, riding, driving a cart, intrusion, jumping, lifting, forcing a door, swimming, throwing, pursuit, shadowing, sneaking, noticing, creation, investigation, repair, research, tracking, carousing, credibility, fast-talk, interrogation, intimidation, oration, performance, plus Primal-Urge senses, tracking by scent and the mage research roll.
- So a Perception + Alertness roll could be made, but only by opening that dialog and either picking "Noticing" in a list of 30 or setting the two selects by hand. The button's name did not say so, and nothing on the menu itself said "Perception".

## What was missing

1. **A way to find the everyday roll quickly.** The free roll was hidden behind the words "Action / Dramatic system". This was the main gap.
2. **V20 dramatic systems for the later eras** (pp. 257-266) not in the list: Driving with Drive (p. 258), Computer Use (p. 263), Carrying Capacity (p. 259), and Science for Research (p. 265). Awakening (p. 262) is already the Awaken by day button.
3. **Common pools from V20's own examples** (p. 255) and Ability write-ups (pp. 100-111) with no preset: Wits + Alertness, Perception + Awareness, Perception + Empathy, Perception + Larceny, Dexterity + Athletics for balance, Stamina + Athletics to keep going, Stamina + Stealth to hold still, Wits + Stealth, Wits + Drive to swerve, Dexterity + Subterfuge for sleight of hand, Wits + Survival for shelter, Wits + Crafts, Intelligence + Medicine, Manipulation + Expression, Charisma + Etiquette, Charisma + Streetwise, Charisma + Intimidation, Charisma + Leadership, Manipulation + Animal Ken, Wits + Subterfuge.
4. **Rolls of a Trait alone named as such**: a Willpower roll (pp. 266, 274), Courage, Self-Control or Instinct, and Conscience or Conviction (pp. 119-120) for characters outside the Kindred menus, Stamina to resist (sunlight nausea is the book's own example, p. 302), and each Attribute alone.

## What 3.13.0 adds

- **Common checks**, a new button second in every line's Act group, next to the free roll. One searchable list of 41 checks in six groups (senses, body, knowing, people, holding firm, an Attribute alone). Picking one shows the pool the character will roll and the page; Roll posts the card. Each check lists its pools best first, and the roller takes the first one the character has dots in, so a Dark Ages character gets Ride or Legerdemain where a modern one gets Drive or Larceny. With no dots anywhere it rolls the first pool untrained: a Talent rolls the Attribute alone, a Skill adds 1 to the difficulty, and a Knowledge is refused (the existing untrained rules and setting).
- The same 41 checks are presets in the free roll's list, marked "Common:", for when a check has to be resisted, extended or rolled as teamwork.
- The free roll is renamed **Any roll / dramatic system**, and its presets now pick the best of their listed pools for the character (research, creation and the new ones).
- New presets: Driving a car (Dexterity + Drive), Computer use, Carrying capacity, and Science in Research.

## Still not covered (by choice)

- Combat rolls (attack, dodge, soak, initiative) stay in WoDX Combat.
- Line powers keep their own buttons; the common checks do not repeat them.
- The flavour line on a card is chosen by the card's theme, so a successful Notice roll can get a general line ("Done, and nobody heard a thing"). Worth a pass on flavor.js later.
