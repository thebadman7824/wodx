# 6b research notes: Werewolf and Fera

Mechanics only, in our own words, with page references. No book text is copied.
Rothenstein is Dark Ages, so Dark Ages: Werewolf (DA:W, 2003) is the basis; W20 fills gaps and covers other eras.
Page offsets for the library PDFs: DA:W book page = PDF page − 1 (OCR in the scratchpad); W20 core = PDF − 2; W20 Changing Breeds = PDF + 7; W20 Kinfolk = PDF − 1.

## Character creation (DA:W pp. 79–80 summary sidebar)
- Breed sets starting Gnosis: homid 1, metis 3, lupus 5 (metis value to confirm from p. 51). Each breed, auspice and tribe gives a beginning Gift list.
- Auspice sets starting Rage: Ragabash 1, Theurge 2, Philodox 3, Galliard 4, Ahroun 5. Beginning Renown: Ragabash 3 as chosen; Theurge 3 Wisdom; Philodox 3 Honor; Galliard 2 Glory 1 Wisdom; Ahroun 3 Glory.
- Tribe sets starting Willpower and Background limits (tribes pp. 56–75).
- Rank 1 (cliath), 5 Background points, 3 Gifts (one each of breed, tribe, auspice), 15 freebies.
- Abilities follow DA:V with changes: Leadership is a Skill; Talent Primal-Urge (required, at least 1 dot); Enigmas replaces Theology; Rituals replaces Seneschal (DA:W p. 79).

## Rage (DA:W pp. 89–91; W20 pp. 144–145, 261–262)
- Spend only under stress. Extra actions: declare at the start of the turn; at most half permanent Rage (DA:W rounds up). DA:W only: the safe number of extra actions is capped by the lower of Dexterity and Wits; each beyond that is at +3 difficulty, except in frenzy. Rage actions can't be split into multiple actions.
- Spend 1 Rage: change form instantly; ignore stun (lost more levels in one turn than Stamina); DA:W only: ignore one health level's wound penalty for one turn.
- Remaining active (below Incapacitated): Rage roll, difficulty 8, once per scene; each success heals a level. DA:W: healing bashing damage frenzies only on 4+ successes; healing lethal or aggravated always means berserk frenzy and a Battle Scar. W20: always berserk frenzy and a Battle Scar.
- Losing the wolf: temporary Rage and Willpower both at 0. The Garou falls to breed form and can't regain Rage until a Willpower point comes back.
- The Curse: for each point of Rage above a human's Willpower, −1 die on Social rolls with that human. Animals are affected too; vampires and mages are not, but DA:W inquisitors are (DA:W p. 91; W20 p. 262 uses avoidance).
- W20 only: Beast Within, −1 social die per point of Rage above the werewolf's own Willpower (p. 145).
- Regaining Rage:
  - First sight of the moon each night: DA:W gives new 1, half or waxing 3, full or eclipse 4. W20 gives new 1, waning 2, half or waxing 3, full 4. All Rage comes back when the phase matches the auspice.
  - The Storyteller may also give Rage for a botch, humiliation or confrontation.
  - DA:W only, Embrace Pain: first wound of a fight +1, once per combat, not in frenzy.
  - New story: roll one die for the Rage pool.

## Frenzy (DA:W pp. 94–95; W20 pp. 261–262)
- A Rage roll with 4+ successes means frenzy. Spending 1 Willpower stops it but ends the turn's actions. On 6+ it is the Thrall of the Wyrm, and Willpower can't stop it.
- Berserk: shift to Crinos or Hispo and attack. Gnosis ≥ Rage spares packmates; Rage > Gnosis attacks anything that moves and has no memory afterwards. DA:W: may cost Glory.
- Fox: shift to Lupus and flee to safety. DA:W: usually costs Wisdom.
- Thrall: a breed-specific horror on a Wits (7) botch after killing or incapacitating someone. Costs Wisdom, maybe Honor.
- While frenzied: no Gifts, fetishes, stepping sideways, maneuvers or pack tactics; only bite or claw; Rage extra actions still allowed; wound penalties ignored.
- Ending it: once the trigger is over, a Willpower roll at difficulty equal to permanent Rage, retried each turn.
- W20 rank bonus: Rank 3 +1 to frenzy difficulty, Rank 4 +2, Rank 5 +2 and 5+ successes needed, Rank 6 +2 and 6+ (p. 144). DA:W rank effects are on pp. 96–97 (to read).
- W20 Rage-roll difficulty by moon: new 8, crescent 7, half 6, gibbous 5, full 4. −1 if the moon matches the auspice, or −1 in Crinos (the two don't stack) (p. 262). DA:W to check.
- Triggers (W20 p. 262 list): humiliation, strong emotion, hunger, confinement, helplessness, taunting, much silver, being wounded, a packmate wounded.

## Gnosis (DA:W pp. 91–93; W20 p. 146)
- Rage and Gnosis can't be spent or rolled in the same turn. This includes stepping sideways, except for Gifts that need both.
- Silver carried: each object −1 to the Gnosis pool, more for potent ones; it ends a day after the silver is put away. W20 table: 5 bullets = 1, klaive 1, grand klaive 2; every 5 objects across the pack affect all members (W20 p. 259).
- Regaining Gnosis:
  - Meditation: at least one hour, Wits + Enigmas difficulty 8, up to 1 point per hour; once per day, +1 difficulty for each further day that week (max 10).
  - Sacred Hunt: the whole pool comes back.
  - Bargaining with a spirit.
  - Between stories: Charisma + Enigmas, DA:W difficulty 7.

## Stepping sideways (DA:W p. 93) — compare with Phase 2 crossings
- DA:W Gauntlet:
  - 8 in major cities, 6 in most towns, 5 in villages and farmland, 4 in wilderness.
  - 3 at an active caern, 2 at a powerful caern; a legendary caern needs no roll.
- Shift time: 1 success takes 5 minutes, 2 take 30 seconds, 3+ is instant. Each failed attempt at the same spot adds +1 (max 10).
- Botch: lost in the Pattern Web, then turns up somewhere else without memory.
- A reflective surface gives −1, and on a botch the surface is ruined instead of the Garou being trapped.
- One "opener of the way" can roll for the whole pack (pack members only).
- Peeking: same roll, 1 success, no time lapse, and no perception of one's own side meanwhile.

## Battle Scars (DA:W pp. 93–94; W20 pp. 259–260)
- Gained when remaining active, or from brutal localized wounds or torture. Healing one costs 1 temporary Glory.
- DA:W tiers by the temporary Glory they give:
  - Common, 1: superficial scars, collapsed lung, broken jaw.
  - Severe, 2: missing eye, missing fingers, brain damage.
  - Extreme, 3: maimed limb; extensive scarring (Appearance 0, constant pain, −1 to Rage-roll difficulty, +1 to extended-roll difficulty).
- W20 adds: deep scar 1, improper bone setting 1, cosmetic 2, gelded 1, spinal damage 2.

## Delirium (DA:W pp. 95–96; W20 pp. 262–263)
- Seeing a Crinos makes humans react by their Willpower. DA:W gives no chart in this passage, only flight, collapse, rationalizing, and weak hearts that may die. W20 chart (p. 263), as reactions by Willpower:
  - 1 catatonic, 2 panic, 3 disbelief, 4 berserk, 5 terror, 6 conciliatory: all forget.
  - 7 controlled fear, 8 curiosity: remember but rationalize.
  - 9 bloodlust, 10 no reaction: remember.
- W20: Wits + Occult (difficulty 9) adds effective Willpower; recordings don't trigger it; Kinfolk are immune.

## Silver, regeneration (W20 pp. 255–256, 259)
- Silver attacks: a homid or lupus in breed form takes normal damage. In any other form the damage can't be soaked, is aggravated, and is at least 1 level. A metis takes unsoakable aggravated damage in every form. Touching silver: 1 aggravated per turn, except a homid or lupus in breed form.
- Regeneration: the worst bashing or lethal level each turn. Under stress, Stamina difficulty 8 each turn (reflexive); a botch means none until rest. A homid or lupus in breed form heals as a human, except 1 level a day when critical. A metis regenerates in every form. Aggravated damage: 1 level a day resting in a regenerating form.
- DA:W silver: "p. 92" cross-reference; the silver section is in DA:W's own systems (to find).

## Forms (W20 pp. 285–287)
- Stamina + Primal-Urge; 1 success per form stepped through; difficulty is that of the form being left (Homid 6, Glabro 7, Crinos 6, Hispo 7, Lupus 6). Spending 1 Rage changes instantly, and a Garou can always return to breed form instantly.
- Partial change: 1 Willpower plus Dexterity + Primal-Urge at difficulty 9.
- Changes by form:
  - Glabro: Str +2, Sta +2, Man −2, App −1.
  - Crinos: Str +4, Dex +1, Sta +3, Man 0, App 0 (versus humans).
  - Hispo: Str +3, Dex +2, Sta +3, Man 0, −1 Perception difficulty, +1 bite die.
  - Lupus: from p. 287 (to read).
- The system fork already builds these as bonus items (create-helpers.js CreateShape) and has a shape-change dialog; WoDX should drive that, not duplicate it.

## Rites (W20 p. 202–203)
- Roll by rite type:
  - Charisma + Rituals at difficulty 7: Accord, Punishment.
  - Wits + Rituals at difficulty 7: Mystic.
  - Charisma + Rituals at 8 − Rank: Death.
  - Caern rites: Gnosis-capped.
  - The others still to read.
- Time: 10 minutes per level; minor rites take 2–5 minutes.
- A rite's level can't exceed the Rituals rating; two minor rites cost one Level One slot (W20 p. 139).

## Delirium chart (DA:W p. 97)
- DA:W has its own chart. The reactions by Willpower are the same names as W20's, but the share of people at each level differs:
  - 1: 13% · 2: 18% · 3: 17% · 4: 14% · 5: 11% — they forget.
  - 6: 10% · 7: 8% · 8: 6% — they remember but rationalize. Willpower 6 is "forget" in W20; DA:W makes it rationalize.
  - 9: 2.5% · 10: 0.5% — they remember.
- DA:W colour for 1200s Europe: the curious tell the Church; the bloodlust kind recall the Impergium. Kinfolk are immune.

## Renown and Rank (DA:W pp. 96–101)
- The three kinds of Renown are Glory, Honor and Wisdom, each with a creed. Every kind is kept as permanent dots plus a temporary pool, and the pool may exceed the dots. Renown can't be bought with experience, and a deed earns nothing if no one hears of it (spirits count). Lying costs Honor.
- Awards are tallied per session alongside experience (DA:W p. 99 sample table). A deed costing 7 or more in one category is a crime.
- 10 temporary in a category becomes 1 permanent. That takes a Rite of Accomplishment by a non-packmate of equal or higher rank, or a won challenge against a higher-ranked non-packmate, or rarely an earth-shaking deed at the Storyteller's call.
- A loss larger than the temporary pool forces 1 permanent to break into 10 temporary. Falling below the rank's needs drops the rank; Gifts already known are kept. Someone with nothing in a category can't lose more there.
- Rank (DA:W p. 101):
  - Frenzy difficulty: Rank 3 +1, Rank 4 +2, Rank 5 +2 and 5+ successes needed to frenzy.
  - Gifts can't be learned above one's rank.
  - Challenges are only legal within one rank.
  - Rising in rank needs the Renown plus a challenge set by a Garou of the target rank.
- Rank chart, Glory / Honor / Wisdom for Ranks 1–5 (Cliath, Fostern, Adren, Athro, Elder):
  - Ahroun: 2/1/0, 4/1/0, 6/3/1, 9/5/2, 10/9/4.
  - Galliard: 2/0/1, 4/0/2, 4/2/4, 7/2/6, 9/5/9.
  - Philodox: 0/3/0, 1/4/1, 2/6/2, 3/8/4, 4/10/9.
  - Theurge: 0/0/3, 1/0/5, 2/1/7, 4/2/9, 4/9/10.
  - Ragabash: total permanent Renown in any mix, 3, 7, 12, 17, 24.
- (W20 p. 251 carries its own chart, to compare. DA:W rules for Rothenstein.)

## Damage, soak, silver and regeneration (DA:W pp. 102, 106–107) — Rothenstein's rules
- Bashing and lethal: soaked at difficulty 6 in any form. Regeneration heals 1 level per turn; for lethal, a Stamina roll at difficulty 8 is needed to act and heal in the same turn.
- Aggravated (Garou or vampire teeth and claws, some spirits, fetish weapons, silver): soakable at difficulty 6 in any form except Homid.
- Silver:
  - A homid or lupus in breed form soaks it at difficulty 6, and the unsoaked part counts as lethal.
  - Otherwise it can't be soaked without a Gift or fetish, and it is aggravated.
  - In Homid form, metis and lupus take unsoakable aggravated damage but still regenerate; a homid in Homid takes nothing special but doesn't regenerate.
  - A lupus-breed in Lupus is spared aggravated silver but doesn't regenerate there.
- Forms (DA:W pp. 106–107), changes and effects:
  - Homid 6: none.
  - Glabro 7: Str +2, Sta +2, App −1, Man −1 (W20 has Man −2). Teeth do lethal damage, claws none. No Delirium.
  - Crinos 6: Str +4, Dex +1, Sta +3, App 0, Man −3. Aggravated teeth and claws; −1 Perception difficulty; −1 Rage difficulty; causes Delirium. Speech: 1–2 words, more costs 1 Willpower a sentence. Social penalties don't count with Garou. Metis breed form.
  - Hispo 7: Str +3, Dex +2, Sta +3, Man −3. +1 bite die over Crinos; aggravated; −1 Perception difficulty; human words cost Willpower.
  - Lupus 6: Str +1, Dex +2, Sta +2, Man −3. −2 Perception difficulty, double running speed, lethal bite, no human speech.
- Experience costs (DA:W p. 107):
  - Attribute current × 4; new Ability 3; Ability current × 2.
  - Gift of the character's breed, tribe or auspice: level × 3; any other Gift: level × 5.
  - Rage current; Gnosis current × 2; Willpower current. Further rows on p. 108 (to read).
- Dramatic systems (DA:W p. 105):
  - Scent tracking in Lupus: Perception + Primal-Urge, difficulty 4–9 by trail freshness.
  - Facedown: Charisma + Intimidation or Rage, whichever is higher, at difficulty = the opponent's Willpower. The first to reach the opponent's Wits + 5 successes wins. With Rage, 4+ successes means frenzy and a loss.
  - Animal Attraction: Charisma + Primal-Urge at difficulty = the target's Willpower, extended until successes exceed that Willpower. A botch triggers Delirium.
- Special maneuvers (DA:W pp. 102–104): jaw lock, leaping rake, taunt, rending armor, harrying, savage, and others. These belong to WoDX Combat (the system's combat list).
- Rite of Renunciation (p. 102): changing auspice drops the Garou to Rank 1 with 3 permanent Renown; known Gifts are kept, but old-auspice-only Gifts can't be learned again.

## Gifts (DA:W p. 111)
- A Gift's level can't exceed the werewolf's rank. Beginning characters get one each from breed, auspice and tribe.
- Four ways to learn one:
  - An elder summons the teacher spirit with the Rite of Summoning at the caern. This takes an hour, at most a night.
  - Another Garou teaches it, which takes a month. Using it before a month of practice is at +1 difficulty.
  - The werewolf summons the teacher spirit personally: risky, and +1 difficulty away from a caern.
  - A quest in the Umbra.
- Experience costs are on p. 107. Each Gift's write-up gives a cost (Gnosis, Rage or Willpower), a roll (pool and difficulty), and a duration (turn, scene, day). That is data to record per Gift item, never text to copy.

## Rites (DA:W pp. 143–155)
- Standard roll by type (DA:W p. 144 table):
  - Accord: Charisma + Rituals, 7.
  - Caern: varies, capped by Gnosis, 7.
  - Death: Charisma + Rituals, 8 − Rank.
  - Mystic: Wits + Rituals, 7.
  - Punishment: Charisma + Rituals, 7.
  - Renown: Charisma + Rituals, 6 (W20 to compare).
  - Seasonal: Stamina + Rituals, 8 − caern level.
  - Minor: no roll.
- −1 difficulty per 5 participants, counting the ritemaster; good roleplay may lower it more. At least 10 minutes per level.
- DA:W only: rites can't be bought with experience; they are taught.
  - The rite's level can't exceed Rituals.
  - One week per level, with a weekly Intelligence + Rituals roll at difficulty 10 − Intelligence.
  - A failed week costs 1 Willpower to carry on. A botch means waiting three moons.
- Rites Background: one level of rites per dot (p. 85).
- Mechanical rites to support:
  - Binding (Mystic 1): spend Gnosis to lower the spirit's Gnosis one-for-one, then roll Willpower at that difficulty; each success is a week of service. A talen's spirit goes free when the talen is used.
  - Talisman Dedication (1): 1 Gnosis per item; at most as many dedicated items as the Gnosis rating; attuned fetishes don't count.
  - Spirit Awakening (2): difficulty = the spirit's Rage.
  - Summoning (2): difficulty by spirit, −1 per hour of summoning, minimum 3:
    - Gaffling 4; Jaggling 5; totem avatar 7; Incarna 8–9; Celestine avatar 10.
    - The Gnosis roll's successes set the spirit's first attitude: 1 slow and hostile, 2 quick and hostile, 3 neutral, 4 benign, 5 friendly. A botch summons the wrong spirit.
    - Summoning an Incarna or Celestine earns Wisdom.
  - The Fetish (3): Wits + Rituals at a base difficulty of 10, −2 per permanent Gnosis spent. A botch means the spirit attacks.
  - Foresight (3): Gnosis at 7.
  - Totem (3): binds a pack totem.
  - Accomplishment (p. 152, renown rite): turns 10 temporary Renown into 1 permanent.
  - Contrition: difficulty = the offended one's Rage.
  - Cleansing: −1 difficulty at dawn; 1 Gnosis per extra target.
  - Renunciation: see above.
  - Passage.

## Fetishes and talens (DA:W pp. 83–84, 92; W20 p. 221)
- Fetish Background: one level of fetishes per dot.
- Attunement: a Gnosis roll at difficulty = the fetish's Gnosis; one success. On failure, no retry until the Garou has made peace with the spirit. Attuning also dedicates the fetish.
- Activating: a Gnosis roll at difficulty = the fetish's Gnosis, or spend 1 Gnosis for automatic success. Not in a turn where Rage is spent.
- Talens are one use; the spirit is freed when the talen is used.
- The system's Fetish item already has gnosis and difficulty fields.

## Spirits and chiminage (DA:W pp. 167–177)
- Spirit traits are Rage, Gnosis and Willpower, as for demons in DA:V pp. 292–293. Essence is normally their sum, spends on Charms, and is lost as damage; at 0 the spirit fades to slumber.
- A spirit with no Essence left can be bound into a fetish or harvested for Gnosis.
- Common Charms: Airt Sense, Materialize (needs Gnosis ≥ the Gauntlet), Realm Sense, Re-form. Others are on pp. 174–177, each with an Essence cost.
- Chiminage is the payment owed to a spirit or caern for a service: teaching, bridges, binding. It is a deal to record (who owes what to whom), not a roll.
- Pack totem (Totem Background, DA:W p. 86): 8 points across Willpower, Rage and Gnosis, plus Airt Sense and Re-form. Extra Background points buy more powers. Packmates may give experience at 2 per Background point.
- Moon bridges (p. 167): need a pathstone and the Rite of the Opened Bridge; a caern's Gatekeeper takes chiminage for their use. A matter for Phase 5's Umbral paths, not for this batch.

## Backgrounds (DA:W pp. 82–86)
- Ancestors: once per session, roll the rating at difficulty 8; each success adds a die to one chosen Ability (max 5) for the scene. A botch blocks it until the new moon. Silent Striders can't take it.
- Kinfolk: 1 = 2, 2 = 5, 3 = 10, 4 = 20, 5 = 50 kin. Red Talon kin are wolves.
- Pure Breed: extra dice equal to the rating on Social and challenge rolls with Garou. Not for Bone Gnawers; double cost for Warders; Silver Fangs need at least 3.
- Hunting Grounds is the best pack Anchor (DA:W p. 84, for pooling as in V20 DA p. 182). Its dots can lower the Gauntlet by 1 (minimum 4) or lower Survival, Animal Ken or herb difficulties by 1 (each at most 3 times).
