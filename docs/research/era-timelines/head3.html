House Events Desk

Round three: at least three hundred events in every era, from real history across Moravia, Europe, the Middle East and Asia, with World of Darkness readings where they fit. History cards are real history. Tied cards add a house reading for one or more lines. Named cards place a book character in a real year. The two prologue tabs (Living Gods and Classical) add Myth cards: legends whose year is the house's reading, which no character can check; years before 1 CE are shown as BCE, and book page references are pdf pages. Every card has a place and tags so other WoDX systems can find it, and links to Encyclopaedia entries where one matches. Round two cards still waiting are here too; round two history cards that repeat a round three event were left out. Events from 1990 to 2009 in the Modern tab belong to the classic branch only. The Future Darkness tab holds the future branch's own history from 1990 to 2099 (Branch history cards), built around the approved Future Darkness timeline, which Show approved events puts beside them. Mark each OK, Fix or Cut, or narrow the list with the filters and OK everything shown.
