# Deconfliction of the approved era timelines (2026-11-11)

Ben approved all 291 cards of run 2026-11-10t1 and asked for a check that no two events rule each other out. `deconflict.py` applies the rulings and writes `timeline-approved-2026-11-11.json`: 289 cards (284 kept as approved, 5 edited, 2 dropped). Precedence: the 20th-anniversary books over Revised, Revised over 2nd edition (B861).

How the check ran: every card was sorted by year and read beside the others on the same subject (a person, a city, a sect, a convention, a war). The same subjects were then checked against the dated tables in the modules: the Chronicle's events, the Great Maelstroms in WoDX Otherworlds, Charon's Stygia in the realm maps, and the natural rivalries in WoDX NPC.

## Checked and found to agree

- The Camarilla: founded 1435 (V20) and the first Conclave in 1486. Both cards stand.
- The Shattering: the 14th century and 1350 are the same event.
- The Virtual Adepts: renamed in 1880 in two books, defected in 1961.
- The Great Maelstroms in Otherworlds (1347, 1600, 1916, 1945, 1999) and Charon's Stygia to 1944 match the timeline.
- The 2004 tsunami in the Bhuvar falls after the Time of Judgment year. It stands only when the Storyteller skips the Time of Judgment endings, like every classic event after 2004.
- The Future Darkness branch (from 1990) is a separate branch, so its events are not clashes.
