# Layouts adapted from Cataclysm: Dark Days Ahead

`cdda_types.json` holds building layouts converted by `cdda.py` from the map data of Cataclysm: Dark Days Ahead
(https://github.com/CleverRaven/Cataclysm-DDA, files under data/json/mapgen and data/json/mapgen_palettes).
That data is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported licence
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/). The converted layouts are an adaptation and keep the same licence.
Each converted type names the Cataclysm map it came from in its `cdda` field.

To convert again: `python3 cdda.py <path to a Cataclysm checkout> [names]`.
