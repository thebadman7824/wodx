# wodx-senses changelog

Newest first. Moved from the module table in docs/MODULES.md on 12 November 2026; add each new release at the top.

- 0.11.1 (The words under sight rings and other-world marks use a plain colour for their outline, as the canvas library in Foundry 14 needs; the object form stopped the Penumbra, mage, chimerical and Shadowlands layers from drawing (B3540-B3546, testbed find))
- 0.11.0 (Other-world sights on the player's own map (B3540-B3547). A successful peek across the Gauntlet lays the Penumbra over the peeker's map: the map's own reflection, Banes from the scene's Wyrm corruption, a web veil for the era, and the spirits there marked friendly, watching, wary or hostile from the peeker's standing. Mages get Prime, Time, Entropy and Correspondence sights at the first rank of each Sphere (Dark Ages mages through the Pillar that fits); turning one on rolls the casting roll and the successes set how long it holds. Prime shows places of power, Tass carried and vulgar strain; Time shows echoes of what happened at the place (map events, wounds, crossings, magick); Entropy marks the hurt; Correspondence the ways to other places. Changelings and the enchanted see other changelings' fae mien, freeholds and the Dreaming dressed, and Banality greying the dull. Wraiths see the living scene from the Shadowlands, with their Fetters lit and other wraiths plain. A new token tool lets the Storyteller enchant mortals, a wraith tie Fetters to things on the map, and a changeling set a mien picture)
- 0.10.0 (Aura Perception rings show only after Read the Soul, and only as much as the successes showed: the shade at one, the colour from two, a false colour on a botch; a reading holds for an hour of game time. Loud noise or a gun fired nearby asks the Storyteller for a Willpower roll from a character using Heightened Senses. Needs WoDX Otherworlds 0.24.1 for the readings. B2900, B2901)
- 0.9.0 (what the senses tell in a mosque, a synagogue and a temple of the faiths of Asia: lines for sight, sound, smell and touch in every era, the call to prayer from the minaret and later through a speaker, the lamp over the ark, incense and bells; the three count as indoors and as holy ground for the spirit and the restless dead, as a church does. Needs WoDX NPC 0.50.0 for the new kinds of place. B2662)
- 0.8.1 (fix: on a scene with no place set, the place guessed from the scene's name is now read as its kind; before, the Storyteller's copy of an answer could fail with an error and the place lines fell back to the plainest set)
- 0.8.0 (the senses read how a character moves: from the air the eye reaches twice as far and sight is easier, low to the ground or in the water it reaches half as far, under the water 8 yards with no smell at all, inside the earth only dull sound; one line says where they are looking from; someone prone or under the water is picked out only on a closer look; Who sees them counts each side's stance)
- 0.7.1 (Day and night follow a scene's own latitude and hour (WoDX Chronicle 0.39.0))
- 0.7.0 (colour-blind safe colours for the sight rings, and words under every ring)
- 0.6.1 (a vampire's wolf or bat shape sharpens the senses)
- 0.6.0 (merits and flaws on the senses: Acute Sense, Bad Sight, Blind, Deaf; Medium and Deathsight channels; signs such as Smell of the Grave or Glowing Eyes for others to sense; a veiled token found only on a closer look)
- 0.5.1 (the Long Night reads sight by V20 Dark Ages)
- 0.5.0 (a quiet interface, Alt+Q; supernatural sight on the map: Heightened Senses, Aura Perception, Lifesight)
- 0.2.0 (tonight's weather in the lines and the difficulty)
- 0.2.1 (feasts counted from Easter are found; the curfew follows the church year)
- 0.3.0 (the screen in frenzy and Rötschreck, scent trails for a wolf's nose, the unseen shown only to those who find them)
- Senses (after Interactables): what a character sees, hears, smells and feels, answered in chat from the place, the hour and sky, and what is on the map, trimmed by light, walls and distance; a free look or a closer look (Perception + Alertness); powers and the other worlds; whispered with Share, a fuller copy for the Storyteller, 0.4.0 (the character's state on screen)
