# wodx-library changelog

Newest first. Moved from the module table in docs/MODULES.md on 12 November 2026; add each new release at the top.

- 0.30.0 (Made supernatural items now do things when used: each power is a Use button that rolls, spends from the item's own pool (blood, Glamour, Faith, Sekhem, Quintessence) or the user's, counts charges and one-use items, adds drawbacks such as Paradox to the user, moves points in and out (a periapt), and posts a card with Undo. Attribute bonuses of a worn item switch on and off with the equipped box through the system's own bonus list. Every line's crafting kit fills the same flag (B3690-B3692))
- 0.29.0 (Soulforged Artifacts and reworked relics join the crafting table as a Wraith kit in the wonders registry, with its own world switch "Soulforging Artifacts and relics (Wraith)". The goal, difficulty and Pathos come from WoDX Actions' soulforge rules, so the table and the forge window always agree The soulfire's Pathos, and an Artificer's pledged Corpus level, are paid when the work ends (B3720-B3722))
- 0.28.0 (Mage can make things: a Talisman (a Device for the Technocracy) holding up to one Effect a dot from the Effect keywords the mage knows, one-use charms (gadgets), Quintessence periapts (matrices), trinkets, and fetishes from the Garou list bound with Spirit 4 or Prime 4, by M20 and the Book of Secrets (pdf 152-162). The work is an extended Arete roll; the finished Wonder carries its Effects with its own Arete, its Quintessence and its Paradox. World setting "Making Wonders, charms, periapts and fetishes (Mage)" (B3684-B3688))
- 0.27.0 (One engine for every line's supernatural items: each game line registers a kit (its table, who may make what, the dialog inputs, the task, the items, the costs) with registerLine, and each kit names its own world switch. Werewolf is now the first kit; nothing changes in play (B3683))
- 0.26.0 (Bane fetishes: the six Black Spiral Dancer fetishes of W20 Book of the Wyrm (pdf 184-185), the baneklaive among them (Gnosis 7, Strength +3 aggravated, difficulty 7, silver). A Black Spiral Dancer binds Banes and sees only these; a Gaian Garou sees none of them. After the book check, Kinfolk no longer make fetishes unless the new world setting "Kinfolk with Gnosis may make fetishes and talens" is on (off by default) (B3680-B3682))
- 0.25.0 (Supernatural items by line, Werewolf first: data/wonders.mjs holds the W20 fetishes and talens with the spirit each needs, their Gnosis and level, klaives and other war fetishes as weapons, and fetishes of the other Changing Breeds; scripts/wonder-rules.mjs says who can make them, the difficulty, the long task and the items made (api.wonders). New world setting for making fetishes and talens (B3630-B3636))
- 0.24.0 (A character with no clothes worn on the sheet wears the clothes of WoDX Paperdoll's figure: station, strange dress and the witness description read them (B3442). What a witness remembers now names a Kindred's uncanny face marks ("with weeping boils") (B3443))
- 0.23.1 (Old imported goods take their own years (B3150-B3151): 34 goods from the imported lists that came in dated 1197 now start in the year they first turn up (ravioli 1300, the kilt 1590, the plague robe 1619, pesto 1860, the water heater 1868, the black belt 1886, shorts 1900, the cheongsam 1920 and others), and 14 that were old in one land and late in the rest say so (koji, tofu, onigiri, sashimi and kombu in East Asia; the hammock in the Americas; sandpaper and firecrackers in China; the fire piston in Southeast Asia; the tree spile and sassafras in North America))
- 0.23.0 (Cookery by region (B3100-B3102): the brews, quids and dishes of other lands get recipes of their own (sorghum beer, maize beer, pulque, kava, foaming cacao, the betel quid, chuño, rice crackers, yak butter, tsampa, injera, pemmican, dried fish), with six new raw goods to make them from (sorghum, millet, maguey sap, kava root, areca nuts, betel leaves). Maize is old in the Americas and new elsewhere. The recipe rules take an optional place ({way, region}) for what is to be had there)
- 0.22.0 (a plain-words Glossary in the Library: 82 of the game's words in our own words, each with the lines that use it and, where it differs, the word or the age it belongs to; a manual page, Finding your way, for the drawers, What can I do now?, Find anything and the window help; api manual.page opens the manual at a named page)
- 0.21.0 (gear by region (B3070-B3073): each piece of Gear by era says where it is at home, so the Americas, the Pacific and the far north before the settlers carry no steel, crossbow, horse lance or plate, plate stays in Christendom, the katana in Japan and the scimitar in the lands of Islam, and guns reach each land in the year it took them up (Japan 1543); 64 weapons of other lands under their own names (the tachi, naginata and yumi, the jian and dao, the talwar and khukuri, the shamshir and kilij, the takouba, shotel and assegai, the kris and dha, the macuahuitl and atlatl dart, the champi, the patu and taiaha, the war club and harpoon), each fighting as a book weapon of its kind with that weapon's page; the Gear window shows what is found where the scene stands, or every land; 0.20.0 is taken by the Player UI branch)
