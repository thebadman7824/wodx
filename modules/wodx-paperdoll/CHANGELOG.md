# wodx-paperdoll changelog

Newest first. Moved from the module table in docs/MODULES.md on 12 November 2026; add each new release at the top.

- 0.15.0 (looks for monsters and for the fae on sprite tokens: thirty-one creatures of legend each drawn after what its own entry says of it, from the dragon and the gryphon to a hedge of blood roses, with those that go on two legs drawn as figures of their own size; a changeling is drawn in its fae mien by kith, and a wraith or a spirit pale, half seen and drifting instead of stepping; new moves: a block and a parry with a spark when the fight rules rolled that defence, a jump, a crawl, and stooping to work at a lock when a lock is picked; the roster carries each character's line and kith; the creatures named in the books, from a book check, are drawn by name in about 120 shared looks by body plan, a wisp, a swarm, a crawler, a flyer, a beast or a figure of their own size; B2613 to B2617; not deployed)
- 0.14.1 (a seated figure is plainly down on the ground, legs out in front or knees apart, where before it only looked a little shorter; of several things in hand the batch draws the largest, and a torch before a knife; found on the testbed; not deployed)
- 0.14.0 (more moves for sprite tokens: a figure sits or kneels and stays so until it stands up or walks off, looks about, speaks under a small speech mark when its character says a line, leans in to feed, and blurs when it changes form; it climbs, swims, flies and rides with a sheet of its own for each; a button on the token for whoever may move it; a torch is carried lit; first versions made from the walk; not deployed)
- 0.13.3 (fix: a figure whose health track is filled by hand, on the sheet or by a macro, now falls and stays down too, not only one harmed from a damage card; found on the testbed; not deployed)
- 0.13.2 (fix: the Figure button on a token's sheet now opens the character's own figure, so a kept figure reaches the batch; found on the testbed; not deployed)
- 0.13.1 (the batch draws each character as itself: only armour and gear count as worn, teeth and claws are not carried, a thing the Library does not list is drawn as the nearest thing it does, a character with no portrait look gets the skin and hair its portrait would start from, one with nothing on is dressed plainly for the chronicle's year, the weapon is carried in the walk and its move comes first; found on the testbed; not deployed)
- 0.13.0 (sprite tokens react: a figure that is harmed takes the blow, one put out of the fight falls and stays down on the map until it is healed or helped up, and one that dies plays its death, a Cainite crumbling to ash at Final Death; a missed attack is dodged; figures lean into a run over a long stretch and go low when they crawl; every sprite has these sheets, made from its own walk; not deployed)
- 0.12.0 (the figure menu: a Figure button on a character's sheet opens a window where the player puts the whole walking figure together, skin, hair and how it is worn, and a piece for each of twelve places from the 458 wardrobe pieces the rig draws, with a search box, the pieces of the chronicle's year first, and a dye for any piece; a quick picture shows the figure as it is built, and the walking sprite is drawn from the kept choice with the next batch; not deployed)
- 0.11.0 (a sprite for every character, drawn in batches outside the game: the game writes a roster of its characters with their skin and hair, what they wear and what they hold; tools/sprites/batch.py draws walk, still and attack sheets for each, people from their worn things and animals from their names; the game learns the sheets from the batch's manifest, offers a token its own character's sprite, and can dress a whole scene; not deployed)
- 0.10.1 (shadow effects have a pale violet rim so they can be seen on a night map; found on the testbed; not deployed)
- 0.10.0 (a power's effect fits the power: 84 effect sheets, twelve colours by seven shapes, laid over the figure's casting move; the look is read from the power's name and group, then its kind, then the character's line, so Dominate shows a violet gaze, Obtenebration rising shadow, a Gift pale green sparks, a wraith's Arcanos grey tendrils; the elder's casting sheet no longer carries a fixed blood bolt; not deployed)
- 0.9.1 (sprite tokens play their casting move when a power is rolled from the Actions menu; attacks and powers rolled on a player's own computer now play too, sent by the computer that rolled them; not deployed)
- 0.9.0 (sprite tokens play every attack rolled in a fight, hit or miss, with the move that fits the weapon: a shot for ranged weapons, a thrust for pointed ones, a swing for the rest, hands and claws, a bite; needs WoDX Combat 3.22.0 for misses and weapons; a hook other modules can call to show a power being worked; not deployed)
- 0.8.1 (fix: the same attack played twice in a row toward the same side did nothing the second time; found on the testbed; not deployed)
- 0.8.0 (sprite tokens attack: each of the six test figures has its own attack moves, six frames in every facing, a strike with the hand, a thrust, a shot, a bite, the Crinos claws, and a worked power with its effect; a blow that lands in a fight plays the attacker's move turned toward the one struck, on every screen; the four people redrawn from one description each so walk and attack match; not deployed)
- 0.7.1 (sprite tokens: a figure that stops while facing left or right stands with its feet together, not in mid-stride; the Lupus is a darker grey-brown so it reads as a wolf at night; not deployed)
- 0.7.0 (sprite tokens, a prototype for the testbed: a token given a sprite shows a whole drawn figure that turns the way it moves and walks as it goes; six figures to try, the Kindred elder, the Garou in Homid, Crinos and Lupus, a townsman and a lady of 1888; drawn by our own code, sharp at any zoom; the bust stays on sheets; given and taken off by the Storyteller through the module api for now; not deployed)
- 0.6.4 (dead code removed)
- 0.6.3 (thinner lips in a colour drawn toward the skin; faces at rest are neutral, smiles and frowns only by choice or mood; "Face drawn as" woman, man, nonbinary or not stated: women's lips a little fuller, men's thinner, nonbinary either way by seed, no facial hair rolled for women; WoDX Creation keeps the sex it rolled on the actor; not deployed)
- 0.6.2 (mouths redrawn in the face's own inked style: flat colour, tapered sepia parting and corners; a Lips part with 12 shapes plus a per-seed nudge so no two mouths match; lips shrink to fit narrow faces; moustaches lie over the mouth; not deployed, art style under review)
- 0.6.1 (the new grounds matched to kinds for NPCs: rose for ghouls, diaper for the faithful, lilies for sorcerers, vine for changelings and changing breeds, chequer for wraiths, punched gold for mummies)
- 0.6.0 (mouths drawn by WoDX itself and fitted under the nose and between the eyes, 15 expressions with shading and teeth, checked as their own stage; 16 fantastic skin tones rolled only for the fae and the uncanny; Mauled and worse drain the face to grey instead of a pale film; no paper grain over the figure; six more grounds: rose stars, diaper, vine, lilies, chequer, punched gold)
- 0.5.0 (lasting changes from WoDX Storyteller that show are drawn on the face: seams, brow ridges, eyes, mouth, skin, the whole face)
- 0.4.0 (fuller lips grown from each mouth's shape, 9 lip colours; lips, brows and nose coloured to stand out from the skin, a light rim under brows on the darkest skin; serious faces: cartoonish Lorelei eyes, brows, mouths and hair left out, saved looks moved to the nearest kept part; soft modelling on every face; each face built in six checked stages, head, features, skin, over the eye, clothes, ring; face outline measured on the bare face so long fringes no longer cut it short; health marks under the mask of visible skin)
- 0.3.0 (illuminated finish: gilt or starred ground, sepia ink, paper grain, woven cloth, gilt trim, a gilt ring with an inner ring by kind; 23 eye colours, 12 strange; 14 afflictions; background and inner ring chosen by the player, or matched to the character's kind)
- 0.2.0 (new faces: 38 skin tones, 25 hair colours, 15 face builds, 7 ages, 8 complexions, 12 marks, 12 scars, 13 kinds of facial hair, drawn under a mask of the visible skin; a placed token that still shows its old picture on the Storyteller's screen is prepared again and redrawn)
- 0.1.4 (eyes closed at Incapacitated; placed tokens updated with diff off and checked again after a moment)
- 0.1.3 (api.last: what the last bake found and updated)
