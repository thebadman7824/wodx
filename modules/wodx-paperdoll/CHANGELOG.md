# wodx-paperdoll changelog

Newest first. Moved from the module table in docs/MODULES.md on 12 November 2026; add each new release at the top.

- 0.24.1 (Portraits and talking faces read the one gender WoDX Chronicle keeps, so a gender written on the sheet or a nonbinary person from the People generator shows as such (B3500))
- 0.24.0 (Children on the map: a character under 18 walks as a child, the same figure drawn shorter with a larger head, in four heights (2 to 6, 7 to 11, 12 to 14, 15 to 17) worked out from the birth year, or from the age at the Embrace or at death. The figure menu has a "Grown or a child" pick and dresses a child the same way as anyone else; "Grown" is not offered under 18, and a child figure picked for an older character marks them a minor, so nothing romantic can involve them (B3480-B3483))
- 0.23.0 (Looks for monsters and for the fae on sprite tokens: thirty-one creatures of legend each drawn after what its own entry says of it, from the dragon and the gryphon to a hedge of blood roses, with those that go on two legs drawn as figures of their own size; a changeling is drawn in its fae mien by kith, and a wraith or a spirit pale, half seen and drifting instead of stepping; new moves: a block and a parry with a spark when the fight rules rolled that defence, a jump, a crawl, and stooping to work at a lock when a lock is picked; the roster carries each character's line and kith; the creatures named in the books, from a book check, are drawn by name in about 120 shared looks by body plan, a wisp, a swarm, a crawler, a flyer, a beast or a figure of their own size; a big beast or figure is set in the middle of its frame and drawn just small enough that no move cuts off a head, a tail or a tentacle (the horse among them), and the small gaps left inside a figure that falls are closed (B2613 to B2617))
- 0.22.0 (the figure menu shows the body, the clothes, the head and the face first; neck, hands, belt and what is carried sit under More, which opens by itself when one of them is worn or picked)
- 0.21.0 (More eyes, hair, heads and noses (B3052): 18 eye colours, 22 hair colours, a crown curve and a chin curve on wider head ranges, and nine noses. Patchwork and fur collars draw patches instead of a fine chequer, and rolling a face's features is fast again)
- 0.20.0 (more skin: 24 everyday tones where there were 8, from light to deep with warm, golden, olive, ruddy and cool undertones; one changeling in three has a fae mien skin of blue, green, lavender, silver, bark, rose, teal, gold, slate or coral, seen only with fae sight; anyone may have a rarer skin, such as albinism, vitiligo, blue-grey, jaundiced, ruddy, ashen, sunburnt or a wide birthmark; half of demons take one of 15 skins in the apocalyptic visage, from black glass and gold to sea green and starless night; not deployed)
- 0.19.0 (many more face features, so that two faces seldom look alike, first batch: features anyone may have go from 18 to 140, among them scars, birthmarks and moles, tattoos, earrings and other jewellery, monocles and masks, hats, helmets and crowns, braids, dyed and streaked hair, moustaches and side-whiskers, black eyes and clouded eyes, paint and powder, and collars; Vampire faces get 51 features any Kindred may have and 15 for each clan and bloodline, with 25 or more for Nosferatu, Gargoyles and Samedi, whose stone and rot always show; Werewolf faces get 50 any Garou may have, 15 for each tribe, auspice and breed and 25 for metis; the other Changing Breeds get 50 shared and 15 for each kind; Mage faces get 50 any mage may have and 15 for each Tradition, Convention and faction; Wraith faces get 50 any wraith may have and 15 for each guild and faction; Changeling faces get 50 any changeling may have and 15 for each kith and court; Hunter faces get 50 any hunter may have and 15 for each creed and order; Demon faces get 50 and 15 for each house and faction; Mummy faces 50 and 15 for each kind of mummy; Kuei-jin faces 50 and 15 for each Dharma; about 3,060 features in all; Nosferatu ears now come in nine kinds, from plain ears and stubs to torn, drooping and finned ears, bare holes and odd pairs, besides the bat's ears; stubble is drawn sparser and beards and goatees solid, so none reads as a chequered patch on the chin; a face with nothing ticked now rolls several features, never two on one spot; objects of one era such as sunglasses are only ticked; each line's own features follow; not deployed)
- 0.18.2 (the other faces brought in line with 0.18.1: pointed ears, for Gangrel, Sidhe and Clurichaun, now grow from the sides of the head at eye height and join its outline, drawn over the hair; a hood or a veil hides every kind of ear, so none shows through the cloth; horns, for metis, trolls, Gargoyles, demon visages and the Kuei-jin demon shintai, break through the brow from a swelling of skin that parts the hair; not deployed)
- 0.18.1 (faces, from Ben's notes: a hood or a veil and wimple now covers the throat, and grave linen wraps it; a Nosferatu's great ears grow from the sides of the head with a hollow and the opening at the root, joined to the head by one outline, no longer seeming to grow from behind it; every chin stands out more, the jaw holding its width longer and the chin lit, with a shadow under the lower lip; not deployed)
- 0.18.0 (the pixel faces are the faces for good: the older drawn portrait faces are retired, their Paperdoll window, menu and picture baking off and nothing deleted, while the Figure builder for sprite tokens stays; the Storyteller can make any actor's pixel face its portrait from the Actors menu; in the Net a character jacked in shows its face as a persona on a screen, the ASCII portraits redrawn in the pixel style: one phosphor colour by chance or chosen, scanlines and a rain of data, on talk cards and on anything it says while jacked in; not deployed)
- 0.17.1 (fix: a talking face now moves when its chat line is drawn again, as when the card updates just after it is posted or a pop-up notice shows it too; before, the face drawn last stayed still while the first, no longer on screen, played; found on the testbed; not deployed)
- 0.17.0 (more on the talking faces: 110 features a face may show, from the books of each line and a few of our own: clan marks such as Gangrel ears and the Salubri third eye, Nosferatu growths, metis deformities, deathmarks by cause of death, each kith's mien, demon visages, Kuei-jin Yin and Yang looks, and scars, paint, jewellery and headwear for anyone; the Storyteller ticks them from the Actors menu, and NPCs with none ticked roll them from their clan, kith, tribe or house; form and power features show only while that form or state is on; not deployed)
- 0.16.0 (talking faces: when an NPC speaks to a character, its line in chat carries its face, drawn on a 64 pixel grid in the browser; the face blinks, its mouth follows the words, and it reacts to the character (a social roll that swayed it, frenzy, its mood, its attitude, pain) before it settles; Kindred fangs only when the Storyteller turns them on; a changeling's mien and a hunter's sigil only for those who can see them; each player picks Moving, Still or Off; not deployed)
- 0.14.1 (a seated figure is plainly down on the ground, legs out in front or knees apart, where before it only looked a little shorter; of several things in hand the batch draws the largest, and a torch before a knife; found on the testbed; not deployed)
- 0.14.0 (more moves for sprite tokens: a figure sits or kneels and stays so until it stands up or walks off, looks about, speaks under a small speech mark when its character says a line, leans in to feed, and blurs when it changes form; it climbs, swims, flies and rides with a sheet of its own for each; a button on the token for whoever may move it; a torch is carried lit; first versions made from the walk; not deployed)
- 0.13.3 (fix: a figure whose health track is filled by hand, on the sheet or by a macro, now falls and stays down too, not only one harmed from a damage card; found on the testbed; not deployed)
- 0.13.2 (fix: the Figure button on a token's sheet now opens the character's own figure, so a kept figure reaches the batch; found on the testbed; not deployed)
- 0.13.1 (the batch draws each character as itself: only armour and gear count as worn, teeth and claws are not carried, a thing the Library does not list is drawn as the nearest thing it does, a character with no portrait look gets the skin and hair its portrait would start from, one with nothing on is dressed plainly for the chronicle's year, the weapon is carried in the walk and its move comes first; found on the testbed; not deployed)
- 0.13.0 (sprite tokens react: a figure that is harmed takes the blow, one put out of the fight falls and stays down on the map until it is healed or helped up, and one that dies plays its death, a Cainite crumbling to ash at Final Death; a missed attack is dodged; figures lean into a run over a long stretch and go low when they crawl; every sprite has these sheets, made from its own walk; not deployed)
- 0.12.0 (the figure menu: a Figure button on a character's sheet opens a window where the player puts the whole walking figure together, skin, hair and how it is worn, and a piece for each of twelve places from the 458 wardrobe pieces the rig draws, with a search box, the pieces of the chronicle's year first, and a dye for any piece; a quick picture shows the figure as it is built, and the walking sprite is drawn from the kept choice with the next batch; not deployed)
- 0.11.0 (a sprite for every character, drawn in batches outside the game: the game writes a roster of its characters with their skin and hair, what they wear and what they hold; tools/sprites/batch.py draws walk, still and attack sheets for each, people from their worn things and animals from their names; the game learns the sheets from the batch's manifest, offers a token its own character's sprite, and can dress a whole scene; not deployed)
- 0.10.1 (shadow effects have a pale violet rim so they can be seen on a night map; found on the testbed; not deployed)
- 0.10.0 (a power's effect fits the power: 84 effect sheets, twelve colours by seven shapes, laid over the figure's casting move; the look is read from the power's name and group, then its kind, then the character's line, so Dominate shows a violet gaze, Obtenebration rising shadow, a Gift pale green sparks, a wraith's Arcanos grey tendrils; the elder's casting sheet no longer carries a fixed blood bolt; not deployed)
- 0.9.1 (sprite tokens play their casting move when a power is rolled from the Actions menu; attacks and powers rolled on a player's own computer now play too, sent by the computer that rolled them; not deployed)
- 0.9.0 (sprite tokens play every attack rolled in a fight, hit or miss, with the move that fits the weapon: a shot for ranged weapons, a thrust for pointed ones, a swing for the rest, hands and claws, a bite; needs WoDX Combat 3.22.0 for misses and weapons; a hook other modules can call to show a power being worked; not deployed)
