# wodx-npc changelog

Newest first. Moved from the module table in docs/MODULES.md on 12 November 2026; add each new release at the top.

- 0.76.0 (NPC memory and grudges (B3346-B3349): every NPC keeps a short list of what each character did to them, taken from attitude changes and trust events or written by the Storyteller (api.memory.remember). How fast a wrong or a kindness fades follows the NPC's Nature: some never forget. Once a season an NPC with a strong grudge and the means picks one move (call in a boon, spread an unkind tale, spoil a venture, tip off the hunters, turn on someone dear, do harm), which waits in the approval queue; approving starts the rumour or adds hunter pressure and fires wodxGrudgeMove. A death recorded on the sheet passes strong grudges to the heirs at half weight. Players see a short word for each NPC who remembers them in the new Who remembers you window. World setting "NPCs remember and bear grudges"; Tales and first meetings (B3350, B3352): a deed recorded with api.standing.deed for a player character starts a tale in WoDX Storyteller's rumour mill, and an NPC's first meeting with a character moves by one step for the tales about them that NPC heard ("a tale they heard" in the reasons))
- 0.75.1 (Off-screen lives: a vampire sheet whose Path or Humanity was never filled in (0) is taken as 7 rather than 0, so it no longer sleeps a thousand years; api.fates.onSkipEnd lets a gate test the skip handler without waking every module's jump work (B3267))
- 0.75.0 (Cults that gather around characters (B3240-B3246): words spoken in character where NPCs can hear, sermons, powers seen, breaches witnessed, willing feedings and social wins build awe in a place, with themes that become the cult's creed. Awe of 10 heard by 3 people puts "A following gathers" in the approval queue (the founder can ask from 5). Each line has its own kind: blood cults (as Herd, V20 p. 114), the Netaru for mummies, a Devil's cult of personality (Demon pp. 155, 251), Dreamers (C20 p. 169), circles of remembrance (Memoriam, Wraith 20th p. 144), mage Cults (M20 p. 310), the old faith and Wyrm cults, Kuei-jin blood cults, hunter flocks and mortal sects, with words by era. A weekly turn grows, wears down, exposes or splits each cult; NPC cult-makers (Setites, Devils, Ecstatics, the Reborn, preachers) may found their own once a month. New window Your following (token tools). World setting cultsOn, off by default)
- 0.74.0 (Off-screen lives of supernatural NPCs: when the clock jumps a year or more, every supernatural NPC lives through the gap. Vampires Embrace childer, fall into torpor for the years their Humanity or Path sets and wake again, commit diablerie (lowering Generation only on a victim of lower Generation) and meet Final Death; Garou rise in Rank and fall in battle; mages reach higher Arete and take apprentices; changelings may forget themselves to Banality; wraiths may fall to Oblivion or Transcend; mummies die and rise again; hunters burn out young. One approval card shows everything; approving it writes torpor, waking, deaths (filed in WoDX · The Dead), Generation, Rank and Arete, and keeps the new childer, cubs, apprentices and fosterlings as records the Storyteller can bring into play (api.fates.bring). Player characters are left to Pass the Years; an NPC flagged fates.keep is left alone. Setting off by default (B3260-B3266))
- 0.73.0 (Factions pay when a town rises (B3330): when WoDX Storyteller's towns report a rising, each mortal faction that holds a place in the town loses a dot of Status and one of Retainers, a faction that laid a tax on its market loses a dot of Status and the holders think worse of it; a sack costs each holder a dot of Resources. Hidden factions keep their dots. The changes wait in the approval queue)
- 0.72.0 (Talk and crowds follow the town (B3320, B3321): overheard talk has a new topic, the town, read from WoDX Storyteller's towns: people talk of the plague, the fire, the flood, the storm, the earthquake, the famine, the riot, the rising or the sack in the year it struck and the one after, else of an angry, grim or thriving town; passers-by may talk of it too. The passers-by crowd thins with plague, famine, anger and ruin and fills in good times)
- 0.71.0 (Children as characters (B3223-B3228): a newborn is a child sheet, marked so the age floor always holds, with Attributes, Abilities and Willpower held to caps by age. A skip of whole years brings one approval card per child: a lifepath scene from the childhood or teen years (never one about love or marriage), schooling or work by century, region and way of life, a Nature from 13, and coming of age at 18, when the mark comes off. Players may not own or make a character under 18 unless the world setting "Players may play children" is on (off by default). Player-asked marriage (B3229-B3231): propose from a lover or friendly tie; an NPC answers by warmth (Devoted yes, Fond rolls Charisma + Empathy), a player answers on a card; the courting custom names who must agree, a rank gap draws an objection and a feud between the families blocks the match; the banns, contract or civil notice run on the clock; then the wedding card goes to the Storyteller, and approving it marries them. Hooks wodxGrownUp and wodxProposal; Pregnancy and birth (B3220-B3222): the Storyteller starts a pregnancy between two adults (or approves a player's ask in the approval queue), the clock counts about nine months, and the birth comes by itself as a roll of Stamina plus the attendant's Medicine against a difficulty by century, from 8 before 1700 to 4 in a modern hospital. A failure is a hard birth, a botch a loss told gently to the Storyteller only, and a safe birth has no roll. Rest after the birth follows the custom of the time and place. Ghouls, mages, Garou, changelings, Kinfolk and hunters add story hooks to the Storyteller's card, never rules. api.family and the hooks wodxExpecting and wodxBirth)
