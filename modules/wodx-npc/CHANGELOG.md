# wodx-npc changelog

Newest first. Moved from the module table in docs/MODULES.md on 12 November 2026; add each new release at the top.

- 0.41.0 (one Followers window per character: every ghoul, retainer, ally and herd member with where they are, their order or errand, loyalty and its last change, the blood bond and any trouble, with the everyday orders as buttons; a description of a culprit that witnesses saw spreads by the means of the year, from the hue and cry to face matching, and fades; NPCs who have heard it may know the culprit again, on a Storyteller card)
- 0.40.3 (the Inquisition's politics follow the book check: a cell with a rector set over it and officers it elects, by order and Rank; a Council of Faith to pick by hand)
- 0.40.2 (passers-by standing still for a fight still remember being fed on, hurt or Ravaged)
- 0.40.1 (the passers-by street can no longer stall: a tick that never finished is given up after 30 seconds, each tick stops after 20, errors are kept for the Storyteller's console, and api.passersby.state() tells a gate whether a tick is running)
- 0.40.0 (the crowd as a resource: each passer-by feels something and keeps a spark of dreaming, by the kind of place, the hour, the kind of person and the age; a Ravaged or inspired passer-by stays on the map; on a large map passers-by come and go by regions the Storyteller ticks in the Passers-by window or names "Passers-by")
- 0.39.0 (an ended battle between factions proposes Status, Retainers and standing changes (wodxBattle))
- 0.38.0 (followers: an NPC serves its domitor, the character whose background made it and its patron as well as anyone the Storyteller marks; WoDX spots hard orders and suggests the refusal roll; Keep watch warns the master and Guard challenges strangers; in a fight a follower minds its orders: Attack the one I point at, Hold back, Guard and Wait, and guards every character it serves; followers on errands: buy, carry, find, ask around, bring someone to feed on, watch a rival, worked as long tasks; help on a master's long task; deal with a witness; join a chase with the master or cut the quarry off; a follower's breach is traced to its master; loyalty drifts with vitae, neglect, feeding and hard orders)
- 0.37.0 (relationships from every tie WoDX keeps, read from both ends; the family and lineage tree; secrets found by Investigation, Auspex, spies or a clue, each find a hook in the Ledger and a charge with proof in the faction politics turn; why an NPC feels as they do on talk cards and NPC sheets, for the Storyteller; talk lines that need a trait go first and say what unlocked them)
- 0.36.0 (missed connections, 12 November 2026: reaction cards read the sound of a fight or a chase; a fight counts as a weapon in the reaction rules)
- 0.35.0 (a fed-on NPC remembers a swoon and a lost hour, or who fed if it fought, is Kindred or a ghoul, or already knows the Kindred; api.nameFor(actor, viewer) is the one lookup for what a viewer calls an NPC; api.knowledgeOf(actor) lists who an NPC has seen for what and who fed on it)
- 0.34.0 (the stranger line: with Faces on, an unmet NPC's nameplate shows how they are dressed, a side they wear, blood or a drawn weapon, what they are doing and how they seem toward you; the Storyteller's own text still wins)
- 0.33.0 (NPCs know what a character is only when it shows, a sense shows it, or they saw it: hunters go for the monster first only once they can tell, and mortals' remarks on a character's oddness need a Perception + Alertness roll; with "Faces you have met" on, chat and the combat tracker show players the stranger's words for NPCs they have not met)
- 0.32.0 (first meetings read the look: dress against the NPC's station by era, dress out of its time, blood, Appearance, Status, fine work; blood sours a later meeting for the scene; guards mind blood and let a worn livery pass; worn habits and uniforms show their side; stock lines on dress and a disguise seen through)
- 0.29.0 (players see the politics of their own factions: who holds each seat, the next gathering, the law, and their own characters' standing, seats, favour, claims and charges)
- 0.28.0 (politics for mages, on the Traditions' side and Reason's, hunters by era from the Inquisition to the imbued, the Shemsu-heru, the Fallen and Kuei-jin courts)
- 0.27.1 (talk cards add what a deranged Malkavian hears, from WoDX Chronicle)
- 0.26.0 (politics for Garou septs, fae courts by era and Wraith necropolises; challenges with a player's character staged as fights through WoDX Combat; Garou rise in Rank by challenge at the moot; a Great Maelstrom empties necropolis seats)
- 0.25.0 (politics inside factions, off by default: a Dark Ages court, a Camarilla court, a Sabbat diocese or an Anarch barony with seats, standing, charges, favours and a gathering each few faction turns; the Vaulderie and Vinculum; boons kept or broken in the Ledger move the debtor's standing)
- 0.24.1 (animal blood follows the V20 animals appendix; cattle, sheep, goats and geese by house rule)
- 0.24.0 (supernatural NPCs by clan, tribe, kith, Tradition and creed: 29 kinds, each where and when it belongs; Orpheus crews only in the Future Darkness, Kuei-jin only in Asia; a Marauder's madness picks its moves)
- 0.23.0 (criminals, combatants and townsfolk: 39 kinds of mortal by era, each with its hours; morale that breaks when a leader falls; cover, formation, surrender and hostages; those who tell someone later what they saw)
- 0.21.1 (passers-by animals are read by their kind)
- 0.21.0 (animals with their own ways: 16 kinds, from stray and guard dogs to wolves, bears and bats, each with its places, years, blood and hours by day, night and season; herds that run together, guard dogs and wolves that hold their own patch, hungry beasts bolder in the lean months; animals shy from wraiths, not from Kindred)
