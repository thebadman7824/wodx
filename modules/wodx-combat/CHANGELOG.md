- 3.36.0 (Fighting in and around vehicles (B4791 to B4796), from Mage: The Ascension 20th Anniversary pp. 458-463. Those inside a closed vehicle (a coach, a car, a lorry) are protected by it unless the attack scores 4 or more successes, which finds a window; inside an armoured vehicle or an aircraft every blow from outside goes into the vehicle. Damage against a vehicle: up to its Durability bounces off, the rest comes off its Structure, and at its Structure it is wrecked, all written on its tile. At the wheel of a land vehicle the attack menu offers "Ram with the ...": Dexterity + Drive (Ride for a cart) held to the vehicle's Maneuver, at 6, for its Durability + a die for every 10 mph covered this turn + its mass (cars +1, lorries +3, military +5) in bashing. Ramming another vehicle strikes its body, and everyone inside both takes the crash less the Durability (and a lorry's +3), halved when strapped in (aircraft always, cars and lorries from 1970). The one at the wheel swerves in place of dodging (Dexterity + the driving Ability held to the Maneuver, +2 against shots); anyone else sat inside a vehicle cannot dodge. A hit with 3 successes to hit and 6 levels of damage on a motor vehicle sets off the fuel: 12 dice of aggravated fire to all aboard, the vehicle burnt out, and a fire hazard on the map over it. quickAttack takes a weaponId. World setting "Fighting in and around vehicles", on by default)
- 3.35.0 (Fighting from the saddle (B4770-B4776), the rules the riding book check left unbuilt. A blow from the saddle may use the mount's Strength and Dexterity when higher (V20 Dark Ages p. 399; a riding horse is the book's large horse, a mule the small one). The mount takes one defensive action a turn for its rider at no cost to the rider: its Dexterity + Athletics, the Athletics held to the rider's Ride, offered in the attack window and chosen by the automatic defense when it is nearly as good as the rider's own. A rider struck rolls Stamina + Ride (Drive on a motorcycle) at the damage successes, 3 at least; thrown, the token is taken off the mount, set down beside it and Knocked down, and takes 2 to 4 dice by the ground, lethal on a botch (Dark Ages: Vampire pp. 244-245, Dark Ages Companion p. 38). The lance needs Ride 3 and Melee 3, or a hit jars the user with bashing dice equal to the damage successes; a botch is 4 lethal dice to the user and a saddle roll; a lance charge that hits makes the target roll Strength + Athletics at 6 or go down, and a rider is unhorsed. A bow shot from a moving horse is +2, +4 at a trot or faster (half the mount's pace or more this turn), in place of the saddle's +1, and nothing with a Mounted specialty (Dark Ages: Vampire p. 244). World setting "Fighting from the saddle", on by default)
- 3.34.0 (Cover on shots (B4671): a shot or a throw reads WoDX Interactables' cover between the two tokens (a table, a counter, a barricade, a low wall, a table tipped over) and adds it to the difficulty, by the V20 cover table (pdf p. 286), with a line on the card)

Shields and parrying weapons work as V20 Dark Ages p. 350 and Sorcerers Crusade p. 202 give them: a carried shield sets the parry difficulty (Small Shield 4) and adds to the attacker's difficulty (Standard +1, Buckler +1, War Shield +2); a dagger parries at 5 and a sword adds +1 against close attacks.
