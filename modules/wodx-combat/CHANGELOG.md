# wodx-combat changelog

Newest first. Moved from the module table in docs/MODULES.md on 12 November 2026; add each new release at the top.

- 3.25.0 (a Crippled arm, hand or leg adds +2 difficulty to attacks made with it; wounds that add up to three lethal or aggravated levels on one part get the maiming offer once; the Crippled effect comes off when the part heals. B2902, B2903)
- 3.24.1 (the attack window no longer says "Black powder:" twice in front of each old gun; testbed run 2026-11-13: gatehuntpowder 8/8)
- 3.24.0 (black powder: hand cannon, matchlock, wheellock and flintlock guns misfire on a botch, and on any 1 in rain, sleet or hail on an outdoor scene or when swimming; heavy rain puts out a match, and a misfire in heavy rain wets the powder until the gun is reloaded; powder kept covered counts as dry; a gun that names no lock takes the lock most guns had that year; percussion caps and cartridges keep the plain botch rule; the attack window lists each old gun and its risk, the automatic attack skips a gun that cannot fire, and a new macro lights the match again; world setting Black powder misfires and wet powder, on; not deployed)
- 3.23.0 (where wounds are: each attack that lands notes the part it hit on the character, as the health track heals the oldest notes of each kind go, and an Undo takes them back; the Storyteller can place a level on a part or clear a part from the damage doll; a Mokole in Suchid or Archid form has a body again, and four-legged shapes know their animal for the doll)
- 3.22.1 (the word that harm was applied also says what it left the character as, out of the fight, dead or at Final Death, so the map can show a fall or a death; not deployed)
- 3.22.0 (every attack rolled, hit or miss, tells other modules who struck at whom and with what kind of weapon, so sprite tokens can play the blow; nothing changes in the fight itself)
- 3.21.0 (fights end by themselves: when one side has nobody left standing for a full turn, Suggest gives you a card with End the fight and Auto ends it)
- 3.20.1 (after the book check: in the saddle no Ability counts for more dots than the rider has in Ride, V20 Dark Ages p. 399, and the attack line says so; the mounted charge's page is 399)
- 3.20.0 (the last of the ways of moving: at the reins of a horse, cart, car or boat the mount's own pace is the limit in a fight, half with no dot in Ride or Drive, and a passenger is carried without spending a move; In the saddle and Aboard a vehicle are stances with their own changes to rolls; a rider's charge adds a damage die for each 10 yards ridden this turn, five at most; worn armor and a burden set from the token HUD slow a swimmer by quarters and take them under at four steps)
- 3.19.0 (the last VtDA20 pieces are gone: the manifest no longer lists vtda20-combat as a conflict, the actor type no longer falls back to the old vtda20 flags, and the dialog class is wodx-form-dialog)
- 3.18.0 (Game time: when a fight ends the clock moves by its turns times the seconds a turn takes (world setting Seconds a turn takes, 3; 0 keeps fights timeless), or the fighters' own time when they are apart; the after-action card names the length)
- 3.16.0 (missed connections, 12 November 2026: fights make noise: firearms and explosives loud, blades, fists, bows and silenced guns quiet; NPCs nearby who hear it react through WoDX Interactables, once a round)
- 3.15.1 (the after-action card names each fighter and their numbers: the start of the fight is stored so the actor's id is not split; it is written only when a fighter is new)
- 3.15.0 (the VtDA20 bridge is gone: no game.vtda20combat alias, no settings read from vtda20-combat, and the module no longer switches itself off beside vtda20-combat; the manifest still lists vtda20-combat as a conflict)
- 3.14.0 (the after-action card for the Storyteller when a fight ends, with a breach button for the Masquerade ledger and the rumour mill; Use the room on the token HUD in a fight)
- 3.13.0 (the body plan: a tree of parts for each shape, from a person to a wolf, bat, mist or Zulo shape; claws, teeth, moves and senses from the parts; hit locations with aiming at a part and a maiming the Storyteller may rule; gear that comes off in a shape that cannot use it, with Undo)
- 3.12.1 (breath, swimming and flight speeds from the books; prone is light cover; unlinked tokens are found)
- 3.12.0 (ways of moving: Fly, Swim, Climb, Crawl, Jump, Into the ground and Through walls, each with its speed and its changes to rolls; deep and shallow water with breath and drowning; a fall when flight ends in the air; height counts in range and reach)
- 3.11.1 (the ambush roll counts Stealth and Alertness dots; it threw before and the attack never happened)
- 3.11.0 (the ambush: an attack from hiding rolls Stealth against Alertness first, V20 p. 274 or V20 Dark Ages p. 345)
- 3.10.0 (fights between NPC rivals)
- 3.9.1 (fight cards accepted or undone through the api)
- 3.9.0 (fights that start by themselves: one starter for sight, alarm, attitude, Reaction regions, frenzy, attacks outside a fight and door or region outcomes)
- 6a, 6b, automation, gridless, the Corpus track, the turn conductor, 3.7.5 (the attack opens on WoDX Targeting's choice)
- 3.8.0 (house rule: count shots and reloads)
- 3.8.1 (players move only on their own turn, setting "Moves out of turn")
- 3.8.2 (a caller can ask that a botched soak turns aggravated)
- 3.8.3 (a Dark Ages fae soaks lethal in the mien and soaks cold iron)
- 3.8.4 (demons and mummies soak by their own books; bites without fangs are bashing; Vicissitude talons from Bonecraft; Celerity off Initiative)
- 3.8.5 (dead code removed)
- 3.8.7 (an attack card with only an automatic defense note no longer throws)
