# wodx-interactables changelog

Newest first. Moved from the module table in docs/MODULES.md on 12 November 2026; add each new release at the top.

- 0.54.1 (Two rules follow the era profile's switches: a ringed target is an archery butt where the firearms switch says no guns, and a clinic draws cyberware where the cyberware switch says so (and never where it says no), with the year and the gear's era list as the fallback (B6668))
- 0.54.0 (Houses of worship are holy ground: a region an adopted town map tags as a church, mosque, synagogue or temple counts as an altar of rating 1 (or its own), kept for the faith of the place, and an altar tile takes that faith too; holyUnder gives the faith with the ground, and a demon who starts a turn in any of them is seared (B6362))
- 0.53.1 (Shared terms (B6180-B6199): time units, dice counts, health levels, distance, eras, lines and player characters come from WoDX Chronicle's shared terms instead of a copy of this module's own. An attempt that takes turns moves the clock by the table's turn length (WoDX Combat's house rule))
- 0.53.0 (Loose gear and real weights (B6083 to B6089): a chest weighs what is in it, a fallen knight weighs their mail, Set gear down here (Carry and drag menu) leaves things on the ground as a heap on the map, picked up from the Interact menu or hauled like a sack)
- 0.52.0 (Snow and rail (B6070 to B6072): sleds and sleighs, snow machines, trains and trams as mounts and vehicles, with the snow and railway grounds for chases and fights)
- 0.51.1 (B5089: the yielded are captives too)
- 0.51.0 (Pictures for each state from a map's sidecar (B5733): a feature or opening may carry `art` and `artRect`; adopting lays a tile with the shut picture over each container and a tile that follows each door and window's wall, so the map shows what is open and what is shut)
- 0.50.0 (Carrying the fallen and moving large things (B5070-B5086, Ben's Carry Bodies Desk): anyone next to a character who is down carries them (under the VtDA20 lifting chart) or drags them (over it), alone or together; furniture, carts and stores are pushed, pulled or hauled by the same chart, with a roll each turn up to two steps over it and help from others past that. Wheels halve the load. A carried character who wakes can struggle free. A thing left in a doorway blocks it and is good cover. Setting "Carrying the fallen and moving large things"; tile settings Weight, On wheels and Fixed in place)
- 0.49.1 (Ghouled animals: a ridden beast's Potence counts toward its Strength, next to its Celerity, which already counted)
- 0.49.0 (Bicycles as a tile, and mounts out of the books: ghouled horses, fleshcrafted steeds, chimerical steeds, Stygian steeds and gryphons (B4960-B4964); Riding the animal itself: a "mountable" tag on creatures, guessed from the name, set in the token's config (B4966, B4967); Vehicles get their own sheet: a module-defined kind of actor, ridden and driven as a token (B4968, B4969))
- 0.48.0 (A player character fleeing a fight starts a chase on its own, as an NPC's flight already did; holy ground is readable for other modules (B5170, B5176))
- 0.47.0 (Calls that reach someone (B4883); Chases and restocking on their own (B4887, B4888); A botched look at a clue misleads (B5016); Fire calls up the Red Fear (B5010) and a look shows who is there (B4889))
- 0.46.0 (Vehicles for a fight (B4790, B4791). New kinds of mount or vehicle on a tile: a lorry or truck (from 1900), an armoured car or carrier (1914), a tank (1916), a balloon or airship (1783), a propeller aeroplane (1909), a jet (1944) and a helicopter (1945). Each runs in a chase with a speed and Maneuver by year (Mage 20th pp. 461-463 for the modern rows, house figures before) and has a body: Durability, Structure, mass, and whether it shelters those inside (Mage 20th pp. 458-463). Aircraft fly over any ground but indoors and take no ground difficulty; tanks go on rough ground at 20 yards a turn, lorries cannot. Pilot is read from the sheet. The book's minimum skills: a lorry needs Drive 3 or the start is a roll; a military vehicle needs Drive 3 and an aircraft Pilot, or there is no option to drive it at all (anyone may ride along). Bring forward turns a tank into an armoured car, a car or a carriage, a jet or helicopter into an aeroplane, an aeroplane into a balloon, a lorry into a cart. Damage is kept on the tile (flags hull: Structure lost); a wrecked vehicle offers only Get out, and its driver who moves climbs out while it stays. The tile config gains "Damage taken" to set or mend it)
- 0.45.0 (Cover on the map (B4671-B4673): a tile can be cover (a table, a counter or a bar, a barricade, a low wall), set in the tile's window or by a made map's file on Adopt. Shots at someone within a square behind it, with the line of the shot crossing it, are at the V20 cover difficulty (light +1, good +2, superior +3; pdf p. 286), and shots from behind it one less. A table is light cover and can be tipped over in a turn, loudly, for good cover, and set on its legs again. The api gives coverOnShot(shooter, target) for WoDX Combat)
- 0.44.0 (Tack in the chase (B3907): each rider's roll reads WoDX Library's road kit)
- 0.43.0 (Investigation (B3364-B3368): the case board gains theories. Anyone writes one in plain words (who, what, where, why) and picks the lines on the board it rests on; a character tests it with Perception or Intelligence + Investigation at 7 (V20 pdf 272), and the board says how much holds up, never which part: one success says whether any of it does, two roughly how much, three the number of lines, five also how many true links it leaves out. The Storyteller marks which lines are true in a window of their own (What is true); players' boards never show it. Deeds leave evidence at the scene, whoever does them: feeding leaves blood and a witness, a lethal or worse wound blood and traces, a rival's probe of a haven tracks, a witness or ash, harm done on a grudge blood and a witness. Evidence fades at its own pace (tracks in two days, blood in a week, a witness in a month, paper in a year; four times as long indoors) and rain or snow washes what lies outdoors. Search here for traces rolls Perception + Investigation; each piece is found at 7, harder as it fades, and goes on the open board. What a find tells follows the year: prints name someone from 1892, blood is typed from 1901, a camera's tape exists from 1970, DNA matches from 1986. The Storyteller's Evidence here window lists what is left and can leave more by hand. New setting "Deeds leave evidence". api.caseBoard.inquiry)
- 0.42.1 (Work done at a door, a lock or a device is announced to other modules with the token and the kind of work, so the map can show it; nothing else changes (B2616))
- 0.42.0 (Houses of worship by the faith of the place (B3200-B3201). Adopting a generated town now tags its chapel for the faith the scene keeps (the scene's own, or its region's in the chronicle's year): a mosque in Cairo, a temple in Lhasa or Kyoto, a synagogue for a Jewish quarter, a church at home. The town generator can also set the faith itself with --faith)
