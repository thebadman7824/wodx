# wodx-interactables changelog

Newest first. Moved from the module table in docs/MODULES.md on 12 November 2026; add each new release at the top.

- 0.40.0 (Fill by place and era draws gear that is at home where the scene stands, by WoDX Library 0.21.0's gear by region (B3032); 0.39.0 is taken by the Player UI branch)
- 0.38.0 (a living tile can also block walls, lock doors or light lamps inside its square while its rule holds: stalls that close a lane by day, the church shut on a fast day, lanterns on a night of revel. B2904)
- 0.37.0 (Bring forward: SWAPS and bringForward in the rules say what a map thing turns into in another year, in every era, for WoDX Sheets' anachronism card: a motor car before 1900 a carriage, an electric light before 1880 a lamp, a radio a telephone or a bell, a keypad before 1970 a lock of its grade, a carriage after 1930 a motor car; sprinklers and live wires have nothing like them. B2720)
- 0.36.0 (Fill by place and era reads where the scene stands: its region and that region's way of life in the chronicle's year, from WoDX Chronicle, so everyday goods are those at home there; the coin of the place is named in the local money from WoDX Storyteller ("7 dirhams"), and lands that trade without coin get none. Draws by era alone, as before, when Chronicle is off. B2682)
- 0.35.3 (mosques, synagogues and temples, WoDX NPC 0.50.0's new kinds of place, fill their containers as a church does and are kept when a map is adopted. B2662)
- 0.35.2 (adopting a generated map: a building that holds several households, such as a block of flats, keeps all of them on its region instead of only the last; a map that is part of a settlement says so in the plan, with where its record goes. B2526)
- 0.35.1 (the seven prologue eras count as early eras for locks and light, as the two old ones did)
- 0.35.0 (riding outside a chase: rideOf tells WoDX Combat which mount or vehicle a token is on, at the reins or as a passenger, with its pace this year on the scene's ground; a carried passenger's move is not counted against their own)
- 0.34.0 (things on a map know their years: datedParts in the rules gives WoDX Sheets' anachronism check each wall's and tile's dated parts and the lines inside containers; a thing the Storyteller kept out of its time works in any year, so a kept motor car can be driven in 1850 and a kept keypad worked)
- 0.33.0 (living maps: a tile can wait for "a caravan in town", so wagons in the square or lorries at the depot show only while a caravan of WoDX Storyteller's markets stands in that scene)
- 0.32.1 (case board cards keep one size, so a card with a long note no longer covers the buttons of the card below it)
- 0.32.0 (the case board: a shared board of clues, people, places, Encyclopaedia entries and notes joined by lines, in the era's look; found clues go on the open case by themselves; the Storyteller can hide a card until it is earned)
- 0.31.0 (a token can join a running chase as a pursuer, level with the slowest: followers with their master)
- 0.30.0 (Game time: an attempt done apart from the group goes on the character's own time, with Undo)
- 0.28.0 (missed connections, 12 November 2026: noiseAt() for a noise with no object (a fight, a chase); a chase is heard each turn (hooves and motors loud, feet quiet); a Hard feelings button after a chase moves NPC attitudes; living tiles resync when a dated event is taken back)
- 0.27.0 (use the room in a fight: knock a lamp or candle over to start a fire, bring a chandelier down, tear a curtain or throw a window open to let the sun in by day, set off the sprinklers from 1874)
- 0.26.1 (chases follow the book check: keeping sight by Perception against the lead, safe and top speeds and Maneuver for cars and motorcycles, crashes rolled as falls, Garou forms run faster, a rider charges in)
- 0.26.0 (chases and pursuit for the Storyteller: on foot, mounted or in the vehicles of the year, over the ground the chase crosses, with Undo and a fight on a catch)
- 0.25.0 (Can't Cross Running Water, Garlic, Crosses, Light-Sensitive and a Phobia go off when a character walks into a tile or region named for them)
