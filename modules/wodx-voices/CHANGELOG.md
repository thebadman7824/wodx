# wodx-voices changelog

Newest first. Add each new release at the top.

- 0.2.0 (2026-11-12): Kokoro. The active Storyteller's browser renders each line once with Kokoro (offline, from Data/wodx-voices-engine, filled by tools/voices/fetch-engine.mjs), saves it to Data/wodx-voices-cache, and the people who see the line play the file, from the NPC's token when its scene is in view (fading with distance, muffled by walls). Each NPC's Kokoro voice is a steady blend of two voices of one accent. The browser's own speech is the fallback (no engine, a failed line, no Storyteller online, an NPC given another tongue). The Storyteller's own lines in character as an NPC are read too (Improvement Desk vo1). An NPC can be given a tongue (cs, de, la) for the browser's speech. Settings: engine, device, Storyteller's lines, sound from the token, range, folders, cache warning, Start a fresh cache. Decisions B2106-B2110.
- 0.1.0 (2026-11-12): NPCs speak WoDX NPC talk cards aloud with the browser's own speech. A steady voice per NPC from its seed, known sex, age and speaking style; the Storyteller's choice in flags wins. Whispered lines are heard only by their recipients. World setting Voices (off by default), Storyteller hears every line; per-player volume and mute. Barks and cries stay silent. Decisions B2100-B2105.
