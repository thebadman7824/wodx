# wodx-solo changelog

Newest first.

- 0.5.0 (Add experience for the lone player (B4806). The Storyteller tools on the solo panel gain an Add experience button: the player names the points (a whole number from 1 to 100) and a reason, and the Storyteller's seat gives the award through WoDX Creation, so it lands on the sheet's experience list and log like any Storyteller award and can be spent at once. It has the same limits as the other tools: solo mode on, outside the Live world, nobody else playing)
- 0.4.0 (Storyteller tools for the lone player (B4800 to B4805). A switch under More on the solo panel shows Storyteller options, so the one person playing can check that everything works as meant and give their character items to test with. The player stays a player in Foundry: each tool is an ask the hidden Storyteller's seat answers. The Storyteller's cards shows every card, roll and whisper meant for the Storyteller alone as it arrives, with its buttons pressed by the seat when picked; Give my character an item searches the world's items and every Item compendium and makes the pick on the character's sheet; Check the systems lists the WoDX parts running and their versions, any setting the switch made that has changed since, the approval queue, cards left waiting and errors the seat has seen; What is hidden here lists the hidden tokens on the player's scene. The tools go on only while The Lone Chronicle is on, outside the Live world, for a player, with nobody else logged in, and go off by themselves when another player logs in or the mode is turned off. A Storyteller can take the switch off the panel with the new setting Offer Storyteller tools to the lone player)
- 0.3.0 (13 November 2026: the panel has one main button, What happens next?, which runs the clock until something happens, an hour at most; time, travel, pause, I am stuck and the curtain sit under More)
- 0.2.3 (13 November 2026: the panel is 340 px wide, since the title was still cut at 290 beside the window's header buttons)
- 0.2.2 (13 November 2026, from the testbed UI check: the panel is wider, so its title shows whole, and Go on carries a play icon while the game is paused)
- 0.2.1 (13 November 2026, from the first testbed gate: a player with no assigned character no longer acts through the first character they happen to own. The panel now takes the one assigned to them, then one of theirs on the scene they view, owned by them alone before a shared one. The gate assigns its own character to Player Test for the run and puts the old one back)
- 0.2.0 (13 November 2026, step 2 of The Lone Chronicle, B2640 to B2644: the player's panel, a window of their own with Let time pass (ten minutes, an hour, until dusk, until dawn, sleep the day; the clock moves ten minutes at a time and stops when a fight starts, the game is paused, word comes or someone new arrives), Travel, Pause or go on, I am stuck (bring me a lead, free my token, end a hung fight or window) and Lift the curtain (tonight's log from the seat, after asking; the night is marked as seen). Every ask is done by the seat. The switch also sets fights to end by themselves. A moon button in the Token controls opens the panel, and it opens by itself when the player arrives)
- 0.1.3 (the seat's reading of cards now lives in WoDX Chronicle, shared with the Waiting tray)
- 0.1.2 (12 November 2026, from the second testbed gate: a world opens paused and NPCs never act while it is paused, so the seat starts the game when the mode begins, when the seat sits down and when the player arrives. A pause made later, such as the X-card's, is left alone)
- 0.1.1 (12 November 2026, from the first testbed gate: the seat now follows the player. Foundry passes on which scene a user views as passing activity, with no hook, so the seat looks every two seconds)
- 0.1.0 (12 November 2026: the first step of The Lone Chronicle, for the testbed only. One world setting, The Lone Chronicle, sets every automation to act by itself: NPCs in combat, routines and factions to Auto, every turn ends itself, fights start by themselves, New Night runs at dusk, sunlight is rolled and applied, the night is drawn at dusk, and each kind of entry in the approval queue but a player's request gets a standing approval. Turning it off puts back every value it changed and leaves alone any changed by hand since. The Storyteller's seat watches for cards whispered to the Storyteller, windows that open there and entries left in the approval queue; after a short wait it takes the choice that goes ahead, never an undo, a refusal or a deletion, and writes what it did in a log. A card it cannot judge is logged and left. The seat views whichever scene the player views. A player who logs in while the seat is empty is told so. It does nothing in the Live world, whatever the setting says)
