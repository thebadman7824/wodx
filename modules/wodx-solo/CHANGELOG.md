# wodx-solo changelog

Newest first.

- 0.11.2 (Arrival fixes from the first walk in the solo world (B5146-B5148): an actor every player owns by default (an old test actor) is no longer taken for the player's character, so the arrival window shows; Foundry's welcome scene gives way to the landing; the player is taken to the scene with Foundry 14's pullUsers)
- 0.11.1 (Shared terms (B6180-B6199): time units, dice counts, health levels, distance, eras, lines and player characters come from WoDX Chronicle's shared terms instead of a copy of this module's own)
- 0.11.0 (The night's work in the Lone Chronicle (B6225-B6226): every button or window choice the seat makes for the absent Storyteller is written down as a piece of the Hand's work, and the Storyteller tools have a new tool, The night's work, which shows the list from the seat and takes back what the player ticks)
- 0.10.0 (Step 4 of The Lone Chronicle (B6045 to B6049). The director (world setting "The director brings the night's trouble", on) looks once a night at dusk: a rest after a hard night or three hard of four, pressure after two quiet ones, steady between; one thing, two under pressure, one fewer on a Quiet dial and one more on Often. I am stuck's lead now comes from the director. Experience (world setting "Experience at the end of each night", on): before noon after a night, ticked from the log (took part, real danger, learned something, a goal reached) on the line's own list, through WoDX Creation's award. The recap: on login, once a night. Gate gatesolodirector)
- 0.9.0 (Step 3 of The Lone Chronicle, its last part (B6040 to B6044). Places with no map: the panel button lists the towns of WoDX Storyteller's country map that stand in the character's own year and have no scene of their own (a scene set at the town itself on the country map), with a search box. The seat makes a card scene for the picked town the first time (a dark square of ground with a grid, flag wodx-solo.card, set at the town on the country map so the trip there is a country-map trip), then sends the character by the Travel window's own trip. The card is a locked drawing in the middle, written again on each arrival and each look. Look around: half an hour on the character's clock, then the town's people may come as a night encounter does (town encounters, a few squares off, in plain sight). Gate gatesolocard)
- 0.8.0 (Step 3 of The Lone Chronicle (B5980 to B5985). Ask the oracle: the player writes a yes-or-no question and picks its odds (Almost certain, Likely, Even odds, Unlikely, Hardly); the seat rolls one d10 that both see and answers "Yes, and", "Yes", "Yes, but", "No, but", "No" or "No, and" on a card whispered to the player, and an "and" sends a foreshadowing through the approval queue. Leads: a window of what the character could follow up, from the signs and dreams sent to them, their open oaths, debts, boons and objectives in the Ledger (a consequence stays the Storyteller's) and the oracle's last answers, each with Let it go. Night encounters: as the clock moves at night, each part of the night rolls once (one in three) and a hit puts the people of one of the night's encounters (town or wilds, by the scene) on the character's scene, four to eight squares off and in plain sight; world setting "Night encounters find the lone player" (on), scene flag wodx-solo.encounters (town, wilds or none). Gate gatesoloworld)
- 0.7.0 (Arrival for the lone player's first login (B5140-B5145): a landing scene (flagged, chosen, a Rothenstein map with the market first, the active one, or a plain night scene when the world has no map), a character made by the seat for a player who has none (a name and one of seven lines), its linked token at the landing's way in, and the player taken there with the wizard open. Setting "Bring the player in" (on); The switch gives every standing approval in one write instead of one write a kind. With some 80 kinds the switch took longer than ten seconds to finish, and the game stayed paused until it did)
- 0.6.0 (Companions carry the fallen (B5079, Ben's Carry Bodies Desk c10). World setting "Companions carry the fallen" (on by default). Needs WoDX NPC and WoDX Interactables)
- 0.5.0 (Add experience for the lone player (B4806). The Storyteller tools on the solo panel gain an Add experience button: the player names the points (a whole number from 1 to 100) and a reason, and the Storyteller's seat gives the award through WoDX Creation, so it lands on the sheet's experience list and log like any Storyteller award and can be spent at once. It has the same limits as the other tools: solo mode on, outside the Live world, nobody else playing)
- 0.4.0 (Storyteller tools for the lone player (B4800 to B4805). A switch under More on the solo panel shows Storyteller options, so the one person playing can check that everything works as meant and give their character items to test with. The player stays a player in Foundry: each tool is an ask the hidden Storyteller's seat answers. The Storyteller's cards shows every card, roll and whisper meant for the Storyteller alone as it arrives, with its buttons pressed by the seat when picked; Give my character an item searches the world's items and every Item compendium and makes the pick on the character's sheet; Check the systems lists the WoDX parts running and their versions, any setting the switch made that has changed since, the approval queue, cards left waiting and errors the seat has seen; What is hidden here lists the hidden tokens on the player's scene. The tools go on only while The Lone Chronicle is on, outside the Live world, for a player, with nobody else logged in, and go off by themselves when another player logs in or the mode is turned off. A Storyteller can take the switch off the panel with the new setting Offer Storyteller tools to the lone player)
- 0.3.0 (13 November 2026: the panel has one main button, What happens next?, which runs the clock until something happens, an hour at most; time, travel, pause, I am stuck and the curtain sit under More)
- 0.2.3 (13 November 2026: the panel is 340 px wide, since the title was still cut at 290 beside the window's header buttons)
- 0.2.2 (13 November 2026, from the testbed UI check: the panel is wider, so its title shows whole, and Go on carries a play icon while the game is paused)

- 0.2.0 (13 November 2026, step 2 of The Lone Chronicle, B2640 to B2644: the player's panel, a window of their own with Let time pass (ten minutes, an hour, until dusk, until dawn, sleep the day; the clock moves ten minutes at a time and stops when a fight starts, the game is paused, word comes or someone new arrives), Travel, Pause or go on, I am stuck (bring me a lead, free my token, end a hung fight or window) and Lift the curtain (tonight's log from the seat, after asking; the night is marked as seen). - 0.1.0 (12 November 2026: the first step of The Lone Chronicle, for the testbed only. One world setting, The Lone Chronicle, sets every automation to act by itself: NPCs in combat, routines and factions to Auto, every turn ends itself, fights start by themselves, New Night runs at dusk, sunlight is rolled and applied, the night is drawn at dusk, and each kind of entry in the approval queue but a player's request gets a standing approval.
