# WoDX Sheets

Character sheets that follow the era and each line's book. Private use only.

- **Changeling** (0.1.0): in a Dark Ages chronicle, or on a sheet set to Dark Ages: Fae, the layout of the Dark Ages: Fae character sheet in the book's order (Court, Origin, Agenda; Attributes; the Dark Ages: Fae Abilities; Dominions, Backgrounds, Cantrips; Willpower and Other Traits; Mists and Weaving; Health and Experience; on the second page Oaths, Echoes and Features). Otherwise it is the system's Changeling 20th sheet. It extends the system's own sheet, so rolls, dots, health, combat and gear keep working. The system's sheet stays selectable in the sheet configuration.
