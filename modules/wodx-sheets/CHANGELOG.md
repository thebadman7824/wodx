# wodx-sheets changelog

Newest first. Moved from the module table in docs/MODULES.md on 12 November 2026; add each new release at the top.

- 0.20.1 (In Beginner view the Show all button under the Abilities has the same frame and colours as the other WoDX buttons, so it reads on every line's sheet, light or dark (it was too faint on most dark sheets))
- 0.20.0 (The Relationships tab shows Family news to the owner (a pregnancy's due season, rest after a birth, a child's age and caps, a proposal on its way), a lover, spouse or troth tie has a button to start a pregnancy (the Storyteller) or ask for one, and a lover or friendly tie has a button to propose marriage (B3440, B3229))
- 0.19.0 (in a player's Beginner view: a Quick rolls row at the top of Core with four common checks and the line's powers, a click rolls at once and Shift+click opens the window, the player can add or take away checks; Abilities at zero fold behind Show all; Settings, Effects, Body and the Wiki link sit behind a More tab button and the power tabs follow Core; hover help in plain words on Attributes, Abilities, Virtues and pools, and the first sentence of a power's own description. Full view draws the sheet as before. 0.18.0 and 0.18.1 are taken by the relationships work. B2822-B2825; the Possessions tab says what the character carries in one line, in the coin of the place and year, with every coin folded under it in Beginner view)
- 0.18.1 (the Relationships tab shows how far a character trusts each person, the warmth of a romance, and names the source of a hidden loyalty or a past life (WoDX NPC 0.57.0))
- 0.18.0 (the Relationships tab says under each bond what it does in play, and where it came from: the pack name, a Fetter, the Vinculum or the family line; B2840; not deployed)
- 0.17.0 (Bring forward on the anachronism card: one button for every thing on the maps and one on each row swap it for its equal in the card's year, in any era, with WoDX Interactables 0.37.0; the goods in containers become the everyday goods of that year where the scene stands, keeping their count; a result card says what changed, names what has nothing like it, and has Undo. B2720, B2721)
- 0.16.0 (the damage doll at the top of the Body tab: a small pixel figure drawn from the body plan that changes with the shape, added and lost parts, and false limbs in wood, steel or chrome by era; each part takes the colour of the wounds on it, purple for bashing, red for lethal, charred black for aggravated; point at a part to name it, and the Storyteller clicks a part to place or clear a wound)
- 0.15.2 (Coin of which land has a new choice, The chronicle's money: the Possessions tab's coins of the time and Add coins then take their names from WoDX Storyteller's money, for every world region and year, and on a scene abroad from the money of that land. The default stays the German lands. B2653)
- 0.15.1 (the Relationships and Body tab buttons have the same tile and glyph colours as the line's own tab buttons)
- 0.15.0 (the layout pass: the title bar always shows the name, with its buttons as icons that say what they are on hover; the Possessions, Relationships, Body and Storyteller's notes sections sit in the same panels as the rest of the sheet; WoDX fields and buttons have one shade and can be read in light and dark mode; item sheets use the width of the window and their text editor follows dark mode; list headings and long section titles no longer run into each other)
- 0.14.0 (the anachronism check reads the maps: with WoDX Interactables on, vehicles, telephones, radios, sprinklers, electric lights, live wires, keypads and the goods inside containers on every scene are named on the card by scene, each with Keep it and Show me; "Out of their time" in the Storyteller's hand lists everything out of its time now)
- 0.13.0 (under the in-play strip: "Changed since last session", each line with its cause (a card, the Storyteller, a player by hand, the time skip, Undo), cleared with Mark as read and started again at each session's end; a Features card listing every line feature in force, when it fires and when it last fired, with a switch for the Storyteller to set one aside for a story)
- 0.12.0 (a Followers button on the Relationships tab opens WoDX NPC's Followers window)
- 0.11.0 (the Ties tab becomes Relationships: every tie WoDX keeps, grouped, with the Storyteller's hidden ties and secrets kept back from players; ghoul and domitor ties show again; Family and lineage and Secrets buttons; how an NPC regards the characters, for the Storyteller)
- 0.10.0 (the Body tab: shape, parts, what the body can do, where blows land, lasting changes)
- 0.8.2 (item descriptions in a fold under each possession, closed by default)
- 0.8.1 (a cast button on each rote in the Rotes list)
- 0.8.0 (the anachronism check: a card for the Storyteller when the clock jumps, naming possessions out of their time, with Keep)
- Phase 6 sheets: the Dark Ages sheets (Fae, Mage, Inquisitor, Werewolf, V20 Dark Ages Kindred and ghoul) and the later eras' (V20 and ghoul, W20, M20, C20, Wraith 20, Hunter: The Reckoning, Demon, Mummy, Kindred of the East, Orpheus)
- following the era. 0.5.0: a look of its own for each line (title face, paper and section rules, light and dark)
- behind the world setting "Sheet look". 0.6.0: every sheet has its second page (Combat, Possessions, History and Description, and the line's own back-page boxes)
- 0.6.1 (the domitor fields work with WoDX Creation off)
- 0.6.2 (the system no longer draws a Core tab that the book's sheet replaces)
- 0.7.0 (on every WoDX sheet: an in-play strip, Possessions with containers, stacks, concealment sizes, out-of-era flags and money by era, a Ties tab, the Storyteller's notes, list search, a History window with Undo, Print)
