# wodx-storyteller changelog

Newest first. Moved from the module table in docs/MODULES.md on 12 November 2026; add each new release at the top.

- 0.55.0 (the world map: "The world map" in the Storyteller's hand shows the whole Earth by night on a coarse grid, one square for each degree, with its ground, great rivers and 44 world regions; a journey between any two spots in the chronicle's year or a typed one, over land and sea and by air from 1935, with the days each leg takes, the Suez Canal open to ships only from 1869 (shut 1967 to 1974) and the Panama Canal only from 1914; the known world: the map and its journeys keep to what the chronicle's homeland knows in the year, so that Europe has no way to the Americas before 1492 and Mexico none to Europe, with Discoveries to mark the lands a chronicle finds for itself; a closer look at any one square, made from the grid the same way every time; with WoDX Otherworlds on, the Penumbra, the Shadowlands and the Near Dreaming as the same map in another dress, and the chart of the worlds. The grid is made from Natural Earth, which is in the public domain. B2540 to B2544)
- 0.54.0 (money of the whole world: 24 money regions, each from its first coin (or none) to the eurobuck, with France given its own; North America has no coin before 1607; Start a chronicle asks for the homeland)
- 0.53.0 (a primer for each of the seven prologue eras, written for the whole world; prologue years read BCE and CE)
- 0.52.2 (Let time pass asks only the party on the asker's scene: the players whose characters are there agree, and only their time passes. Characters elsewhere, and a fight they are in, no longer hold it back. B2460)
- 0.52.1 (Let time pass and the small rule for time count only a fight on the scene where the waiting characters are, so a fight left running on another scene no longer stops time everywhere. B2459)
- 0.52.0 (Let time pass: a player asks to wait, rest or sleep the day, and the clock moves when everyone here agrees and nobody is fighting; small rules for markets, time for a long action and downtime; the wrap-up names what is still waiting)
- 0.51.0 (ships at sea: a new window from the Storyteller's hand for two ships of the year, from the longship and dromon of Spoils of War p. 40 to steam ironclads, dreadnoughts, missile frigates and the Future Darkness drone corvette as house rules; the chase every 4 hours at 500 yards a point, the turn for position for the initiative, navigation, shooting with the siege engines of p. 33 or with gun crews by range and torpedoes after Wraith: The Great War, ramming and breaking away, each health level lost costing Speed and Maneuverability, flooding every quarter hour below 0 Speed, repair as an extended roll, boarding until the defenders yield; each step has its cards and an Undo; also fixes the siege report line that read past the walls' health. api.naval)
- 0.50.0 (the campaign around a siege, from Spoils of War pp. 40-42 and the siege tables of pp. 33-34: foraging twice a day for man-days of food, supply lines by the year, the camp's own stores and hunger; keeping order each week by pay and supply, each success short +1 to every army roll the next week; mines planned, dug in yards by the ground and fired under the walls for 3 health levels a success, with powder and high explosive in later ages; the garrison listening each day and countermining, with or without shoring; siege engines gathered and put together, then shooting once a day against the walls' soak, and the guns of later ages; walls and forts with their soak and health levels filling the breach clock; a ditch filled before rams can reach. All of it rides on the week of siege and its Undo. api.siegeWorks)
- 0.49.1 (Bring it in works on a caravan that set out this very moment, as straight after a market turn; before, it stayed on the road until the clock moved; testbed gate step 3)
- 0.49.0 (the rest of the living economy. Factions act on markets: a tax by the faction that holds the place, a monopoly on one kind of goods, smuggling past a rival's tax, and raids that take a caravan or strip the stalls and close a road, each in the words of the year and each through the approval queue where a player character stands. Traders can go broke: a week's takings follow supply and taxes, four weeks the till cannot cover shuts the shop, and it opens again after eight weeks once trade is good. Caravans: goods traded between linked markets leave as a pack train, wagons, a goods train, lorries or an automated convoy, arrive after the days the road takes, can be lost, and are noted when a character travels the same road. A Caravans window, and the Markets window shows and sets faction moves, closed roads and shut shops)
- 0.48.2 (a book army loses at most a third of the units it began with in one turn, so a small army is no longer gone in the first hour; faction wars now run three or more phases)
- 0.48.1 (approving a market change from the queue applies it: the Markets approval handler now registers when WoDX Chronicle is ready, as the other Storyteller handlers do; testbed gate step 5)
- 0.48.0 (the weekly market turn: every market makes, uses and trades goods once a week of game time; supply comes back toward normal, workshops hold it up, linked markets trade and meet on price, prices follow supply and the season by era; big swings where a player character is wait in the approval queue, the rest apply with one Undo card; long skips play twelve weeks and settle the rest; talk of dear and cheap goods starts in the rumour network; markets list their trade links and can be cut off; Run a week now in the Markets window)
- 0.47.1 (those the sun burns stop for the day at a village anywhere along a road, so a short summer night no longer leaves a road trip with no shelter; only a stretch off the roads or a paid ride must fit one night. Travel places: the Near column is typed and searched from one list of towns)
