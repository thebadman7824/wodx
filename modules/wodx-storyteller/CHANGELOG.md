- 0.78.0 (Living caerns: the Garou kind of place of power. A caern on the Places of power register has a level, a kind from the W20 Caerns chart, a totem, its sept with Garou, tribes and offices (the editor suggests the highest-ranking Theurge as Master of the Rite), and the Gnosis of its last moot. Hold the Moot Rite (each Garou gives Gnosis in turn, the rest of the sept can add more), open the caern for its power, meet a raid, fight a caern war, raise it after a year of full moots, or take it back once lost. On the clock a caern without its monthly moot lapses, sinks a level every three months and dies at 0; raids by the Wyrm and the Weaver come by era and settle themselves after a week. A lapse or a lost raid steps the scene's Gauntlet drift (WoDX Otherworlds). API: api.upkeep.power.caern)
- 0.77.0 (Patronage of the arts (B3374-B3378): the Storyteller keeps a list of artists (named, or taken from the towns' NPCs with Expression or Crafts of 2 or more) in a new Artists and patrons window, which also shows what is in fashion. A player commissions a work as a downtime project of the new kind Commission a work of art, naming the artist, the form (from a portrait or an illuminated book to an opera from 1600, a novel from 1740, a film from 1900, a record from 1925, a video game from 1975) and the money spent. Finished, the work is made as an item on the patron; its quality comes from the artist's skill, a dot for every two of money, and one when the form is in the fashion of its year and way of life (thirty movements, from Romanesque, Gothic and the troubadours, icon painting, Persian miniatures, ink landscapes, Noh and the floating world, Mughal painting and the griots, through the Renaissance and Baroque, Romanticism and Modernism, to jazz, punk, hip hop and the internet). A fine work adds a Status die at gatherings, two for a masterpiece in fashion, is told to the Storyteller for the Toreador's eye, and a Toreador patron is asked whether it becomes a Muse. A fae patron makes the artist a Dreamer, or Ravages them by harsh handling. Artists without a patron for six months starve and may die or find one. The Storyteller can hold a salon (by era: a court of song, a salon, a gallery opening, a launch party) for a host: a kind tale starts and the host's artists may rise. api.patronage)
- 0.76.0 (Haven works (B3359-B3363): havens can be improved from an upgrade list dated by year and built by the way of life of the place (an iron-bound door and a stout bar until 1850, shutters, a wall and gate, a courtyard house in the Islamic, Indian, Chinese and African worlds, bars from 1650, better locks from 1784, a strongroom from 1835, a bell alarm from 1858, steel shutters from 1950, cameras and a watched alarm from 1975, networked locks from 2005, a ward for mages and a spirit watcher for the Garou, and a room furnished for show in each era). An upgrade is a downtime project of the new kind Improve a haven; finished, it asks the Storyteller to put it in, raising security up to the Domain's cap. The Storyteller can also name staff by post (porter, servant, guard, groom, driver, a security firm's guard). Works leave traces (builders talk, deliveries seen, an installer's records) that fade by half each half year, and standing ones while they last (servants can be bought, a ward glows, the feed can be reached). Each month an NPC with a grudge against an owner may probe: scout, bribe a servant, read the ward, reach the feed, ask the spirits, or set a fire when they know the way already; each asks the Storyteller and fires wodxHavenProbe. A Works button on each haven in the Havens window. api.havenWorks; Dreams and omens of what is coming (B3369-B3373): once a day the Storyteller's client reads what the world is about to do (the chronicle's dated events within 90 days, NPC grudges near the mark against a character, hunters investigating or worse in a region, a plague of history on its way to a region where a character is). A player character with a gift (Auspex, Oneiromancy or a dream power, the Oracular Ability Merit, a Malkavian, a Theurge, the fae, a mummy, a mage's Dream, a Visionary hunter from 1999) may dream of it in symbols from a table by kind and way of life; the nearer the signal to the dreamer, the likelier. One dream a week at most; 1 in 10 lies, borrowing another kind's symbols. Each dream asks the Storyteller first and then joins the Dreams list for the next sleep, where its words can still be changed. Its card has Try to read it: Perception or Intelligence + Enigmas or Occult at 7; one success tells the kind of danger, two more, three plainly (who or where), and four or more unmask a false dream. A great sign may also show in the world (crows on the roofs, a red glow to the east), a line the Storyteller approves for everyone. New setting "Dreams and omens of what is coming". api.omens)
