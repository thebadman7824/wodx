# wodx-creation changelog

Newest first. Moved from the module table in docs/MODULES.md on 12 November 2026; add each new release at the top.

- 0.21.0 (lifepath drill-down: dated local content for the other 43 world regions, 210 origins open only in a span of birth years and 684 things that marked a life stage while they happened there, from the Khan's armies and the gold pilgrimage to the tests on the atolls, each marked good, hard or neither; the first screen says the latest year a character can be born in)
- 0.20.0 (lifepath drill-down: for those who lived on after the change, one optional pick of five for each age they lived at least twenty years of, with Surprise me, Five others and Leave untold; the Storyteller can limit a chronicle's lifepaths to some regions and leave out kinds of origin, from a fold-out on the lifepath's first screen, with the chronicle's own land always open)
- 0.19.0 (lifepath drill-down: dated local content, origins open only in a span of birth years and things that marked a life stage while they happened in that land; Moravia and Bohemia get 17 dated origins and 37 dated marks sorted from the older lifepath scenes, from the crusades and the Hussite wars to the White Mountain, 1848, the occupation, 1968, 1989 and the floods; one of each tone comes first among the five, and a dated mark is not offered twice in one life; the older scenes stay for older records)
- 0.18.0 (lifepath drill-down: every one of the 44 world regions now has its own ages, origins, a teenage path and a calling for each age from the Middle Ages to the future, 218 ages in all with about 590 local origins; Antarctica from the first sealers on)
- 0.17.1 (lifepath drill-down: the first pick of each life stage, who raised you, what you were set to, how you lived, also shows five options, two good, two hard and one neither, with five others on request; the land's own path is among the first five; more beginnings for those born as wolves and for orphans)
- 0.17.0 (lifepaths that drill down: continent, region, your people where more than one lives there, origin, then childhood, the teenage years and a chapter of adult life about every twelve years, as far as the character's age reaches; coming of age moves with the era and the region; each stage asks how those years were spent and what marked them, with five things on offer, two good, two hard and one neither, and five others on request; any stage after childhood can happen in another region, and a life lived elsewhere ends with what brought the character here; Surprise me and Something else on every step, a player's own answers and characters younger than 12 pass the Storyteller first; at the end the ticked Abilities, Backgrounds, Merits and Flaws go on the sheet at the book's cost and within its caps; births of a line's own kind where the line fits; local origins, paths and callings for six sample regions so far; people from a life abroad are named as that land names its people; the prelude runner plays one scene for the birth and one for each life stage)
- 0.16.0 (people of the whole world: 39 peoples with their own given and family names, older and newer where names changed, family name first where a people says it so, and "son of" and "daughter of" names where a people uses them; who lives in each of the world's regions in each age; the industrial trades serve every region)
- 0.15.1 (the People generator takes an origin to ask for (api.people.generate({origin})), kept when the era's tables name it, so WoDX NPC can draw a household's kin of its own faith; with no origin asked every person is drawn as before)
- 0.15.0 (an XP spend by the book (an Attribute, an Ability, Willpower or an own-clan Discipline at the book's cost, with the points there) can pass the approval queue on its own under a small rule; new Disciplines, Virtues and the Road wait)
- 0.14.0 (lifepath threads: the open threads of a kept lifepath, a rival, a debt, someone lost, an enemy, for WoDX Chronicle to deal out as story chains; a guest in another character's prelude scene, where their years allow, with the beat written to troupe ties)
- 0.13.1 (a Family tree button in the People window, beside the Lineage journal)
- 0.13.0 (Before the First Night: the guided builder with a live count for every line's creation totals, the prelude runner with rolls with stakes and up to 3 freebie points of gains, each with Undo, the written prelude, troupe ties in a ring onto the Group actor, How they met, and the short prelude for a late joiner)
- 0.12.0 (Before the First Night: a hub of steps for each player character with Storyteller approval and sign-off, the trouble forecast from flaws, blood, people, factions, rivals, coming events, language and gear, the trouble dial, the story hook, and seed cards with Undo at sign-off)
- 0.11.0 (lifepaths: a step-by-step chain of scenes for every line and splat, 1197 to the World of Future Darkness; the lifepath goes along through a change of state)
