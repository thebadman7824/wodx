# wodx-otherworlds changelog

Newest first. Moved from the module table in docs/MODULES.md on 12 November 2026; add each new release at the top.

- 0.20.0 (the chart of the worlds: every realm of the realm maps catalogue has a spot in rings round the living world, on four sides (the spirit wilds, the high and far reaches, the Dreaming, the lands of the dead), with the barrier each side lies behind; the Penumbra, the Shadowlands and the Near Dreaming dress WoDX Storyteller's world map as the Earth seen from the other side. B2535 to B2537)
- 0.19.0 (a small rule for Otherworld journeys: no storm, not a great journey, no hazard or Maelstrom on the way)
- 0.18.0 (the Shroud drifts on a track of its own: mass death thins it a point, it heals back; a place with a Shroud rating reads it with the drift; WoDX Storyteller's battle aftermath sends it)
- 0.17.0 (wild land with no edge: the open Umbra and the Tempest's storm sea are drawn a piece at a time from a seed and a place, pieces side by side meet square for square, a moon path or a Byway winds across the whole wild, and Walk on in Realm maps draws the next piece and sets the travellers down on it; Reflect reads a map from under the ground: a warren piece by piece and sewers run by run, with webbing on built works, sour ground along filth and in lairs, buried water running wild, and falls and closed ways standing for the dead; a reach of the Labyrinth and a Black Spiral cavern are read their own way)
- 0.16.0 (standing with the spirit courts: Bird, Beast, Elemental, Wyld, Weaver, Wyrm and the totem, -5 to +5, moved by chiminage, Banes and Bans, changing the difficulty with that court's spirits)
- 0.15.1 (after the book check: the Labyrinth is the Storyteller's call (WR20 pdf 307); the Far Dreaming, Arcadia and the Deep Umbra cards give their pages; the Shadowlands and the Tempest keep the world's time)
- 0.15.0 (Game time: the journey card says how much time passes outside (a Moon Bridge's thousandth, a trod's slip) and has a button to let it pass; realm time table)
- 0.13.1 (the aura overlay names its colour)
- 0.13.0 (sendAcross: a power that has worked takes a token across the Gauntlet or the Shroud with no second roll)
- 0.12.0 (a layout of its own for every realm, drawn from what the realm is: no two realms share one, every place reachable)
- 0.11.0 (map keywords: 22 kinds of feature, each with its own ground and marks; realms that share them look alike, the seed keeps them apart; some set the islands' form, caverns or a square city; the Storyteller can add more)
- 0.10.0 (each realm map checked against its description and redrawn where it did not fit: walled island cities in the Sea of Shadows, storm seas, the Signpost and battle works by year, rivers, the black train, Enoch's spires, Ti Yu, Phoebe; Shadowlands relics show only the newest house on each spot)
- 0.9.1 (module.json says WoDX Chronicle must be installed even when off)
- 2, 5, 8 (Otherworld journeys, the Shadowlands' decay)
- 0.4.0 (the Near Dreaming and trods, Moon Paths and Bridges, the Avatar Storm and Great Maelstroms by date, Disembodiment, the Net, layer sound)
- 0.4.1 (a refused gateway or Net returns nothing)
- 0.5.0 (the looks of the other worlds: the Dreaming's shimmer, the Net's lines)
- 0.6.0 (ways and realms: realm scenes; trods, Moon Paths, airts, Anchorheads and Byways as regions)
- 0.7.0 (a Gauntlet that moves; Banality of places drifts)
- 0.7.1 (journeys work with WoDX Chronicle off)
- 0.7.2 (dead code removed)
- 0.8.0 (realm maps: 34 realms past the near worlds drawn from a seed and the year, in seven layouts, with walls and regions; send tokens there and bring them back)
- 0.9.0 (generated town maps reflected in the Penumbra and Shadowlands: looming, webbing, souring, Wyld growth, relics of what later ages pulled down)
